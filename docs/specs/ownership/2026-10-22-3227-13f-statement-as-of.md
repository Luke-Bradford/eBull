## Problem

Every tier picks M's statement C as the latest filed before P + 1 day (UTC), P being the 13F's period. The 13D arm's
spec chose that because `blockholder_filings.date_of_event` was NULL on all 122,134 rows (its § Dates). #3948
(`43d2454f`) has since parsed it. A 13G that reports ownership at a quarter end is filed after it by rule (Source rule),
so the filing-date bound excludes exactly the statements made for P.

Example: TDAY. Apollo Management Holdings, L.P. (CIK 1449434) files a 13F of 12,128,756 for 2026-06-30.
- Schedule 13G/A `0001104659-26-093619` was filed 2026-08-11 with date of event 2026-06-30. Its filer of record is
  Apollo Management Holdings GP, LLC (CIK 1449433). Of its 16 blocks, the GP's, M's and two others report 12,128,756,
  and none reports more.
- Its observation is the only kept row of the GP's `blockholders` node.
- Filing-date selection picks the 2026-05-14 13G/A (date of event 2026-03-31) instead. Its blocks are the same, but the
  GP's kept row is not its observation, so (O) refuses (condition 2).

Example: CALM. American Century Companies, Inc. (CIK 748054) files a 13F of 2,520,562 for 2026-06-30. Schedule 13G
`0000052388-26-000256`, filed 2026-08-14 with date of event 2026-06-30, is filed by American Century Investment
Management, Inc. (CIK 52388) and names M with 2,520,562. M has no statement filed before 2026-07-01, so no tier looks.

## Source rule

**Where the rules fix a reporting date** (paragraph (b) filers):
- Rule 13d-1(b)(2): a Schedule 13G is filed *"within 45 days after the end of the calendar quarter in which the person
  became obligated"*, *"to report the person's beneficial ownership as of the last day of the calendar quarter"*.
  Above 10% before the quarter end, *"the initial Schedule 13G shall be filed within five business days after the end
  of the first month"*, with ownership computed at that month's end.
- Rule 13d-2(b): a Schedule 13G is amended *"within 45 days after the end of each calendar quarter if, as of the end of
  the calendar quarter, there are any material changes in the information reported"*.
- Rule 13d-2(c): an accelerated amendment is due *"within five business days after the end of the first month"* in
  which ownership, measured at that month's end, passes 10%, and afterwards when it *"increases or decreases by more
  than five percent"*.

**Where they fix none, or this spec quotes none:**
- Rule 13d-1(c): a passive investor's 13G is due *"within five business days after the date of an acquisition"*.
- Rule 13d-2(d): a paragraph (c) filer amends *"within two business days after acquiring"* more than 10%, and afterwards
  on *"increasing or decreasing its beneficial ownership by more than five percent"*.
- Rule 13d-1(d): due *"within 45 days after the end of the calendar quarter"*; this spec cites no as-of wording for it.
- Rule 13d-2(a): a Schedule 13D amendment is due *"within two business days after the date of such change"*, with no
  as-of date for the cover's figures.

On those routes the date of event is the date of the event that triggered the filing, and the cover's figures may be
as of a later day.

**The field.** The cover page carries the *"Date of Event Which Requires Filing of this Statement"*. The parser reads it
from `eventDateRequiresFilingThisStatement` (13G) and `dateOfEvent` (13D) (`app/providers/implementations/sec_13dg.py`,
`_extract_13g` and `_extract_13d`) into `blockholder_filings.date_of_event`. We store no field naming the filing route.

**So the rule below is a policy.** It dates every statement by its date of event.
- **What the rules fix.** On the paragraph (b) routes the reporting date is a quarter end or, on the accelerated routes,
  a month end.
- **What the policy assumes.** That on such a filing the cover's date of event is that date. The rules quoted name the
  event and the reporting date separately and do not state that they are equal.
- **On the other routes** the date of event is a stand-in, and nothing quoted orders it against the figures' date.

All three are declared (risk 11). The figure check against T applies on every route, as built. The case review reads
each new duplicate's filing route and reporting date from its cover (§ Change 3). The measured lags are under
§ Full-population verification.

## Rule (frozen)

**Statement date.** A cover row's **statement date** is its `date_of_event` when that is non-NULL and not after the
filing date (`filed_at` as a UTC date). Otherwise it is the filing date.
- The fallback is not a repair. It keeps such a row's filing-date eligibility. Its rank against other statements can
  still change when they have usable dates: with P = 07-31, A filed 07-01 with no usable date and B filed 07-02 with
  date of event 06-30, the built rule selects B and this rule selects A. Selection is unchanged only when every
  competing statement takes the fallback.
- A date of event after the filing date is impossible by rule, so such a row has no usable date. Measured in §
  Full-population verification.

**Selection.** Every place a built tier reads *"filed before P + 1 day (UTC)"* now reads **statement date ≤ P**, and
every *"latest by `(filed_at, accession_number)`"* now reads **latest by `(statement date, filed_at,
accession_number)`**. This covers:
- the 13D arm's cover for M;
- M's statements in the 13G extension, by CIK and by name, and the 13G accessions they range over;
- hence C for (U) and (O).

Nothing else in any tier changes: matching, uniqueness, reservation and the folds are as built. With no usable date of
event on every competing statement the rule is today's, since `filed_at < P + 1 day` is `filed_at::date ≤ P`.

**Order.** A later filing for the same statement date (an amendment restating it) wins. A statement for a later date
wins over one filed later for an earlier date.

**Wording.** Each built template's cover parenthetical `(filed {filed}, …)` gains `date of event {date}` after the
filing date, showing the stored `date_of_event` as it is, and only when it is non-NULL. No other built wording changes.
The text does not call the statement "as of" any date.

## Accepted identification risk (declared, not measured)

The built specs' risks 1–10 apply. Added:

11. **The date of event as the statement's date.** On a paragraph (b) route the policy assumes the date of event is
    the rule's quarter-end or month-end reporting date. On any other route the cover's figures may be as of another
    day, possibly after P. A position that changed between them and still equals T is folded on the cover's figures.
    The census counts the new duplicates whose date of event is not a quarter end.
12. **A wrong date of event.** A mis-keyed date that is not after the filing date is not detected.
13. **Selection is not monotone.** Re-dating can change any candidate's C, and through the matched sets, uniqueness and
    reservation of other candidates. No general guarantee is claimed that a built fold survives; the census measures it
    on the current corpus, and the A/B on the build.

## Change

### 1. `ownership_rollup.py`

- `_CoverRow` gains `date_of_event: date | None` and a `statement_date` property implementing § Rule.
- `_COVERS_13F_SQL` selects `date_of_event` and bounds on the statement date instead of `filed_at < bound`:
  `instrument_id = %(iid)s AND LEAST(COALESCE(date_of_event, (filed_at AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')::date), (filed_at AT TIME
  ZONE 'UTC')::date) <= %(period)s`, with `period` the latest candidate's P. `LEAST` reproduces the fallback: a date
  after the filing date is replaced by the filing date, which is then the statement date. The SQL bound and the Python
  property must agree on every row; a test asserts it.
- `_read_13f_covers` passes `{"iid": instrument_id, "period": max(periods)}` and keeps its no-candidate early return.
- **Access path.** The predicate can use only the instrument prefix of `idx_blockholder_filings_instrument_filed_at`,
  not its `filed_at` range. No filing-date bound is restored, since it would exclude the late filings. The PR records
  `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` for the instruments with the most cover rows, and the A/B's latency gate decides. If it
  fails, the fix is an expression index on `(instrument_id, <statement date>)` in its own migration.
- `_read_13g_filers` is unchanged. Its `period_end` window is computed from the filed dates of the covers it is given,
  so it covers statements filed after P.
- `_fold_13f_duplicates`: both selections use `statement_date <= period` and the key `(statement_date, filed_at,
  accession_number)`. Remove `_cover_bound` if nothing else uses it.
- Every built template gains `date of event {date}`.
- Docstrings and the section comment name this spec.

### 2. The A/B

A sibling of `scripts/ab_3227_13f_13g_observation_block.py`, base the real code at `ffd8590e`:
- **Built folds.** Compare every built fold by `(tier, M, k, T, C)`. Any built fold lost, changed or moved to another
  tier or C fails the A/B (the census measured none). Correction text is compared after removing the added
  `date of event {date}`, and must otherwise be identical.
- **One snapshot.** The A/B runs the base code, the treated code and the census oracle in one `REPEATABLE READ`
  snapshot. The treated increment must equal that snapshot's oracle set by `(tier, M, k, T, C)`. Differences from the
  73 recorded here (live ingest) are reported separately, each with its cause.
- **Pie.** pie = base − Σ T(new) per instrument.
- **Latency.** p95 regression ≤ 10% over all instruments and over the affected ones.

### 3. Case review in the PR

Parse each new duplicate's EDGAR `primary_doc.xml` (issuer CIK path), comparing namespace-local tag names as
`sec_13dg.py` does:
- every tier: the reporting date and filing trigger, read from the cover's rule designation (Rule 13d-1(b), (c) or (d)
  for a 13G), its amendment status, Item 4, any explanatory text and the exhibits. The designation alone does not
  separate a quarterly amendment from an accelerated one. Each case records the evidence found and its date, or
  `undetermined`. An `undetermined` case stands on risk 11 and is counted in the PR. A case whose cover establishes a
  reporting date after P stops the build;
- every tier: the XML date of event equals the stored `date_of_event`; the statement date ≤ P; the XML block count
  equals the stored blocks and every block's figures equal the stored block's; the filer against
  `filerCredentials/cik`;
- the 13D arm: M's and k's blocks report T;
- (U): every block reports T;
- (O): the largest aggregate is T; the observation's name and every block named as M are in the XML and report T.

A mismatch stops the build.
