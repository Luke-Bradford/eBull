# #3227 — a Form 5's Table I holding lines reach the insider observation

Status: spec (2026-10-18). This is a residual of #3227, named in the Form 4 holding-row spec
(`2026-10-18-3227-form4-holding-rows.md`, "Not in this slice: Form 5 holdings"). Rung: corpus
change. This document is the judgement artefact (Codex checkpoint 1).

## Problem

#3882 taught the Form 4 parser and both Form 4 observation writers to read
`nonDerivativeHolding` rows. Form 5 uses the same ownership XML, but its holding rows still reach
no XML observation:

- `parse_form_5_xml` (`app/services/insider_transactions.py:601`) reads transactions only. A
  Form 5 with holdings and no transaction returns `None` and is tombstoned (docstring `:613-620`).
- `form4_holding_observations` returns nothing outside Form 4/4A (`:2074`). The weekly writer's
  holding units select only `document_type IN ('4','4/A')`
  (`app/services/ownership_observations_sync.py:388-391`).
- The DERA bulk path writes `NONDERIV_HOLDING` rows, but only for accessions with no
  `NONDERIV_TRANS` row. It lags by a quarter. Its `ownership_nature` holds the relationship flag,
  not D/I (`sec-edgar.md`, "ownership_nature is an OVERLOADED column").

Example: iid 1048597, filer 0001998412, Form 5 `0001998412-26-000002`, fiscal year ended
2025-12-31.
- The XML has a `D` holding of 4,000 and an `I` holding of 2,070.436.
- `_current` holds `direct` = 2,070.44, from the DERA `:NDH:` row of the same accession, which
  filed the indirect line under `direct`. It holds `indirect` = 714.89, from a February 2025
  Form 4.

## Source rule

From the SEC Form 5 form face and General Instructions (`https://www.sec.gov/files/form5.pdf`,
read 2026-10-18), verbatim:

- Item 3: *"Statement for Issuer's Fiscal Year Ended (Month/Day/Year)"*.
- Table I, column 5: *"Amount of Securities Beneficially Owned at the end of Issuer's Fiscal Year"*.
- 1(b): a reporting person no longer subject to Section 16 *"must check the exit box …
  Transactions and holdings previously reported are not required to be included on this Form."*
- 4(a)(i): *"… the following transactions, and total beneficial ownership as of the end of the
  issuer's fiscal year (or the earlier date applicable to a person ceasing to be an insider during
  the fiscal year) for any class of securities for which a transaction is reported, shall be
  reported"*, including under (C) *"any transactions or holdings that should have been reported
  during the issuer's fiscal year on a Form 3 or Form 4, but were not reported."*
- 4(b)(iii): *"Report transactions in securities beneficially owned directly on separate lines
  from those beneficially owned indirectly. Report different forms of indirect ownership on
  separate lines."*
- 4(b)(iv): an indirect amount may be the proportionate interest or, *"at the option of the
  reporting person, the entire amount of the entity's interest"*.
- 4(b)(v): joint filers *"may file Form 5 individually or jointly"*.
- Instruction 8: *"To indicate that a holding should have been reported previously on Form 3,
  place a '3' in Table I, column 3 … Indicate in the space provided for explanation of responses
  the event triggering the Form 3 filing obligation."*
- Instruction 9(a)/(b), on amendments: *"Do not repeat lines of transaction or ownership
  information that were disclosed in the original Form and are not being amended."*

**What the rule settles:**
- **The line grammar.** A Form 5 holding row is a stated balance for its class and form of
  ownership, on its own line by D/I and by form of indirect ownership. That is the grammar of
  Form 4 4(b)(iii), which #3882's verdict applies. 4(b)(iv) applies to both forms, so the
  verdict's nested-block residual carries over unchanged.
- **The date, which the form fixes, unlike Form 4's.** Column 5 is the balance at the end of the
  issuer's fiscal year. Item 3 states that date, and the XML carries it as `periodOfReport`. On
  dev, the census's Item 3 section compares every stored `form5_xml` payload (1,631) with
  `instrument_sec_profile.fiscal_year_end`:
  - 1,500 match the month and day exactly;
  - 87 are within 7 days, consistent with 52/53-week fiscal years;
  - 41 are further off;
  - 3 have no fiscal year end on file;
  - 0 have an unreadable fiscal year end.

  The rule takes the date from Item 3, so it does not depend on the profile column. The query
  left-joins the filing and profile rows, and every payload has a filing row. A malformed `MMDD`
  counts as unreadable (0 here). A match is exact only when the period is the year end of its own
  year; a `0229` year end falls on 02-28 in a non-leap year. The signature
  date plays no part.
- **An amendment is partial** (Instruction 9). A 5/A's holding lines are only the lines being
  added or amended, so a 5/A group is not a balance for its key.

**What it does not settle, so these write nothing:**
- **An exit-box filing.** The balance is as of an earlier, undated exit date (4(a)(i)), and the
  filing may omit previously reported holdings (1(b)). Such a filing sets `notSubjectToSection16`.
- **A delinquent-Form-3 line** (`transactionFormType` 3). Instruction 8 ties it to an earlier
  triggering event, while the column-5 header says fiscal year end. The form does not say which
  date its amount carries.

## Change

### 1. Parse

`parse_form_5_xml` also returns the filing's holdings, via `_extract_holdings`, as
`parse_form_4_xml` does since #3882. It returns a filing when it has transactions **or**
holdings, so a holdings-only Form 5 is no longer tombstoned. That includes the
derivative-holdings-only ones.

`ParsedHolding` gains `holding_form_type`, the holding's `transactionCoding/transactionFormType`
(`None` when absent, which is the Form 4 case). `_PARSER_VERSION_FORM5` is bumped, and so is the
`form5_xml` rewash spec in lockstep (the comment above the Form 5 `register_parser` in
`rewash_filings.py`).

### 2. Store

Holdings go to `insider_form4_holdings`, through the `upsert_filing` path that already writes it.
A migration adds the nullable `holding_form_type` column. There is no new table: the schema is the
ownership XML's, and `insider_filings.document_type` records the form.

The readers:
- **Weekly writer** (`ownership_observations_sync.py:280`, `:293`): widened, see (3). It also
  selects `holding_form_type`.
- **Drill-through count** (`ownership_drillthrough.py:373`). It counts holdings for
  `document_type = '4'`, but transactions for `('4', '5', '5/A')` (`:353`). The holdings count
  takes the transactions' set, so a holdings-only Form 5 never reads as a rewash candidate.
  - Both sets already omit `4/A`. That is a pre-existing gap and is kept, because this slice does
    not change the 4/A counts.
- **The other three readers.** The table stores every parsed line, including the ones the
  observation gates refuse, so each reader's treatment of those lines is stated here:
  - The holder-name resolver (`holder_name_resolver.py:175`, unchanged, latest by signature date)
    matches a DEF 14A name to a filer CIK. A gated line still attests
    the filer's identity. Its shares feed only the drift check, which already compares a single
    line (#3883), and partial 4/A lines already reach that check.
  - **The component scope (`ownership_rollup.py:1255`) excludes the gated Form 5 lines**: lines
    of a 5/A, of an exit-box filing, and lines with `holding_form_type = '3'`.
    - It reads a winner accession's lines as amount evidence for a control-group match.
    - A line the observation writers refuse, because its date or completeness is unknown, is
      not evidence of the winning balance, even when its accession wins another key through an
      ordinary group or a transaction.
    - Form 4 lines are unchanged; their partial 4/A lines stay an accepted residual of the
      Form 4 slice.
  - The record-holder evidence (`ownership_rollup.py:3617`, unchanged) reads each `I` line's
    nature-of-ownership text, keyed by `(accession, amount)`. It neither dates nor sums lines, so
    a gated line contributes only its own text to a holder already matched on that amount.

### 3. Observe

`form4_holding_observations` takes Form 5. Most of the rule is shared by both writers, so the
weekly re-derivation cannot revert the live one (#3874): the holding-only test, the verdict, the
multi-title whitelist, the manifest-`filed_at` requirement and the observation fields. For
`document_type = '5'`, four things differ:

- **Gates**, each emitting no holding observation:
  - a 5/A (Instruction 9);
  - an exit-box filing;
  - a group containing a line with `holding_form_type = '3'`.

  The helper gains a `not_subject_to_section_16` argument. The live caller passes
  `parsed.not_subject_to_section_16`; the weekly unit query selects
  `insider_filings.not_subject_to_section_16`, already stored (`upsert_filing`); the weekly holding
  query selects `holding_form_type`.
- **Date (`period_end`):** `period_of_report` when it is on or before the filed date and not
  before the Section 16 floor (`predates_section_16`, the transactions' own floor, #2441), else no
  observation. The filed date is the UTC date of the manifest `filed_at`. `form4_holding_as_of`'s
  signature rule does not apply.
- **Retention:** `form5_within_retention` on that UTC date, with no fallback dates. The gate sits
  in the helper, so it binds every entry point that reaches it: the manifest parser, the
  registered `form5_xml` rewash (`rewash_filings._apply_form5`, which has no retention check of
  its own), and the backfill. The manifest
  parser's own gate (`manifest_parsers/insider_345.py:623`) calls `filed_at.date()` in the session
  timezone. Dev returns `Etc/UTC`, so the two agree there, and the build pins UTC in both.
- **Weekly writer:** holding units add `document_type = '5'` with `form5_retention_cutoff`.
  `since` applies to `period_of_report` for those units, and `LIMIT` to the union, as for Form 4.

### 4. Backfill

This backfill is the only supported apply path. The operator CLI `scripts/rewash.py --kind
form5_xml` is not part of the change, and nothing schedules `run_rewash`. After the version bump
the CLI would re-apply every stored Form 5, the 403 expired holding payloads included, and store
their typed holding rows. Its holding observations would still be refused by the helper's
retention gate. Transaction observations follow their own unchanged path, so no equality is
claimed for them. The Form 4 rewash already behaves this way (residual below).


A script replays every stored `form5_xml` payload containing `erivativeHolding` through the
production parse and upsert path, with no refetch. That covers the 466 plus the 23
derivative-only payloads. Each needs an `insider_filings` row (the rewash precondition) and a
manifest `filed_at` in the Form 5 window, on the UTC date. The script then runs `sync_insiders`
with no `since` and no `LIMIT`, followed by the batch `_current` refresh. This is the shape of
`scripts/ab_3227_form4_holding_rows.py --apply`, and its `--verify` gains the Form 5 units.

### Unchanged

- **Form 5 transaction observations**, including their date.
- **The vehicle sum and the mixed-group fold.** `FORM4_VEHICLE_SUM_DOCUMENT_TYPES` stays
  `{"4", "4/A"}`.
- **The DERA ingest and de-collision.**

## Accepted residuals (not fixed here)

- **Mixed groups on Form 5** (23 `D`, 58 `I`). Each keeps today's latest-line value, because the
  fold is Form 4-only. All of them have a transaction line the writers keep.
- **Form 5 transaction dates.** A Form 5 transaction observation is dated by its `txn_date`
  (`insider_transactions.py:1921`), but its column 5 is the year-end balance, so the observation
  is dated earlier than its balance. This slice's holding groups never share a filing and D/I with
  one, because the holding-only test excludes that. Across filings, a Form 5 transaction balance
  can still lose to an older-dated balance that it postdates.
- **Gated groups** (5 amendments, 8 exit box, 11 delinquent Form 3) emit no holding observation.
  Their key is not guaranteed to keep today's value: when another group of the same accession
  takes a key, de-collision clause 1 drops that accession's DERA winners on every other key of the
  holder, gated or not. The census counts every such drop (2, both mislabels). The A/B lists every
  gated key whose `_current` row changes.
- **Refused groups** (21 multi-title, 5 equal amounts, 1 joint), as on Form 4.
- **Singleton groups skip the multi-line conditions.** A one-line group is written without the
  negative, blank-title, equal-amount or transaction-balance checks (0 such groups here). It also
  lands on the classless key whatever its class.
- **Nested blocks** (4(b)(iv)): an included block is refused only when its amount equals
  another's. Two singleton groups (`D` and `I`) of equal amount are both written.
- **One-line joint groups** (6) are written to `filers[0]`.

  All three are the Form 4 slice's residuals, unchanged.
- **Source rank over date.** `form4` outranks `form3` whatever the date; 0 keys are affected here.
- **Late filings** (11 over a year after the fiscal year end) are dated by the year end they state.
- **An operator rewash of expired filings** (`scripts/rewash.py --kind form5_xml`, as with
  `form4_xml`) stores typed holding rows the backfill skips. The holder-name resolver and the
  record-holder evidence would then read them. Holding observations are unaffected.

## Acceptance

Corpus-change evidence per `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, recorded on the build PR with SHAs:

1. **Unit tests**, both directions:
   - the Form 5 parser returns holdings and `holding_form_type`;
   - a holdings-only Form 5 is a filing, whether its holdings are non-derivative or derivative only;
   - a group dated `period_of_report`, with the signature date ignored;
   - `period_of_report` after the filed date, missing, or a missing manifest `filed_at`: each
     writes nothing;
   - the retention cutoff, on the boundary date and the day before, with a non-UTC timestamp;
   - each gate (5/A, exit box, code 3) writes nothing;
   - a summed group, and a refused multi-line group;
   - a mixed group keeps today's value;

   - a period of report before the Section 16 floor writes nothing;
   - the helper's retention and manifest gates refuse whatever the caller (live, weekly, rewash);
   - Form 4 behaviour unchanged.
   - A DB test drives both writers on stored Form 5 filings and requires equal observations from
     each. It is parameterised over: holdings-only; mixed; mixed with a transaction the writers do
     not keep (no balance, and an invalid date); each gate, including a code-3 line beside an
