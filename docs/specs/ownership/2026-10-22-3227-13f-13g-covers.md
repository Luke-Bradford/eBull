## Problem

The 13D arm finds M's cover C by `blockholder_filings.reporter_cik` = M. That column is NULL on every Schedule 13G row
(§ Source rule), so no 13G is ever a cover. A manager whose block is reported on a joint 13G stays counted twice: once
as its 13F figure, and once as the co-filer's figure.

Example: SARO. Carlyle Group Inc. (CIK 1527166) files a 13F of 84,587,035 for 2026-06-30.
- Its Schedule 13G/A `0001493152-26-023844` (filed 2026-05-15, Rule 13d-1(d)) is a joint statement of ten reporting
  persons. Every one reports sole dispositive 0, shared dispositive 84,587,035 and aggregate 84,587,035.
- One of them, Carlyle Partners VII S1 Holdings II, L.P. (CIK 2035691), has a kept `insiders` figure of 84,587,035
  (Form 4 `0001493152-26-008561`).
- The pie counts 169,174,070 for the two.

## Source rule

- **Rule 13d-1(k)(1)**, the cover-page fields and **Rule 13d-3(a)** apply as in the 13D arm's Source rule. A joint 13G is
  one statement *"with respect to the same securities"*.
- **Schedule 13G XML schema.** Source: EDGAR Schedule 13D and 13G XML Technical Specification, package 23,
  `eis_Schedule13G_Filer.xsd`, type `CP_REPORTING_PERSON_INFORMATION`. Each reporting-person block carries:
  - an **optional** `reportingCik` and `reportingCikNotPresentInEdgarFlag`;
  - `reportingPersonName`;
  - the voting and dispositive fields;
  - `reportingPersonBeneficiallyOwnedAggregateNumberOfShares`.

  The filer of record is `headerData/filerInfo/filer/filerCredentials/cik` (required).
- **EDGAR Filer Manual, Volume II (v75, June 2025), §8.1.12 "Completing a Schedule 13G Submission".**
  - *"The Filer CIK will be used to pre-populate the first Reporting Person CIK and name."* The "no EDGAR CIK" box
    *"will not be applicable for the first reporting person entry"*.
  - For any other reporting person, the CIK is entered *"if a CIK is available"*. The name is *"pre-populated from
    EDGAR based on the Reporting Person CIK, if a CIK is provided"*, and is *"entered manually"* otherwise.
  - This is the online-forms path. A filer submitting its own XML is held to the schema, not to the screens.
- **What the public document carries.** `reportingCik` appears in **0 of 41,580** stored 13G `primary_doc.xml` bodies
  (`filing_raw_documents`, `document_kind = 'primary_doc_13dg'`, every 13G accession in `blockholder_filings`; dev,
  2026-10-22).
  - Four separate facts, kept apart:
    - **schema:** the field is optional;
    - **online form:** the Filer Manual pre-populates it for the first person;
    - **SEC sample:** `Sample Schedule13G.xml` in the same package carries it;
    - **our corpus:** it never appears.

    Why the public bodies lack it, whether omitted at submission or removed at dissemination, is not documented, and
    this spec does not depend on the reason.
  - The EDGAR header of `0001493152-26-023844` (`-index-headers.html`) lists one `FILED BY` (1527166) besides the subject
    company.
  - **Result:** in our data, the only party to a 13G identified by CIK is the filer of record, from the header. Its
    place as the first reporting person follows from the Filer Manual on the online-forms path, and is an accepted
    assumption otherwise (risk 6). Any reporting person is identified by name only.
  - **Incidental:** the 13G parser reads `reportingPersonCik`/`reportingPersonCIK`
    (`sec_13dg.py` ~501), not the schema's `reportingCik`. This is moot while the element is absent, and it is noted on
    the PR.
- **The observation layer's 13G identity.** `resolve_blockholder_reporter_identity` (#1638) takes the chosen (largest)
  person's own CIK when present, and otherwise falls back to `<filerCredentials><cik>`.
  - With no per-person CIK, an observation's `reporter_cik` is the filer of record. Its `reporter_name` is the largest
    person's name, which need not be the filer's.

**Names.** For a person with a CIK, the Filer Manual ties the name to EDGAR's conformed name for that CIK; we never
receive that CIK. No rule makes an equal string the same entity. Name equality is a data-treatment decision with no
source rule, fixed by construction below and frozen here.

## Rule (frozen)

Everything in the 13D arm's § Rule applies, with these additions.

**Name.** `_normalise_holder_text` of the stored string:
- case, punctuation and `&` are folded;
- entity suffixes are kept, so `ACME LP` ≠ `ACME LLC`;
- two names match when the normalised strings are equal and non-empty.

**Name sources** are restricted to rows where the name and the CIK come from one reporting-owner record:
- **M's names:** its kept 13F rows' `filer_name` (the 13F filer).
- **k's names:** its kept Form 3, 4 and 5 rows' `filer_name` (`reportingOwnerId` pairs the owner's CIK and name).
- A kept 13D or 13G row never supplies a name, because its observation can pair one person's name with another's CIK
  (round 1, finding 7).

**13G accession.** A `blockholder_filings` accession with `submission_type` `SCHEDULE 13G` or `SCHEDULE 13G/A` on this
instrument, filed before P + 1 day (UTC).
- Its blocks are its rows there.
- Its **filer of record** is defined only when both hold:
  - every one of its rows has a NULL `reporter_cik`, so the observation fell back to the filer CIK;
  - its current `ownership_blockholders_observations` rows (`source = '13g'`, `known_to IS NULL`) are at least one, every
    one of their `reporter_cik` values gives a non-NULL `_digit_cik`, and those results are one distinct value.

  That value is the filer of record. Any NULL or non-digit current value makes the filer undefined; it is not filtered
  out. Measured on dev, 2026-10-22: 0 NULL and 0 non-digit among 37,977 current 13G observation rows. Closed rows are ignored: they can carry a pre-#1638 issuer CIK. 631 13G accessions
  have two distinct values across all rows, and 0 among current rows.

**M's statements**, filed before P + 1 day (UTC):
- the 13D arm's cover rows for M, by canonical `reporter_cik`;
- every 13G accession whose filer of record is M;
- every 13G accession with a block whose name matches one of M's names.

**C (13G)** is the latest of M's statements by `(filed_at, accession_number)`, when that latest statement is a 13G.
- If the latest statement is a 13D/G row found by CIK, this extension does nothing.
- C **matches** when it is **uniform at T**: every block on C has both dispositive fields non-NULL, sole + shared = T,
  and aggregate = T.

**Twin k (13G).** A #3898 node whose canonical CIK differs from M's and whose total equals T, found by **exactly one**
identity route, chosen by who filed C:
- **M is C's filer of record:** k has a name matching a block name on C. M is identified by CIK as the filer, and is
  taken to be on C as the first reporting person (risk 6). k is on C by name.
- **otherwise:** k's canonical CIK is C's filer of record. k is identified by CIK as the filer, and is taken to be on C
  as the first reporting person (risk 6). M is on C by name.

A case never rests on two name identifications.

**Order and uniqueness.**
1. The 13D arm runs exactly as built. Its matching, its uniqueness count and its folds are unchanged, and nothing below
   feeds back into them.
2. **The 13D arm's matched set** is the built `matched` dictionary: candidates whose 13D-arm cover reports T **and**
   carries at least one twin. This is before the 13D arm's uniqueness refusals, so it includes matches later refused.
   - A cover that reports T with no twin is **not** in the set.
   - The extension considers only candidates outside the set. A 13D-arm match refused for uniqueness is not
     reconsidered.
3. A 13G-matching candidate is a **duplicate** when:
   - it has exactly one twin;
   - that twin is no other 13G-matching candidate's twin;
   - that twin is no twin of any member of the 13D arm's matched set.

   A conflict refuses the 13G candidate only.

**Exception, declared.** The 13D arm sees no 13G. If M's newest statement is a 13G but an older 13D cover reports T
with a twin, the 13D arm still decides M on the older cover: it folds, or it refuses for uniqueness. That is the built
behaviour, and it stays. If the older 13D cover reports T with no twin, M is outside the matched set, and the extension
decides M on its newest statement.

**Why uniform.** No block on our 13G copy is tied to a person by CIK, except the first block by the Filer Manual's
convention. Attributing each block to a person would need a second name match. Requiring every block to report T means
the figure check needs no attribution. The cost is the non-uniform near misses below.

**The policy assumption.** As the 13D arm's, with C a 13G: the policy treats M's 13F figure as the block k's figure
already counts.

**Accepted identification risk (declared, not measured).** The 13D arm's risks 1–4 apply, plus these:

5. **Name identification.** One of M and k is on C by name only. A different entity with the same normalised name,
   among C's blocks or among the name-source rows, would be taken for it.
6. **The filer of record as first reporting person.** The Filer Manual makes the filer the first reporting person on
   the online-forms path. A directly submitted XML is not held to that screen convention, and in that case the
   filer-of-record party may not be on C at all.
7. **Statement selection.** Three cases:
   - a later 13G that names a same-named other entity (risk 5) displaces M's earlier genuine statement as C;
   - a genuine later 13G that names M under a spelling the normaliser does not equate (a rename or an abbreviation)
     is missed, so an older statement is chosen instead;
   - a genuine later 13G filed by M whose filer of record is undefined (no current observation, mixed current values,
     or a non-NULL block CIK) and which names M under no matching spelling is likewise missed.

   In the second and third cases an older uniform statement can decide the fold. This is declared, not prevented: an
   accession whose filer cannot be read is not evidence about M.
8. **Stored blocks.** Uniformity is checked over stored rows. The `blockholder_filings` upsert key
   `(accession_number, COALESCE(reporter_cik, ''), reporter_name)` collapses blocks that share a name on one accession.
   - Measured: 9 of 41,580 stored 13G bodies have more XML blocks than stored rows (`f13b_blocks.py`). All 9 are this
     duplicate-name collapse.
   - A collapsed block reporting another figure would read as uniform.
   - None of the census's duplicates is among the 9. The case review reconciles block counts against EDGAR for every
     duplicate.

**On risk 3 for a `blockholders` k.** When k's kept row is the observation of C itself, the date gap in risk 3 is
absent. It does not show that the observation is k's own block: its name is the largest person's. Uniformity makes
every block on C report T, which the policy relies on, conditional on risk 8.

## Change (read path only)

### 1. The 13G read

In the caller, only when a candidate exists, read two things:
- this instrument's 13G accessions filed before `max(P) + 1 day` (UTC), with accession, `filed_at`, `reporter_cik`,
  `reporter_name`, both dispositive fields and the aggregate;
- for those accessions, the current observations' `reporter_cik` (`source = '13g'`, `known_to IS NULL`).

Both reads are bounded by instrument and date, like the 13D arm's read. The 13D arm's cover read already returns the
13G rows, with a NULL `reporter_cik`. The build may extend that one read with `reporter_name` and `submission_type`
instead of adding a second query, and must say which it did. The filer-of-record conditions above are applied in
Python.

### 2. `_fold_13f_duplicates`

The extension runs in the same pure function, after the 13D arm's matching and uniqueness. Its own uniqueness is as
above. The fold itself, the tie-breaks, the provenance union and the guarantee are the 13D arm's. The correction is the
13D arm's, with this detail:

*"13F duplicate counted once (#3227): {M} ({M CIK}) 13F {T} for {P}; on joint Schedule 13G {C accession} (filed {date},
filer of record {CIK}) every stored reporting-person block reports dispositive power and aggregate amount {T}; {M | k}
is taken to be on it as filer of record and {k | M} is matched by name. Counted once by policy: the filer's presence
on the statement, identity by name, completeness of the stored blocks, overlap on that statement, across dates and in
class scope are not verified."*

### 3. Docstrings

- `_fold_13f_duplicates` and the section comment name this spec beside #3949.
- `_normalise_holder_text`'s docstring example becomes a real suffix distinction (`ACME LP` / `ACME LLC`). Its
  `BIOS FUND III/IV` example differs in the fund number, not the suffix (round 1, finding 14).

## Accepted residuals

1. The declared identification risk (§ Rule, items 1–8).
2. A non-uniform 13G stays counted twice. Example: a parent reporting T above fund blocks that sum to it.
3. Class B pairs with no 13G naming both, or none uniform at T, stay counted twice. These are RXT, BOBS, CRBP, MNTN, WLTH,
   and ten with no block equal to T at all (2026-10-22 research comment). The Form 3/4 joint filing carries no power
   field.
4. A case whose filer of record is neither M nor k, with both on C by name only, is not taken (one name identification
   per case).

## Acceptance (build PR)

As the 13D arm's Acceptance, with these changes.

**Full-population A/B**, either `scripts/ab_3227_13f_duplicates.py` extended or a sibling script. The base is the
real code at a pinned SHA where the 13D arm is built (`09da6224` or later main). The treated arm is the build. Two sets
of assertions are kept separate:
- **Combined pass:**
  - the treated pass's 13D-arm corrections equal the base's, member for member;
  - its 13G corrections are told apart by the detail's "joint Schedule 13G" wording.
- **Increment:**
  - the independent oracle carries its own frozen copy of the name sources, the filer-of-record read and the 13G rule,
    and it re-derives the 13D arm's matched set to compute reservations;
  - its 13G duplicate set equals the treated pass's 13G corrections, member for member;
  - per instrument, treated pie = base pie − Σ T over **13G duplicates only**;
  - #3898's edges and collapse list are unchanged;
  - newly oversubscribed = 0;
  - input − output total = Σ `shares_removed` over all corrections, both arms;
  - base, treated and oracle errors are each 0.

**Census reconciliation:** the 17 cases above, with any drift explained. Named figures are incremental against the base.

**Case review table in the PR:** for every 13G duplicate:
- M, k, T, C, its filer of record, P, and the identity route;
- every kept row of k and M;
- C's blocks reconciled against EDGAR `primary_doc.xml` (served under the issuer CIK path): **block count and every
  block's three figures**;
- **identity evidence**, also against that XML and the stored rows:
  - the filer of record equals `filerCredentials/cik`;
  - the matched name is a `reportingPersonName` in the XML;
  - the matched name comes from a permitted name-source row (M's 13F row, or k's Form 3, 4 or 5 row), which the table
    names.

  Any mismatch stops the build for a re-decision, as the 13D arm's gate failures do.

**Latency:** the 13D arm's gate, on the full population and on the affected instruments.

**Named figures:** SARO (−84,587,035); RXT unchanged (residual 3).

**Corpus evidence:** smoke panel pie totals before and after; no backfill; a live re-check of
`/instruments/SARO/ownership-rollup`.

**Tests** (pure unless stated):

- **Folds:** the SARO shape (M filer of record, k by Form 4 name), and the WAY/EQT shape (k filer of record, M by
  13F name).
- **Identity routes:**
  - M not filer, with k named on C but not the filer, refuses (no two-name case);
  - k not M's filer but C's filer of record, which also has a permitted name matching a block on C, is found by the
    filer route only, and the correction names that route;
  - k with two permitted names, each matching a different block on C, gives one twin;
  - k's only matching name comes from a kept 13G or 13D row, which is not a name source, and refuses.
- **Each refusal alone:**
  - a block off T on C;
  - a NULL dispositive field or NULL aggregate on a block that is neither M's nor k's;
  - k's total ≠ T;
  - k's canonical CIK = M's;
  - no 13G naming M or filed by M;
  - M named only on a 13G filed at or after P + 1 day 00:00 UTC (P 23:59:59.999 is included).
- **Filer of record:**
  - **undefined** for:
    - zero current observation rows;
    - two distinct valid current values;
    - a valid value plus a NULL;
    - a valid value plus a non-digit value;
    - a lone non-digit value;
    - a row on the accession with a non-NULL `reporter_cik`;
  - **defined** for:
    - zero-padded and space-padded forms of one CIK, which count as one value;
    - a closed row with another CIK, which is ignored;
  - an undefined filer refuses a case on either route: M cannot be the filer, and k cannot be found by filer.
- **C selection:**
  - a newer 13D row by CIK pre-empts an older uniform 13G, so the extension does nothing;
  - a newer non-uniform 13G naming M pre-empts an older uniform one (no fallback);
  - a 13D and a 13G with the same `filed_at` are ordered by accession;
  - two candidates with different P are served by one batched read.
- **Order and uniqueness:**
