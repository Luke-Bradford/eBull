Title: Foreign entity classification for UK tax purposes: List of classifications of foreign entities for UK tax purposes
Public updated at: 2023-12-12T07:59:59+00:00

This list is intended to provide HMRC officers and customers with a general view as to whether the members will be liable to UK tax on the profits, income or gains of the entities or only on any distributions made by the entities. INTM180040 however explains how our view of a specific entity may vary and how we would reach a definitive view.
