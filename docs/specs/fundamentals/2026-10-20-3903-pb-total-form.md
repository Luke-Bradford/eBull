# #3903: the valuation view's P/B as market cap / equity in both arms

**Ticket:** #3903 (remainder: legacy `pb_ratio`; refs #3850, #3841). **Status:** spec v4. Codex checkpoint 1 ran three
rounds, the cap: round 1 raised 21 findings, round 2 left 4 open and added 8, round 3 left 3 open and added 7, each
UNSOUND. v4 applies round 3 without a fourth round, as #3910 and #3915 did ("Checkpoint record").

`instrument_valuation.pb_ratio` is the last per-share column. Its legacy arm divides `metric_price` (`current_price`
÷ a curated ADS ratio, else 1) by a stored book value per share; its complete-TTM arm already divides the #3850 market
cap by equity. This spec gives the legacy arm the complete arm's form on the same equity column, adds the source's
18-month staleness bound and a USD guard to both arms, and so makes the column count-based under #3850's invariant.
P/B is the reciprocal of JKP's book-to-market `be_me`. It follows #3911's P/E and #3917's dividend yield
(`docs/specs/fundamentals/2026-10-19-3903-pe-total-form.md`, `…-dividend-yield-total-form.md`).

### Local rules, stated as departures

- **The equity is the stored `shareholders_equity`, a proxy for `be_x`, unchanged.** The legacy arm moves to the
  column the complete arm already divides by. The normaliser stores `StockholdersEquity`, else
  `StockholdersEquityIncludingPortionAttributableToNoncontrollingInterest`
  (`app/providers/implementations/sec_fundamentals.py:227-230`): `seq` with preferred stock included and no deferred
  taxes added, or, in the fallback, equity including noncontrolling interests. Section 7 measures the gap against a
  **partial SEC proxy of `be_x`**, not against `be_x` itself: SEQ + TXDITC − PSTK at the quarter end from
  companyfacts, first concept with a USD instant from the lists `app/services/factor_panel.py:129-131` uses, without
  JKP's SEQ fallbacks or its `sum(txdb, itcb)` branch, and without an established equivalence between those SEC
  concepts and the Compustat items.
  - Which concept the stored E equals: complete arm SEQ 2,303, NCI-inclusive 124; legacy SEQ 115, NCI-inclusive 2.
  - Complete arm, 2,427 publications: stored E ÷ proxy in [0.95, 1.05] on 1,810, outside on 491 (TXDITC alone 421,
    PSTK alone 49, both 21), no SEQ instant 124, proxy ≤ 0 on 2. Legacy, 117: in band 91, outside 24 (TXDITC alone
    21, PSTK alone 2, both 1), no SEQ instant 2. MTD's stored E is 0.081 of its proxy.
  - `financial_facts_raw` holds 0 rows on the TXDITC and PSTK lists, so the view cannot read them. Moving both arms
    to `be_x` needs those concepts ingested, the NCI fallback separated, and an equivalence established; it is a
    separate corpus change, filed as a follow-up. This spec keeps one equity definition across both arms.
- **BE ≤ 0 is NULL.** JKP publishes `be_me` for negative BE. The view's existing display policy (the complete arm,
  sql/201) refuses a P/B on non-positive equity; kept.
- **No 4-month publication lag.** JKP's `lag_to_public = 4` stands in for an unknown filing date. A stored period
  exists only from a filing the normaliser has read.
- **The bound is evaluated at the session.** "Usable through" is the month-end 18 months after the equity's quarter
  end (`intnx('month', datadate, 18, 'e')`); the value is NULL when `session` is later. Section 5: no proposed
  publication is refused by it at this run; the oldest equity quarter end is 292 days before its session (C).
- **The view reads the latest quarterly row, not the next value's start.** `financial_periods_ttm`'s stock items are
  the latest Q1–Q4 row's (`sql/508…`'s `FILTER (WHERE rn = 1)`); an FY row is not read. Section 8 finds 12 cap-published rows whose latest
  normalized FY row with equity ends after that quarter: 11 complete-arm (NTNX −725.6M quarter against +702.6M FY,
  HRB −24.4M against +117.5M, MCFT 189.1M against 381.1M, and 8 more) and 1 legacy (PURR 743.5M against 1,872.9M).
  This is a property of every stock item in `financial_periods_ttm` (ROE, D/E and EV read the same row), so it is
  filed as a follow-up for that view, not patched into P/B alone.
- **The view pairs today's latest filing with the session close.** Section 5: one proposed publication's equity
  quarter was filed after its session (SKYT, filed 2026-08-07, session 2026-07-30); every other one was filed on or
  before it. This holds for every count-based column of the view, which is a live view, not a PIT panel.
- **Currency.** JKP converts with `fx`. The equity's unit: the extractor reads only the units in `_UNIT_PRIORITY =
  ("USD", "USD/shares", "shares", "pure")` (`app/providers/implementations/sec_fundamentals.py:446`, `:495`), so no
  currency other than USD can enter; it does not bind a unit to the equity field, and section 6 measures that every
  stored fact on the two equity concepts is in `USD` (178,750 and 86,260). That is evidence on today's facts, not a
  field-level invariant; a field-level unit check in the normaliser is part of the currency follow-up. The cap is in the
  instrument's trading currency, and the #3841 PIT build checks no currency (`grep -i currency
  sql/499_market_cap_pit.sql` returns nothing). Section 6: every view row with a published cap trades in USD
  (2,774), and the 6 rows with NULL `instruments.currency` all have a refused cap. That is this run's population,
  not a guarantee, so the construction refuses a P/B unless `instruments.currency = 'USD'` (NULL refuses). No
  conversion is added. The other count-based columns carry the same exposure; follow-up.

## Construction

### One view (sql/509)

**`instrument_valuation`** (sql/507's body; sql/508 replaced only `financial_periods_ttm`):

1. Both arms drop `legacy_pb_ratio` and carry two internal columns in its place: `pb_equity` and `pb_equity_end`.
   - `new_pipeline`: `ttm.shareholders_equity`, `ttm.ttm_end`.
   - `legacy`: the same two columns from a `LEFT JOIN financial_periods_ttm ttm` on the instrument. The arm's
     `NOT EXISTS` already excludes a complete window; an incomplete window still carries its latest quarter's stock
     items. The P/B no longer reads any `fundamentals_snapshot` value, but the arm's row still exists only when a
     snapshot row exists (its `JOIN LATERAL … fs ON TRUE`, `sql/507…:345-351`, kept), so an instrument with stored
     equity and no snapshot row has no legacy row and no P/B, as today.
   - The final SELECT lists its outputs explicitly and emits neither.
2. Final SELECT: `pb_ratio = market_cap_live / pb_equity` when `pb_equity > 0`, `pb_equity < 'Infinity'` and
   `session <= (date_trunc('month', pb_equity_end + interval '18 months') + interval '1 month - 1 day')::date` and
   the instrument's `instruments.currency = 'USD'` (a `LEFT JOIN instruments` on the instrument id); else NULL. `market_cap_live` is NULL whenever `market_cap_reason` is set, so the legacy arm's dual-class and FPI
   suppression (`sql/507…:389`) is subsumed by those reasons and removed. `shareholders_equity` is `numeric(20,4)`
   and holds no ±Infinity; `NaN` sorts above every number, which the upper guard refuses (#3911 Amendment 1,
   finding 2).
3. Output columns, their order, types and typmods are unchanged; the legacy arm's output `shareholders_equity` stays
   NULL. Every other column is unchanged, including the legacy arm's `debt_equity_ratio` (`book_value ×
   shares_outstanding`) and both arms' output `shares_outstanding`, which still carries the stored quarter-end count
   (MTD's 21,683,802).
4. **Comments.** sql/509's header and `COMMENT ON VIEW instrument_valuation` state the rule and drop "the legacy arm's
   pb_ratio is the last per-share column".

## Acceptance (corpus rung)

`scripts/measure_3903_pb_acceptance.py`, in one REPEATABLE READ transaction that is rolled back. **Control:** sql/507's
`instrument_valuation`, as deployed. **Treatment:** sql/509's body created as a temporary view. It runs after the
08:20Z PIT build and before the evening candle refresh (the `basis_stale` churn, #3917 spec "Problem").

1. **Wiring, full population.**
   - The public interface: ordered column names, types and typmods of control and treatment (`pg_attribute`) are
     equal.
   - Rows: the multiset of `instrument_id` is equal; every column other than `pb_ratio` is equal under `IS NOT
     DISTINCT FROM` on every row.
   - Values: the NULL mask of treatment `pb_ratio` equals that of an independent evaluation, and every published
     value agrees within relative 1e-12. The evaluation also derives each expected NULL's cause; the view exposes
     only `market_cap_reason`, so a P/B-specific cause (E, the bound, currency) is checked by the synthetic fixtures
     of item 5, not read from the view. The evaluation reads `financial_periods` directly (the latest Q1–Q4, normalized,
     non-superseded row after sql/220's per-period-end dedup, latest filed winning), its `shareholders_equity` and
     end date, and the control's `market_cap_live`, `market_cap_reason` and `session`, and applies the construction
     in Python with the non-finite and currency guards.
   - Currency: no treatment `pb_ratio` is published on a row whose `instruments.currency IS DISTINCT FROM 'USD'`.
   - Ties: no instrument's latest quarter has two normalized, non-superseded Q rows at the winning filed-date rank
     (`filed_date DESC NULLS LAST`) that disagree on `shareholders_equity` (the view's `DISTINCT ON` would pick
     either); any such row fails. A tie at a losing rank is printed, not failed. Section 9: 5 tied groups, 1
     disagreeing, 0 at a latest quarter's winning rank.
2. **A/B, row-level.** The publication-state table (section 2's form) is built from the full population, both arms.
   Every row whose state or value differs from the control is then listed with exactly one attribution, read from
   the control's and the treatment's own publication predicates:
   - lost: the cap is refused; E is NULL, non-finite or ≤ 0; the 18-month bound; or the currency guard;
   - gained: the control's predicate failed on `book_value` NULL (the quarter count NULL or zero, printed),
     `book_value` ≤ 0 with the stored E positive (the four-place scale or a snapshot quarter that differs from the
     stored latest quarter, printed), or `metric_price` ≤ 0;
   - changed value, publishing both ways: item 3's decomposition, for every such row.
   Any row without an attribution fails. The counts are compared with "Expected movement" and the difference
   reported; it is not a failure, because the cap population moves between runs (`basis_stale`), and each changed
   row is already attributed within the run.
3. **Factor decomposition.** For every legacy row publishing both ways whose value changed, the three factors are
   printed and their product asserted equal to today ÷ treatment within 1e-9. Each factor outside [0.99, 1.01] must
   carry its provenance, else the row fails: price, `current_price` with its source (a quote with `price_as_of`, or
   a daily close with its date) against the session and its close, the legitimate cause being a quote or a daily
   close later than the session; equity, the snapshot's `book_value` and count against the stored equity (the
   four-place scale, or a snapshot quarter that differs from the stored latest quarter); count, the stored instants
   and the PIT step (concept, accession, context date).
4. **External values.** Against a published P/B and market cap on one page (stockanalysis, else companiesmarketcap),
   recording the page's P/B, market cap, price and date. External equity E_x = market cap_x ÷ P/B_x, derived from
   the page's own two figures.
   - Slots, by category, distinct names: panel AAPL, MSFT, JPM, HD; gained DVN, FERG, CNMD; count factor PURR, MTD;
     the two kept legacy rows with the largest proposed values. GME is checked against its expected state (published
     or NULL with its cause) and is not a slot.
   - **Decomposition, algebraic:** ours ÷ external = (cap ÷ cap_x) × (E_x ÷ E), asserted per row within 1e-9.
   - **Pass:** |ours ÷ external − 1| ≤ 15%. Otherwise only these are divided out, and the residual must be within
     ±15%: the cap factor, only under #3911 Amendment 1 rule 2 (the count factor; price is never divided out); the
     equity factor, only when both endpoints are verified: our E equals, within 0.01%, the filed instant of the
     concept the normaliser's priority selects at E's period end (accession printed), and E_x matches, within 1%, a
     filed figure for a later period than E or that filing's NCI-inclusive total (figure and accession printed).
     Anything else fails.
   - **Coverage:** availability and correctness are separate. Every slot with a published external P/B and market
     cap is measured, and every measured slot that fails the rule above blocks. A slot may go unmeasured only when the
     page publishes no P/B or market cap for the name (recorded with the URL and date); at most 2 of the 11 may be
     unmeasured, every category keeps at least one measured name, and PURR and MTD are mandatory while their caps
     publish. A slot whose cap is refused at the run is reported with its
     reason and the row's treatment `pb_ratio` must be NULL; it is replaced by the largest proposed-published name of
     its category not already a slot. ±15% and 1% are chosen by construction, as in #3911 and #3917.
5. **DB tests (synthetic), each in both arms where the arm applies:**
   - a legacy row publishes market cap / E; a legacy row with E and NULL quarter shares publishes (sql/507 NULL); a
     legacy row with E and zero quarter shares publishes;
   - a refused cap → NULL with its reason; E ≤ 0 → NULL; `NaN` E → NULL; the latest quarter's E NULL with an older
     quarter's E positive → NULL (no fallback);
   - the bound: E quarter end 2024-02-29 publishes at session 2025-08-31 and is NULL at 2025-09-01; a 52/53-week
     quarter end 2024-03-30 publishes at session 2025-09-30 and is NULL at 2025-10-01;
   - the price: a row whose `current_price` differs from the session close publishes on the session's cap;
   - a legacy `dual_class` row and a legacy `fpi_adr` row → NULL with the reason;
   - a row with a published cap and `instruments.currency` `EUR`, and one with it NULL → NULL;
   - an instrument with stored equity and a cap but no `fundamentals_snapshot` row and no complete window → no row,
     as under sql/507;
   - a split: quarter-end count 10M, E 100M, post-split PIT count 40M, close 25 → 1,000M / 100M = 10, where sql/507's
     per-share form on the back-adjusted price gives 25 / 10 = 2.5.
6. **Latency.** Control and treatment each run: the bulk read `SELECT * FROM <view>`, and 50 per-instrument reads
   (`WHERE instrument_id = %s`) over a fixed list, 25 per arm, the lowest `instrument_id`s with a published cap.
   One warm-up pass, then 5 timed passes; the statistic is the median wall time per query shape. Treatment ÷
   control above 2× on either shape blocks (#3850's bound).
7. **Corpus evidence** (CLAUDE.md "Corpus changes"): the smoke panel; item 4 as the cross-source check; backfill
   none (a view); live re-check after deploy; the thesis-window listing.
