# #3903: the valuation view's P/E on the total/ME form

**Ticket:** #3903 (refs #3850, #3841). **Status:** spec v4. Codex checkpoint 1 ran three rounds, the cap: round 1
18 findings, round 2 18 new or remaining, round 3 11, each UNSOUND. v3 narrowed the scope to P/E; v4 applies round 3
without a fourth round. "Checkpoint record" gives every disposition.

`instrument_valuation.pe_ratio` divides a price by a per-share earnings figure summed across a split. This spec moves
it onto the #3850 market cap and TTM net income. The other per-share columns, `dividend_yield` and the legacy arm's
`pb_ratio`, stay on #3903 (see "Not in scope").

## Problem

Figures are printed by `PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/measure_3903_premise.py` (dev DB, run 2026-10-19 19:22Z,
code `5e6ead9b`, PIT build 7). Its reads share one REPEATABLE READ snapshot. Section numbers refer to that output.
Today's P/E is priced on the live quote, so counts that compare it move by a row or two between runs (a 19:25Z rerun
put 80 rows outside the band, not 79); the proposed P/E is priced at the session close and does not.

sql/505 computes `pe_ratio = metric_price / eps_diluted_ttm` when `eps_diluted_ttm > 0` in the complete-TTM arm
(`sql/505_instrument_valuation_pit_market_cap.sql:122`), and `metric_price / fs.eps` in the legacy arm (line 179).
`eps_diluted_ttm` sums the window's four quarterly `EarningsPerShareDiluted` values
(`sql/220_ttm_strict_flow_sums.sql:171-174`). Each quarter is on the share basis of the filing that reported it, and
eToro's price is back-adjusted for every split (#3841 "Problem"). Section 6 prints each named window:

1. **Summed across a split.** BKNG's window is EPS 84.41 and 43.66 (weighted diluted shares 32.6M), then 1.36 and
   2.53 (794M and 770M): `eps_diluted_ttm` 131.96, `pe_ratio` 1.23.
2. **A derived Q4 across a split.** NFLX's Q4 2025 row carries EPS −17.14 beside net income +$2.42B; TPL's carries
   −8.59 beside +$123M. NFLX's `eps_diluted_ttm` is −9.24, so it publishes no P/E.
3. **A positive per-share sum beside a loss.** 44 published P/E rows have `net_income_ttm ≤ 0`; 31 of them have
   P/E ≤ 10 (section 4), which `_value_score`'s fallback path maps to the maximum `pe_score` 1.0
   (`app/services/scoring.py:808-810`).
4. **APH** publishes P/E 20.2 while #3841 refuses its cap as `basis_unresolved` (section 6).

**Discrepancy inventory** (sections 2–3). Over the 1,439 complete-TTM rows publishing both today's P/E and the
proposed one, today ÷ proposed lies in [0.8, 1.25] for 1,360, and below 0.5 or above 2 for 26. Each of the 79 outside
the band is tagged with the first stored-operand predicate it matches, in this precedence (the predicates overlap,
so the order sets the counts; `inventory_cause` in the script). A tag is a **candidate cause**, not a verified one:

| precedence | predicate on the stored window operands | rows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | weighted diluted shares spread ≥ 1.5× inside the window | 17 |
| 2 | a quarter's EPS sign opposes its net income | 7 |
| 3 | Σ (EPS × weighted diluted shares) differs from `net_income_ttm` by more than the band | 26 |
| 4 | the PIT effective count against the latest weighted diluted shares, outside the band | 12 |
| — | no predicate matches (untagged) | 17 |

The thresholds (1.5×, the band) are chosen by construction. The 17 untagged rows are listed in section 3.

### The numerator is a proxy

`net_income_ttm` sums four quarters of `us-gaap:NetIncomeLoss`
(`app/providers/implementations/sec_fundamentals.py:201`), strictly by sql/220. That is the IB fallback branch
without its `xido*` deduction (discontinued operations and extraordinary items). The published P/E is therefore
**market cap ÷ TTM `NetIncomeLoss`, a stated proxy for JKP's ME / NI***.

- **Why not the documented construction now.** `financial_periods` has no `xido*` or IB column, and
  `financial_facts_raw` holds none of the concepts it would need: `IncomeLossFromContinuingOperations`, both
  `IncomeLossFromDiscontinuedOperationsNetOfTax` concepts and `ExtraordinaryItemNetOfTax` have 0 rows against
  `NetIncomeLoss` 179,554 (section 9). Building IB is a corpus change (tracked concepts, normaliser, backfill), filed
  by the build PR.
- **The deviation on today's view, measured.** `PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/measure_3903_ib_deviation.py`
  rebuilds NI* per window quarter from the local SEC `companyfacts.zip` (2026-10-17, SHA-256 file `12bad36a…`):
  `IncomeLossFromContinuingOperations` when filed, else `NetIncomeLoss` − DO − XI, an unfiled DO or XI counted as
  zero. That zero is a simplification of the panel's witness rules, which the script states. Over the 2,471
  complete-TTM rows with a published cap and a `net_income_ttm`:
  - **the rule's own check:** applied to `NetIncomeLoss`, it agrees with the stored quarterly `net_income` within
    0.5% on 9,437 quarters, differs on 349 and cannot resolve 36; 32 windows have a quarter with neither IB nor NI;
  - **size:** where both TTMs are positive (1,471 windows), NI* ÷ `net_income_ttm` lies in [0.95, 1.05] for 1,343.
    The proposed P/E would move by more than 10% on 102 of them if NI* replaced `net_income_ttm`, and by more than
    25% on 63. The largest moves (IBEX, FDX, AIT: factors near 10³–10⁶) come from IB facts far below NI, which the
    measurement does not adjudicate, so they bound the deviation from above rather than measure it;
  - **sign:** `net_income_ttm > 0` with NI* ≤ 0 on 21 rows. The proxy publishes a P/E for them where JKP's E/P is not
    positive. The reverse, `net_income_ttm ≤ 0` with NI* > 0, is 40 rows the proxy refuses.

  The historical #3609 panel agrees in kind: of 189,884 name-months on the NI − `xido*` branch, a DO term was read
  from a filing in 1,738 + 5,513 + 246 (section 9).
- **Why ship the proxy anyway.** Today's per-share P/E is not ME / NI* either. Its numerator is income available to
  common, with preferred dividends and NCI between it and `NetIncomeLoss` (ASC 260-10-45-10, -11 and -16, as cited at
  `app/services/fundamentals/__init__.py:1235`). On top of that it carries the split defects above, which are wrong
  by construction. The proxy's measured deviation (above) is smaller than today's form's disagreement with it. ALB shows the numerator gap: its window's Σ (EPS × diluted
  shares) is $98.1M against `NetIncomeLoss` $224.2M (section 6), and its P/E moves from 367 to 50.

**Why not restate the per-share figures.** Restating needs each split's date and ratio. There is no split register
(#3841 "Problem"). #3841's factor certifies the share basis of the session close and nothing else. A derived Q4
cannot be repaired per share without knowing which of its two inputs is on which basis. A dollar total has no share
basis, so the only basis left in the ratio is the cap's, which #3841 checks.

## Construction

### The view (sql/506)

`CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW instrument_valuation`, sql/505's body with these changes. Output columns, their order and
their types are unchanged.

1. **Complete-TTM arm:** `pe_ratio = market_cap_live / net_income_ttm` when `net_income_ttm > 0` and
   `net_income_ttm < 'Infinity'`, else NULL. PostgreSQL sorts `NaN` above `Infinity`, so the second guard refuses
   `NaN` too. It is computed in the final SELECT beside the other count-based columns. `market_cap_live` is already
   finite-checked by PIT (`invalid_input`).
2. **Legacy arm:** `pe_ratio` NULL. `fundamentals_snapshot` carries no earnings total, and its `eps` is the same
   per-share sum. No legacy row publishes a P/E today (section 7).
3. **Consequences:**
   - `pe_ratio` is a count-based column under #3850's invariant: NULL whenever `market_cap_reason` is set. The
     separate FPI suppression on `pe_ratio` is subsumed by the `fpi_adr` reason and is removed.
   - It is priced at `valuation_price`, the session close, not `current_price`.
   - Nothing else changes: `pb_ratio` (both arms) and `dividend_yield` keep sql/505's expressions and suppressions.
4. **Comments.** sql/506's header and `COMMENT ON VIEW` state the new formula and price base, and that
   `dividend_yield` and legacy `pb_ratio` are the per-share columns left.

`net_income_ttm` has no non-finite value today (section 8); the guard's latency is measured under acceptance item 6.

### Readers

- **Scoring.** `_value_score` (`app/services/scoring.py:728-830`) reads `pe_ratio` only on its fallback path, when
  `base_value` is NULL (no thesis, or a thesis without per-share targets) and `current_price > 0`. There it blends P/E
  (nominal 35%), FCF yield (35%) and price-target upside (30%), re-normalised over the components present. With P/E
  alone it is 100% of the score; with no component present the score is 0.5 and the notes carry `NO_INPUT_NOTE`. A
  P/E gained, lost or changed can move the value score either way, or into or out of the no-input result. No code
  change.
- **Peer comparison** (`app/services/peer_comparison.py:187`) shows `pe_ratio` as "P/E (TTM)", lower is better. Its
  module docstring states the new definition; label and ranking are unchanged.
- **Thesis.** `_WRITER_SYSTEM` (`app/services/thesis.py:1339-1342`, `:1446-1447`) names the price base per multiple.
  The build changes it so that:
  - P/E joins the market-cap set, priced at `valuation_price`;
  - P/E is defined as market cap ÷ TTM net income, and P/B (when `is_complete_ttm`) as market cap ÷ equity. Neither
    is price ÷ the context's `fundamentals.eps` or `fundamentals.book_value`, so a target built as "multiple × eps"
    or "multiple × book_value" chooses its own multiple and does not reuse `valuation.pe_ratio` or
    `valuation.pb_ratio` as that basis's current multiple;
  - dividend yield, and P/B when `is_complete_ttm` is not true, stay on `current_price`.

  `_PROMPT_VERSION` becomes v9, because a context field's meaning changes.
- **Not readers.** `app/api/instruments.py` key stats and `fair_value_band.py` compute their own per-share P/E from
  `fundamentals_snapshot` and do not read the view's `pe_ratio`. Until #3904 lands the instrument page's P/E and the
  view's are different constructions and can disagree; the build PR posts that on #3904.
- **Frozen consumers.** `ranking_pot` and `ai_trial_pack` do not read `pe_ratio` (grep at `5e6ead9b`). The build PR
  runs `scripts.ai_trial_policy_guard` to confirm no policy hash moved.

### Deploy order and the thesis window

The API process applies migrations at boot (`app/main.py:141`); the jobs child picks up new code only when it
respawns, which waits for running jobs (CLAUDE.md "Branch, PR and merge" item 6). Between the two, a thesis run uses
the v8 prompt over the v9 view: it reads P/E as priced at `current_price` and as price ÷ EPS. A thesis built in that
window can re-rate P/E from the wrong price and definition, and its targets feed `_value_score`'s primary path, so the
effect can reach scores, not only the context text.

The view and the code cannot switch atomically without a gate the thesis path does not have. Instead the window is
measured and repaired:
- **forward:** every `theses` row with `prompt_version` v8 whose run (`thesis_runs`) overlaps the interval from sql/506's
  `schema_migrations.applied_at` to the jobs child's PID change (`started_at` before the end and `finished_at` after
  the start, or not finished) is listed on the PR and regenerated through the existing manual trigger. The next
  scoring run reads the regenerated targets;
- **rollback:** the same query with `prompt_version` v9 over the interval from the rollback migration's apply time to
  the reverted jobs child's PID change.

## Acceptance (corpus rung)

`scripts/measure_3903_acceptance.py`, in #3850's form: the control is sql/505's body and the treatment sql/506's,
each created as a temporary `instrument_valuation`, read in one REPEATABLE READ transaction that is rolled back.

1. **Wiring, full population.** The row sets are equal. Every published `pe_ratio` equals `market_cap_live /
   net_income_ttm` recomputed in `Decimal` within relative 1e-12 (#3850's `REL_TOL`; PostgreSQL numeric division and
   `Decimal` division round differently). NULL decisions are exact. Every other column equals the control exactly.
2. **A/B.** Sections 1, 3 and 5 re-printed over the two arms. A difference from "Expected movement" is explained by
   data that changed since the premise run.
3. **Scorer, both arms** (bulk load, overlay and `_score_from_data`, no persistence). Value-family score changes,
   segmented by path (primary or fallback), by which fallback components are present in each arm, and by transitions
   into and out of the no-input result (identified by `NO_INPUT_NOTE`, not by the value 0.5); then full-population
   rank moves. Each move is attributed to the instrument's own P/E input or to peers; an own-input move with any
   other cause fails.
4. **Independent values.**
   - **Expected NULL:** BKNG (`net_income_ttm` NULL) and APH (cap refused).
   - **External P/E** against a published TTM P/E (stockanalysis or companiesmarketcap), recording the external
     price, its date and its TTM EPS, for: the five-name panel (AAPL, GME, MSFT, JPM, HD); NFLX, ORLY and TPL (split
     windows); ALB (numerator); and the five untagged rows with the largest |log(today ÷ proposed)|.
   - **Reconciliation, an exact identity.** With V = `valuation_price`, N = the PIT effective count (so
     `market_cap_live` = V·N), I = `net_income_ttm`, P and E the external price and TTM EPS, and L the latest
     weighted diluted share count **from the filing** (not the stored column):
     ours ÷ external = (V·N/I) ÷ (P/E) = (V/P) × (N/L) × (L·E/I).
     The three factors are price, count and earnings. None reads our per-share EPS, which this change displaces. The
     acceptance script asserts the identity numerically for every checked row before any factor is interpreted.
   - **Pass:** |ours ÷ external − 1| ≤ 15%; or each factor outside [0.85, 1.15] is matched to a filing figure the PR
     cites (the earnings factor to `NetIncomeLoss` against income available to common, or a window difference; the
     count factor to the cover or balance-sheet count against L). A factor that exposes a wrong **treatment input**
     (`net_income_ttm` or the PIT count) is a defect: the PR files it, and the row fails. ±15% is chosen by
     construction.
   - **Treatment-input check, flagged population.** For every row in the section 3 inventory and every
     `net_income_ttm > 0`, NI* ≤ 0 row of the IB measurement, the script compares `net_income_ttm` with the
     companyfacts `NetIncomeLoss` TTM by the measurement's rule. A mismatch beyond 0.5% is listed as a treatment-input
     defect candidate. `net_income_ttm` already feeds `net_margin`, `roa` and `roe` in the same view, so such a defect
     is pre-existing: the PR files the list as one ticket and does not block on it. The external sample is
     corroboration of the construction, not proof over the population.
5. **DB tests (synthetic):** published; `net_income_ttm` 0, negative, NULL, `NaN`, `Infinity` and `-Infinity` → NULL;
   a refused cap → NULL with its reason; an `fpi_adr` row, NULL before and after, with its reason; a `dual_class` row
   and an `ads_ratio` row whose sql/505 P/E is positive → NULL with the reason; a legacy row with a positive `fs.eps`
   whose sql/505 P/E is positive → NULL; a window summing
   per-share EPS across a 25-for-1 split, which sql/505 publishes at about 1.2 and sql/506 at the total form.
6. **Latency.** Per-instrument and bulk reads, both arms; a regression above 2× blocks (#3850's bound, by
   construction).
7. **Corpus evidence** (CLAUDE.md "Corpus changes"): the smoke panel; item 4 as the cross-source check; backfill none
   (a view); live re-check after deploy; the thesis-window listing above.

## Not in scope

- **`dividend_yield`.** Section 10 records coverage observations, not causes:
  - sql/220 sums `dps_declared` over its present members, reading an absent quarter as no dividend. Of 1,068 rows with
    `dps_declared_ttm > 0`, 223 sum fewer than four non-NULL members. Whether an absent member is a missed fact or a
    quarter with no dividend is not established here.
  - Over 773 rows with both a DPS yield and a paid-cash yield, the two agree within [0.8, 1.25] on 598.

  The total form (`dividends_paid_ttm` ÷ cap) shares the coverage question. A construction needs filing evidence that
  separates missing from zero, and a discrepancy decomposition, first.
- **Legacy `pb_ratio`** (`metric_price / fs.book_value`, per share). Section 7: 147 published; against market cap ÷
  the period row's equity, 99 of the 101 comparable lie within [0.8, 1.25]. Moving it needs a provenance-exact equity
  source: `book_value` is stored at 4 decimals, and the snapshot carries no reference to its source row.
- **The derived-Q4 per-share rule** (FY − nine months across bases). After this change no view column reads
  `eps_diluted_ttm`.

Filed by the build PR:
- `net_income_ttm` NULL on 309 complete-TTM rows (173 with a published cap): a normaliser coverage question;
- IB / `xido*` for the numerator: a corpus change, carrying the measured deviation above;
- the treatment-input defect candidates from acceptance item 4.
