## 1. What this is

**Hypothesis.** Inside v1's fixed policy (same S₀ rules, R_t and F_t, N = 25 buy/hold band, ATR stop and target,
monthly calendar, costs, simulator), ordering R_t by a composite of the v1.5 score, low FINRA days-to-cover and
recent opportunistic insider purchases earns more per open position-session than (a) the same composite with the two
**new** components attached to names at random within score strata, and (b) the v1.5 order itself on the same
inputs; and beats SPY.

**What the decision rests on.** Two things, both required (§7): the **attachment test** — given each name's own v1.5
score and its score stratum, does the real attachment of the new components beat random attachments (§6; an
observational permutation comparison, read as v1 spec §1 reads its own, under the assumption stated in §6); and the
**v1-reference condition** — over the window v2's shadow must have realised strictly more per position-session, and
no less NAV, than a book run on v2's own snapshots with the v1.5 order (§4). The first alone could pass for a
component that merely re-weights the score (a score proxy reproducing v1's order beats degraded permutations, then
ties the reference and fails the strict inequality); the second alone has no null. The pass reading is therefore
narrow: "beat stratified-random attachment, and out-earned the v1.5 order on the same inputs in this window" — not an
estimate of expected superiority over v1. The reference is v1's order **under v2's gates** (a month v2 refuses for its
own data is refused for the reference too), not v1's ordinary operation. Neither attributes
the result to one component: the bundle is tested, and a pass says nothing about DTC or insider buying separately.

**Tier.** Demo research seat, **shadow and controls only: no executed book, no capital, no broker I/O.** Purpose
`demo_trial`. A pass is an argument on #2437 for an executed v2 declaration, never an authorisation (§8).

**Why shadow-only (scope decision, recorded).** v1's executed-book modules (`ranking_pot_exec`, `_executor`,
`_intent`, `_activation`, `_loss`, `_exits`, `_held_levels`) hard-code `ranking-pot-v1` and are policy-hashed; once
v1 is frozen, editing them is `ranking_drift` for v1 (the #3574 precedent). Executing v2 means either copying those
modules under a v2 identity or adding version-preserving adapters beside them; both are real build and coupling
cost, spent before any evidence that v2's order is worth executing. The forward record needs no capital. v2's hash
covers only the v1 modules it imports (§8), none of which is an executed-book module, so a later executed
declaration (a new `strategy_version`, a new family charge; sql/445 never reuses a version) can parameterise those
modules without drifting this seat. What shadow-only loses — fill, minimum-ticket and capacity evidence — is
not replaced: the snapshot's per-entrant minimum ticket and spread are proxies only, and real fills are the first
thing an executed declaration must measure.

## 5. The v2 order (pure; one function for the shadow, the variant and every control)

Inputs at one rebalance, all in the snapshot's `v2` block, so the order is re-derivable from it:

- **Insider evidence:** every purchase row in the look-back window for an S₀ name (`insider_transactions.id`,
  accession, filer CIK, issuer CIK, `txn_date`, `txn_code`, `acquired_disposed_code`, `filed_at`), and for each
  (filer CIK, issuer CIK, purchase year) pair the history rows behind its (year, month) cells (id, accession, filed
  date). The classification (classified, routine, opportunistic) is re-derived from them by a frozen function. Rows:
  `insider_transactions` ⋈ `insider_filings` ⋈ `sec_filing_manifest` on `accession_number`, as read in the
  rebalance's REPEATABLE READ snapshot. Amendment (4/A) and superseded-row treatment follows the
  `.claude/skills/data-sources/sec-edgar.md` Form 3/4/5 rules, cited in slice 2. Counted per rebalance, never gated:
  rows excluded for a NULL owner or issuer CIK, and Form 4 manifest rows in the window not yet parsed or tombstoned.
- **DTC:** S\* = the latest settlement date ≤ `as_of`'s date with a row for an S₀ name observed by `as_of`; per S₀
  name, its **latest observed revision** at S\* (by `known_from`, then `source_document_id`), the row's
  `known_from`, source id and `average_daily_volume` stored. That revision's `days_to_cover` if finite and ≥ 0, its
  ADV positive (zero or NULL = FINRA's N/A, §2) and the value non-zero (0 = the field's default, §2), else missing
  (counted by reason, in that precedence); an older revision is never resurrected. ⚠ (v5) FINRA's ingest re-stamps
  `known_from` on every refresh of a settlement it holds (all 5,692 rows at 2026-09-15 carried the 2026-10-02 12:00Z
  refresh), so `known_from` is last-refreshed, not first-observed; S\* and "observed by `as_of`" therefore use the
  reading transaction's own time as `as_of` (the snapshot's, and the pre-score check's own), never an earlier fire
  time, or a refresh landing mid-fire would hide every row at S\*.

Order: compute u_score from R_t's own scores, u_dtc from −dtc, u_ins from ins, composite as §2 in exact `Fraction`
arithmetic; the order is sorted on the exact composite (`ranking_pot.rank_order`'s Decimal key is not used for v2, so
no Decimal context can split or merge a tie). The composite is
always in (0, 1], so v1's missing-score tail never applies; ties break by `instrument_id` (v1 r3-5). F-rank is the
induced order on F_t; v1's `ranking_pot.decide` applies unchanged (same N, band, `recently_exited`, unfilled slots).

Per rebalance the attempt records: R_t's buyer count, DTC coverage and each missing reason, and whether either new
component was constant in R_t.

**Explainability.** Each shadow `enter` row stores its three percentiles, the composite, its DTC and settlement date,
and the qualifying purchase accessions with the pair's classification cells, beside v1's score reconciliation. The
page shows them (§9 slice 4).

## 6. Controls: the conditional permutation null

**Assumption (stated, not designed in).** Under H₀, given every name's own-fact path (fixing R_t and F_t), its own
v1.5 score path, its freeze-time score stratum and its subsequent price path, which name's (dtc, ins) path is
attached to which name is exchangeable **within each stratum**. A conditional randomisation test (Candès, Fan,
Janson & Lv 2018) would draw the components from their distribution given the score; we do not model that
distribution and approximate it by permuting within freeze-time score deciles, which keeps the coarse score–factor
dependence (measured +0.162 for DTC, §3) and breaks the rest. DTC is built from the name's own volume, so it carries
liquidity, size and volatility exposure that even the stratified permutation breaks. Donors can lie outside R_t or
F_t at a rebalance, so a control's DTC distribution inside R_t is not exactly the real one. A rejection therefore
reads only: "within this policy, the real attachment beat stratified random attachments, holding each name's own
score". The readout's exposure section (v1 spec §9.4, reused) shows what the attachment loaded on; the
v1-reference condition (§7) is what guards against a pass that is only a re-weighting of the score.

**Construction.** Control k: π_k = the union over strata (ascending stratum index) of
`draw_bijection(sorted stratum, seed_s, k)` with seed_s = sha256(`control_seed(v2 declaration sha, v2 first decided
snapshot sha)` ‖ the stratum index as 4-byte big-endian) — v1's draw, per stratum. In control k, R member i takes donor π_k(i)'s dtc (missing
if the donor has none) and ins (0 or 1; never missing) and keeps its own v1.5 score; the percentiles are recomputed within R_t from
the donated values, then §5. The same π_k is used at every rebalance (persistence follows the donor's paths, as v1).
K = 9,999. The rebalance records, per control, how many R members received a missing donor DTC (the readout reports
the median and maximum share, as v1's missing-donor figure).

**p-values:** v1 spec §9.2 unchanged. The `1/(K + 1)` resolution check is not a power guarantee: ties and repeated
induced orders can make the attainable p-values coarser. The readout reports, per rebalance, the number of distinct
control entrant sets, per-stratum within-stratum rank correlation of score and DTC, and the controls' buyer prevalence
in R_t beside the real one (the insider indicator's unknowns travel with donors).

Residual, stated: the permutation moves names one at a time, so cross-name structure (share classes, industries,
insider clusters) is not preserved.
