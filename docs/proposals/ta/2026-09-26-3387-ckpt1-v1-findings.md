1. **L22–31:** The `band × 252/h` multiplication and rounding are correct. These are arithmetic annualised charges, not compounded wealth losses; “365%/yr” needs that qualification.

2. **L22–24:** Spread-as-fill-price accounting makes the table approximate. With unchanged price, round-trip loss is `s/(1+s/2)`, not exactly `s`.

3. **L22,124:** `252/h` assumes continuously populated, entered cohorts. Idle slots, missing entry bars and terminal exits invalidate the claimed universal minimum cost.

4. **L124–129:** Forced cohort liquidation is a harness construction. It does not establish minimum executable turnover where overlapping positions could be retained or netted.

5. **L35:** Matching the cost model does not match realised costs: selection can change price-band composition, entry availability and terminations. Arm-minus-control need not be nearly cost-neutral.

6. **L21–36:** No actual tracker comparison is specified. Freeze the tracker, currency, total-return/tax basis, expenses and executable comparison; published alpha cannot enforce this gate.

7. **L23:** Zero commission alone does not establish a priced lane. The supplied harness also requires USD account/orders, real settlement and current fee-page verification.

8. **L24,124:** Frozen contemporary p75 bands are neither historical spread measurements nor bounds. The document omits the required counterfactual qualification when drawing economic conclusions.

9. **L11:** “Four … gross edge smaller than spread” contradicts its own table: 3a exceeds the cheapest spread but misses the worthwhile margin.

10. **L42:** GKM’s reference-return figures combine high- and low-volume components; they are not directly the high-volume leg. The reported high-volume excesses are **0.45% small, 0.29% large**, versus 0.47%/0.25% combined. [GKM §4.1](https://rodneywhitecenter.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/9901.pdf)

11. **L42:** “Fail at every band” is false even after that correction: `0.45 × 252/20 − 0.322 × 252/20 = 1.613%/yr`, above 1.5%. Whether small firms occupy that band remains unestablished.

12. **L42:** Market capitalisation does not determine nominal share-price band. Neither matching small-stock premia to expensive bands nor applying their average premium to a cheap-band subset is justified.

13. **L43:** A 100-session return cannot be annualised at 100 sessions and compared with 63-session costs. The 63-session return is unknown.

14. **L43:** Halving the 100-day long–short premium assumes long/short symmetry at that horizon; 20-day similarity does not establish it.

15. **L42:** The market-order quotation concerns the paper’s particular implementation; its limit-order results differ. It cannot independently prove this harness construction fails. [GKM §7](https://rodneywhitecenter.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/9901.pdf)

16. **L38–46,126:** Published averages are not upper bounds on families. Medium firms, alternative volume constructions, conditional overnight concentration and cross-sectional scheduled-flow effects remain unexcluded; programme family 8 explicitly prohibits discarding interaction-only features through univariate filtering.

17. **L41:** Overnight return is not overnight-minus-buy-and-hold return. The comparison also requires intraday returns and treatment of uninvested cash.

18. **L41,46:** Unsupported comparisons and absent economic edges are different conclusions. Matched books can select different names over identical overnight/event windows; calendar-only timing needs another comparator. Harness limitations do not exhaust these families.

19. **L44–49:** H&S’s 41/115 bps figures are long-portfolio alphas; they should **not** be halved. However, the numerical discussion is in the introduction, not the abstract as cited. [H&S](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X13000585)

20. **L44–49:** Four-factor alpha is neither raw excess over a cap-weighted tracker nor excess over this payer-domain control. Acknowledging the mismatch does not justify using the figures as interchangeable hurdles.

21. **L45,93:** “Passes for names ≥$5” transports an aggregate estimate into a newly filtered population without evidence. It also excludes tax drag despite proposing taxation as a base gate.

22. **L45,79–81:** H&S restrict their sample to US common shares and exclude ADRs. Applying their premium to an ADR-inclusive semiannual universe changes the population materially. [H&S data section](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X13000585)

23. **L69–75:** The worst-base-cell BY requirement is real. “Can never pass, whatever its true edge” is false: sufficiently positive price-only active returns can pass. A likely economic handicap is not a structural refusal or guaranteed null.

24. **L71–73:** The omitted-dividend penalty is the **difference in dividend contributions between books**, not the whole arm dividend. Even the stated simplified assumptions imply roughly `5/6` of it; yields and capture probabilities are unmeasured.

25. **L71:** Predicted ex-dates are not realised ex-dates. Forecast error, skipped dividends and entry timing invalidate the assumed capture probabilities.

26. **L74,104:** Dividend price pressure is not equivalent to receiving the dividend. H&S identify pre-ex-day gains and subsequent reversals; positive price-only performance is compatible with the proposed mechanism. [H&S](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X13000585)

27. **L77–85:** Replacing zero credit with 65% credit is both a plausible cash-return modelling correction **and removal of an existing robustness requirement**. Being pre-look permits reconsideration; it does not make the change gate-neutral.

28. **L83–84,106:** Completeness checks do not replace protection against wrong amounts, duplicate records, misdating, split/currency errors or already-adjusted prices. The same dividend records construct the signal and payoff; both new cells can share the same error.

29. **L79–82:** UK residence alone does not establish treaty treatment actually applied by the broker. Eligibility/documentation and realised withholding require verification; “treaty truth” overstates the evidence. [IRS Publication 515](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p515)

30. **L79–82:** A universal 35% upper bracket is unproved. eToro identifies 37% withholding for effectively connected income; either exclude such instruments explicitly or model them. [eToro](https://www.etoro.com/en-us/investing/dividend-calendar/)

31. **L78–82:** Uniformly increasing withholding is not necessarily conservative for **active** returns: it can penalise the control more. Different issuer-country rates can also change rankings.

32. **L81:** ADR distributions can carry depositary fees outside withholding. For example, Diageo charges a per-ADR dividend fee; establish whether archive amounts already include these deductions. [Diageo](https://www.diageo.com/en/investors/shareholder-centre/adr-shares/adr-dividends)
