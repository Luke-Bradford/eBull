2. **Round-3 disposition:** **#5, #6 and #7 remain; #10 has a residual ambiguity.** **#8, #9, #11 and #12 are handled. #13 is specified at the design level**, with a mandatory implementation change identified below.

4. **Still #6 — pre-look closure is not durable.** “The refusal left no declaration” prevents immediate evaluation, but does not prevent another freeze before `HUNT_CLOSED` lands. Moreover, a substantive refusal **at the look** can occur after a declaration already exists. Persist a terminal lineage refusal under the programme lock; declaration absence is not that record. [Spec](docs/proposals/ta/2026-09-28-3454-hunt-3-spec.md:125)

5. **Still #7 — removing `trial_register.py` removes decision code too.** Its effects are not confined to M: entry validation, evidence parsing and `trial_for_declaration` affect acceptance. Those rules can change while M remains identical. #8’s circularity is removed, but the decision-procedure pin remains incomplete; separate or selectively hash register code and data. [Register](app/services/trial_register.py:527)

6. **Forward D: dimensionally correct, but the binding implication remains unproved.** On the *same* sessions and cell, band excess minus D equals quote excess. Here D is measured forward while the margins come from historical samples. Matching units and evaluator does not establish transport across turnover, utilisation, returns or spread–return dependence. This is a replacement operational criterion unless that bridge is supplied; it is not yet demonstrated equivalent to R3’s frozen round-trip-cost threshold.

7. **D’s evaluator contract is incomplete.** The current evaluator accepts one symmetric `half_spread` per position, used at both entry and exit; it has no separate ask-entry/bid-exit inputs. Specify the asymmetric adapter and its frozen identity without changing the historical model ID. Merely naming `hunt_evaluator` does not define this computation. [Evaluator](app/services/hunt_evaluator.py:171)

8. **D lacks a cell-selection rule.** The margins minimise across six cells; D is singular, with no dividend/terminal-policy choice or aggregation rule. Define cellwise drags or a bound covering every gated cell, including unavailable/refused-cell disposition. A canonical-cell D need not protect the worst historical cell.

9. **Endpoint mids do not fully define daily arithmetic drag.** The evaluator also needs intermediate marks. Different mark paths can change `252 × mean(return difference)` with identical entry/exit quotes. Specify common intermediate marks; clarify that cost-interaction *formulas*, not their numerical contributions, are identical. Also, choosing bands at quote mids can cross a band boundary relative to the printed open; F2 must cover that effect. [Spec](docs/proposals/ta/2026-09-28-3454-hunt-3-spec.md:283)

10. **Terminal re-judgement mostly passes.** Reusing the original endpoint evidence once, tightening to `min(M_disc,M_val)`, and closing after FAIL/undetermined handles the prior terminal gap. But “if validation closes hunt 3” literally includes successful validation, because every outcome closes the lineage. Say “fails validation promotion.” Also distinguish the endpoint’s provisional verdict from the final verdict explicitly. [Spec](docs/proposals/ta/2026-09-28-3454-hunt-3-spec.md:303)

11. **`PASS_CONTINGENT` matches the code, with one ambiguity.** It means all base cells satisfy `t > 3`, `DSR ≥ DSR_BAR`, and positive mean; every reason is a stress failure, including **refused stress cells**. V4’s later blanket “a refused or missing cell key” can reintroduce the stress gate. Restrict numerical/refusal disqualification to gated inputs and distinguish missing schema keys from present, refused stress cells. [Verdict code](app/services/hunt_inference.py:686)

12. **Identifier and accounting pass.** The non-`hunt-` identifier is valid; one re-registration adds **BY m +1**, and the additional validation reservation gives **global M +2**. Evaluation replaces that reservation. However, the current tripwire unconditionally rejects a missing discovery entry—even with zero rows—so amendment is required, not hypothetical. Permit absence only for verified inherited hunts with zero actual discovery rows; retain the validation claim checks. [Tripwire](app/services/hunt_door.py:478)
