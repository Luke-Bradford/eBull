# #3454 ckpt-1 v2 findings (Codex, 2026-09-28)

Verbatim from `codex exec` on spec v2. Disposition in the spec's Status section; forward-trial items are the forward declaration spec's checklist.

Line references are to [v2](docs/proposals/ta/2026-09-28-3454-hunt-3-spec.md). “R1” below identifies round-1 findings.

1. **Framing verdict — conditionally honest, not yet established as confirmatory (R1 #1, #2, #6).** An explicitly amended exploratory continuation, followed by a frozen SPY test and terminal closure, is defensible. Low power alone does **not** require closure. But v2 still acknowledges that R2 did not amend condition 4, and disclosure of overlapping validation exposure is not an independence audit. Document the amendment and audit what #2481 revealed and influenced. Without amendment, the old condition closes this hunt; without defensible independence, close the historical **confirmatory claim** or use untouched data.

2. **NEW — “stricter than the gate it replaces” is false (L31–35, L64–65).** Pre-look power and realised significance impose different constraints. This candidate fails the former but can pass the latter. The new validation significance condition is additional stringency; the overall admission procedure is relaxed. The discovery-based 0.164 is also not a fixed validation cutoff: validation estimates its own SE.

3. **Unresolved R1 #9 (L334–337).** Acknowledging upward biases does not neutralise them. Capital qualification still uses the affected return series and headroom without correction or a bound.

4. **Unresolved R1 #15 (L89–95, L355).** Closure is a later code change, leaving a crash/concurrency interval after exposure. Consume the lineage’s look durably before releasing results; cover interrupted looks and substantive pre-look closures. Family/hash checks also need a rule for equivalent implementations whose family and source hash both change.

5. **Unresolved R1 #16, #20 (L125–130).** Hashing selected functions does not pin their un-hashed helpers, imported constants or configuration. Specify the complete decision dependency set.

6. **Unresolved R1 #19, #30 (L146–186, L217–223).** Freeze-time finiteness checks are explicit; validation excess/t checks are not equally explicit. Define refusal for invalid cells, missing tracker coverage, degenerate HAC estimates and non-finite t. Apply the non-positive/non-finite headroom rule explicitly to H_val too.

7. **Unresolved R1 #23, #31, #32 (L195–223).** The purported exact replacement still compares incompatible cost quantities and incorrectly drops tariff interactions; details below.

8. **Unresolved R1 #33, #34 (L204–207, L239–247).** Disclosing midpoint substitution and capture delay does not bound their effect. Band crossings and print-to-capture movement remain outside the capital cost test, although R3 calls for realised next-open cost.

9. **Unresolved R1 #36, #37, #45.** Provider timestamps alone do not establish fresh, synchronised, executable quotes. Quote depth, indicative/stale prices, symbol mapping, delayed delivery, unscheduled closures and security halts still lack deterministic treatment.

10. **Unresolved R1 #38, #39, #41 (L225–232).** Append-only cohorts fix retrospective membership changes, but not reproducibility: pin universe membership, input bars, signal/band versions, broker mappings and corporate actions. Specify completed-bar availability, formation cutoff and late-data handling.

11. **Unresolved R1 #42, #43 (L249–256).** The stress charge is not a worst-case missing-data bound. V2 expressly says the census does not bound opening spreads and accepts observations above the stress charge. Both proposed analyses can therefore pass while actual missing costs fail.

12. **Unresolved R1 #48, #50 (L258–266).** An estimated AR(1) multiplier followed by tₙ₋₁ is not justified by Cameron–Miller’s independent-cluster framework. The citation does not validate this dependent-session procedure. [Cameron–Miller](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1jq5d0pq)

13. **Unresolved R1 #49.** Define empty cohorts, sessions with no complete positions, too few usable sessions, zero variance and undefined autocorrelation. Complete-case analysis cannot silently drop outage sessions while retaining the original n and df.

14. **Unresolved R1 #51, #54, #55 (L268–295).** Sizing still targets H, not the potentially smaller capital threshold; capital power is consequently unspecified. “Achieved power” and “achieved MDE” lack executable definitions and assumptions. A plug-in calculation is estimated power, not known achieved power.

15. **Unresolved R1 #58 — FLAG (L305–308).** Fixing the endpoint removes optional stopping, but **10 sessions × 3 positions** still has no power/precision justification. Published sample-size formulations exist; cite and adapt one to session dependence and the remaining margin. [NIST](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm)

16. **Unresolved R1 #59, #61, #62 (L304–314).** Uniform sampling helps, but selection implementation, round-trip aggregation, decision/arrival/fill timestamps, delayed exits and repeated-name broker netting remain unspecified. A failure charge does not define how outstanding demo positions are closed.

17. **Unresolved R1 #60 (L316–319).** Two marginal 95% upper bounds do not generally produce a 95% bound on their sum. Without additional distributional justification, simultaneous coverage is guaranteed only at 90%. Specify a joint method or allocate error, for example 97.5% component bounds.

18. **Unresolved R1 #65 (L297–328).** Terminal failure is now specified, but expiry is not. “Valid for the window’s regime” supplies no operational expiry or regime-change rule; “larger-size calibration” also needs an explicit relationship to the no-retrial invariant.

19. **NEW — tariff does not cancel (L195–207).** `_half_spreads` returns **band + commission**. With per-side commission c, the comparable factors are `(1−b−c)/(1+b+c)` and `(1−s_x−c)/(1+s_e+c)`. Equal c remains inside unequal denominators; omitting it is not exact cancellation. [Code](app/services/hunt_compute.py:398)

20. **NEW — e_i is a factor difference, not realised return drag (L204).** Without dividends, return drag is midpoint gross return **R_i × e_i**. Dividend timing and terminal paths further invalidate the blanket statement that every position retains one fixed fraction of “its gross.” [Evaluator](app/services/hunt_evaluator.py:207)

21. **NEW — H and e_i have different meanings (L209–218).** H is δ added as δ/2 to each half-spread. Writing `a=b+c`, its factor decrement is  
   `δ / [(1+a)(1+a+δ/2)]`, **not δ**. Thus `U_e ≤ H` can accept costs exceeding the supposedly exact headroom.

22. **NEW — matching average weights is insufficient (L258–261).** Even after fixing units, a mean heterogeneous forward cost is not equivalent to a uniform historical spread increment. Band mix, entry/exit asymmetry, returns and cost covariance affect portfolio drag. The exact historical sensitivity calculation supplies no bound for that substitution.

23. **NEW — H does not preserve the new significance gate (L209–223).** Its root protects only the 8% point estimate. Consuming headroom can reduce excess t below 3 while retaining excess ≥8%. State that capital significance remains a base-cost claim, or require significance under the qualified cost adjustment.

24. **NEW — H can use different data from its inherited outcome (L211–215).** Candidate and model hashes do not freeze mutable historical prices or corporate actions. Pin the original input snapshot and require δ=0 to reproduce the inherited result before computing H; similarly bind H_val to the validation snapshot.

25. **NEW — recording-row invariant violation (L214–215).** Existing `hunt_trials` recording rows count in M **and** the discovery budget. The proposed row contradicts “no BY count,” budget zero and the asserted M+1 transition. A sensitivity artifact needs a separate storage contract or an explicit counting-rule amendment. [Recorder](app/services/hunt_harness.py:1429)

26. **NEW — bisection boundary contract is incomplete (L211–218).** Define refusal when δ=0 already fails or an intermediate computation refuses. Return the last **feasible lower endpoint**, not an arbitrary value within 1e−6. If 0.05 passes, report capped headroom, not the “largest” charge.

27. **NEW — missing-leg substitution can erase known expensive data (L249–253).** If one observed leg exceeds stress and the other is missing, replacing both legs with stress understates even the known contribution. Retain observed legs and bound only missing components.

28. **NEW — “mid” is undefined (L199–205).** Specify whether it is `(bid+ask)/2` or a provider field, its timestamp and validity checks. The stated bid/ask checks do not validate an independently supplied mid.

29. **NEW — lag-1 correction misses the construction’s own dependence (L263–264).** Entry and exit captures from different cohorts can share a session at lag 4. Positive lag-4 dependence can coexist with zero lag-1 correlation, producing no inflation under this rule. Also define the autocorrelation estimator and handling of ρ̂₁ approaching 1.

30. **NEW — sample-size formula ignores its own SE inflation (L271).** Pilot SD estimates marginal session variability, whereas the final SE adds serial dependence. The stated 80% target is therefore not derived for the specified final test, nor for the requirement that both missingness analyses pass.

31. **NEW — pilot claim exceeds the citation (L273–274).** Wittes–Brittain’s normal-means setting does not establish negligible size distortion for five correlated, potentially skewed session observations, missing-data substitution and estimated AR correction. Later work explicitly shows parameter-dependent distortion. Calibrate this procedure or use a supported design. [Wittes et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/%28SICI%291097-0258%2819991230%2918%3A24%3C3481%3A%3AAID-SIM301%3E3.0.CO%3B2-C)

32. **NEW — pilot execution is ambiguous (L268–286).** Specify whether σ̂ comes from complete-case or imputed sessions, what happens with missing/constant pilot data, and when n becomes immutable. Five complete entry-plus-exit observations exist only after the fifth cohort exits; intervening cohorts must continue entering without selection.

33. **NEW — adaptive endpoint needs a deterministic solver.** “Iterated for the df” supplies no initial value, convergence or cycle rule for an integer fixed-point equation. Specify the smallest admissible n satisfying a stated inequality, then apply the cap.

34. **NEW — FLAG: pilot/floor calibration is absent.** Five pilot sessions and the 20-session minimum are bespoke design choices, not consequences of the cited formula. Supply a precision/error calibration; identify 60 explicitly as a resource cap rather than evidence of adequacy.

35. **NEW — PASS and FAIL overlap (L288–291).** At `SE=0` and `ē=threshold`, both definitions hold. Make dispositions disjoint and specify zero-variance inference.

36. **NEW — outage handling assumes a cohort exists (L229, L284).** A signal/data outage can prevent formation itself, leaving no denominator to mark “every position missing.” Specify a frozen fallback or terminal disposition. Also reconcile the first entry session with formation before its window.

37. **NEW — F2’s slippage is not additive in e_i’s units (L311–319).** Let q be the quote factor and l_e,l_x the stated signed slippages. Additional factor loss is  
   `q × [1 − (1−l_x)/(1+l_e)]`, not simply `l_e+l_x`. Return normalisation and tariff interactions remain necessary.

38. **NEW — U_quote is ambiguous (L317).** The forward trial produces two bounds. F2 must identify which enters the sum; choosing the complete-case bound can discard the missingness safeguard.

39. **NEW — F2 drops the dependence adjustment (L318).** Ten consecutive sessions still contain overlapping holdings and recurring names. A plain t bound with 9 df does not resolve that dependence.

40. **NEW — F2 combines different populations (L305–319).** Quote costs come from the earlier window; slippage comes from later sessions. No stationarity assumption, concurrent quote-cost check or bound connects their sum to total cost in either window.

41. **NEW — final-quote benchmark is not executable prospectively (L241, L309–312).** The last quote before close is identifiable only afterward. Specify F2’s causal order trigger and reconcile its same-moment benchmark with the forward trial’s retrospectively selected exit quote.

42. **NEW — F2 small-cohort and draw edge cases are missing (L305–307).** Define sampling without replacement, cohorts with fewer than three names, canonical cohort ordering, PRNG/seed interpretation and no-redraw rules. A seed alone does not determine the draw.

43. **NEW — F2 failure charges are not loss bounds (L313–314).** Stress-spread substitution cannot bound a halted exit, delayed liquidation or partial fill. It also conflates spread with incremental slippage. Such events need a terminal qualification rule and an actual position-resolution protocol.

44. **NEW — F2 timing and verdicts remain incomplete (L305, L321, L356).** Its declaration must freeze before the immediately following entry sessions; define what happens if it misses that deadline. Define FAIL versus undetermined, final exit/arrival deadlines and dispositions when validation or H_val is unavailable.

45. **NEW — size extrapolation is unsupported (L298, L327–332).** Calibration at one demo notional does not establish all smaller notionals when minimum fees or lot constraints matter. Repeated names across slots can also produce broker exposure or netted orders larger than the per-position cap.
