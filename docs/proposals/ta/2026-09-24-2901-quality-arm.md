# #2901 — quality arm (gross profitability, GP/A): construction and census

Step 4 of `docs/proposals/ta/2026-09-24-selection-programme-v2.md` (first arm). Refs #2899, #3360 (fundamentals
bundle), #3361 (linkage), #3362 (termination), #2908 (the control lesson), #2829/#2599 (declaration register).
Codex ckpt-1 rounds 1 (84 findings), 2 (94) and 3 (71) ran on 2026-09-24; see "Design history".

## Contract (deliberately narrow)
This spec fixes **which issuers are eligible, how GP/A is built, and which names form the arm**, as a
content-addressed artefact plus a descriptive census, both produced **before any return is read**. It does not
define the simulator, statistics, verdict or power: those are the **declaration spec** (PR B), which must answer
every item under "Carried to the declaration spec". No return has been computed for this spec.

At each June formation D, from the #3360/#3361 bundles and the pinned Intrader mirror only:
- **eligible set E(D)**: linked, non-financial, annual-report, domestic-form issuers with a public annual GP/A,
  positive assets and non-negative equity, executable on the next session;
- **arm A(D)**: the top GP/A decile of E(D); **control C(D) = E(D)** (the #2908 lesson: the identical construction
  without the signal). Weighting, costs and termination belong to the declaration and apply to both identically.

**Complete-case selection is not neutral, and this spec does not claim it is.** Admission depends on what an issuer
tagged, which can correlate with both profitability and distress, so conditioning on it can create a
profitability–return association inside E(D) that the full market lacks (a collider). Sharing E(D) between arm and
control does not remove that. The programme requires showing that complete-case selection does not manufacture the
result; the census below describes who is excluded, and the **diagnostic with an acceptance rule is carried to the
declaration**. Separately, most admission inputs are public at D, so the rule is one a live sleeve could apply —
subject to R3/R4/R6/R7, which a live run would not reproduce.

## Source rules
- **Signal: HXZ global-q technical document §2.4.9 "Gpa"** (artefact sha256 `15635d76…6d8e4015`, pinned by
  #2908): "total revenue (REVT) minus cost of goods sold (COGS) divided by total assets (AT, the denominator is
  current, not lagged, total assets). At the end of June of each year t, we sort stocks into deciles based on Gpa
  for the fiscal year ending in calendar year t − 1." The section states no winsorisation or size floor. Novy-Marx
  (2013) is the origin; HXZ is the definition on disk.
- **Sample: same document, "Stock Sample"**: CRSP share code 10/11; "We exclude financial firms (SIC between 6000
  and 6999) and firms with negative book equity." Book equity (same document, "Bm"): "stockholders' book equity,
  plus balance sheet deferred taxes and investment tax credit (TXDITC) if available, minus the book value of
  preferred stock."
- **XBRL mapping.** No Compustat↔us-gaap crosswalk was found for REVT / COGS / AT / SEQ, and XBRL has no
  "fiscal-year" flag in companyfacts. The FASB taxonomy element definitions were **not** consulted for this spec; PR A
  records each alias's taxonomy standard label and definition in the builder's `POLICY` so the scope differences are
  auditable. The alias map, period rule and sign screens are fixed **by construction**, frozen in `POLICY`, and not
  claimed equivalent to Compustat (R2). Concepts are exactly #3360's hashed set
  (`docs/proposals/ta/2026-09-24-3360-companyfacts-pit-bundle.md` rule 1), read through its reader (rules 7–9).
  companyfacts carries only non-dimensional standard-taxonomy facts (sec-edgar skill §7.17): "absent" means no such
  extracted fact, not "not reported".
- **SIC** = the submissions JSON `sic` (sec-edgar skill §1, "Submissions JSON top-level"): a **current** value (R3).
- **No dated exchange listing exists in our inputs for 2013–2018**: the #3361 linkage carries none and
  `submissions.exchanges` is current (skill §1), so NYSE breakpoints are not computable point-in-time (D2).
- **Prices come only from the pinned mirror** (`read_price_series`; adjusted open = raw open × adjusted close / raw
  close). Liquidity uses the mirror's raw close × raw volume columns, not `research_price_daily`, so the mirror commit
  pins it. (One check that the DB stores the same raw basis: Intrader `AAPL` 2020-08-28 = 499.23 / 46,907,479 in
  both; adjusted close 121.97. A single observation, recorded as such.)

## Construction (frozen by construction; every constant in `POLICY`)
1. **Formations**: the last NYSE session of each June, **2013–2024** (12); the programme's price-corpus end
   2024-09-27 bounds the last holding. **Deviation D5 from the programme's 2011-06 start**: the #3360 census found
   1,739 of 11,590 members with any public event at 2011-06-30, and the #3362 census found 0 of 12,636 terminating
   Intrader series with a last bar before 2013, so terminations are unobservable in the corpus for all of a 2011
   holding year and the first half of a 2012 one. That is a corpus observation; a 2013 start does not certify the
   corpus complete after it (R6). X(D) = the first NYSE session after D (`market_calendar`).
2. **Population P(D)** — one row per CIK after dedup; every other candidate series is recorded as `not_chosen`:
   - candidates: in-scope Intrader series with `link_as_of(S, D)` = `linked`, grammar `plain` or `class:*`
     (`q_alias` allowed: dropping bankrupt `…Q` names favours survivors). No bar is required ON D. `link_as_of`
     already requires D within the series' eventual bar range and applies the inventory-wide collision guard (R6);
   - **one series per CIK, chosen before executability**: highest liquidity at D, then lowest `series_id`. Liquidity
     = `census_3361_security_linkage.liquidity` over the mirror's rows, validated first (dates strictly increasing,
     finite values): median raw close × raw volume of the latest 21 rows with both positive, strictly before D, the
     oldest ≥ D − 42; else unavailable, ranking below any value. The window can span an issuer succession (R1);
   - **executable** = the chosen series has a valid mirror row on X(D) (positive finite raw open, raw close, adjusted
     close) with volume > 0. Otherwise the CIK is `not_executable`; no substitution. This uses the X(D) close (the
     adjusted-open convention) and the vendor's retrospective adjustment, identically for arm and control (R7);
   - the chosen series' vendor symbol maps one-to-one to its mirror file and `research_price_series` row, else the
     build refuses.
3. **Ladder**, first failing rung recorded, every field status counted independently (with `not_evaluated`):
   `fundamentals_integrity_excluded` → `no_companyfacts_entry` → `sic_missing` (SIC absent, not 1–4 ASCII digits or
   an integer, or outside 100–9999) → `sic_financial` (6000–6999) → `no_public_annual_period` →
   `several_annual_starts` → `foreign_private_issuer` → `gross_profit_unavailable` → `component_sign_invalid` →
   `assets_unavailable` / `assets_nonpositive` → `equity_unavailable` / `equity_negative` → `not_executable` →
   **eligible**. A reader status of `after_capture` or `concept_not_in_policy` aborts the build (configuration, not
   economics). Integrity exclusion is snapshot-wide (#3360 rule 5; R4).
4. **Annual period** (HXZ "fiscal year ending in calendar year t − 1", t = D.year): candidates are public events
   **and public blocking rejections** of the revenue and COGS aliases, unit `USD`, with a `start`, from an accession
   of form family (`pit_fundamentals.form_family`) `10-K`, `20-F` or `40-F`, with `(end − start) + 1 ∈ [350, 380]`
   days and `end.year = t − 1`. End = the latest candidate `end`, even when only a blocking rejection carries it (the
   #3360 rule: never fall back past a later blocking rejection). More than one distinct `start` among that end's
   candidates → `several_annual_starts` (0–3 per production formation; a corrected start that leaves the superseded
   one public is an accepted cost). A fiscal-year change is taken literally: the latest ≥ 350-day period ending in
   t − 1 wins and a short transition period is never a candidate (D7). No fallback to an older year.
5. **Components** by `value_as_of` at `(start, end)` (instant `(None, end)` for balances). Among an item's aliases
   whose read is not `absent` (the reader returns an acceptance for `value`, `ambiguous` and `blocked_by_rejection`
   alike, `pit_fundamentals.value_as_of`), the **latest public acceptance** decides; at equal acceptance the
   **declared order** decides (broadest first: REVT is total revenue); the winner's status is the component's
   (`ambiguous`/`blocked_by_rejection` → unavailable, no fallback). Disagreement at the latest acceptance is counted.
   - revenue: `Revenues`, `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`,
     `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerIncludingAssessedTax`, `SalesRevenueNet`;
   - COGS: `CostOfRevenue`, `CostOfGoodsAndServicesSold`, `CostOfGoodsSold`;
   - assets: `Assets`; equity: `StockholdersEquity` only (parent equity; SEQ without HXZ's TXDITC and preferred, D3).

   Signs: revenue ≥ 0 and COGS ≥ 0 else `component_sign_invalid` (D7); assets > 0; equity < 0 excluded, **0 kept**
   (the quoted rule says "negative"). No winsorisation or floor (HXZ states none); an extreme GP/A takes one arm slot
   and can move the boundary, so the census reports the GP/A and assets distribution of A(D).
6. **Foreign private issuer** (D4): the latest public **original** (non-`/A`) accession of form family `10-K`,
   `20-F` or `40-F` is a `20-F`/`40-F` (any such form at that acceptance counts), **or** any winning component
   accession is one.
7. **Signal and rank**: convert each operand's canonical decimal to `fractions.Fraction`, then GP/A = (revenue −
   COGS) / assets exactly; the artefact stores numerator and denominator. Order by (GP/A, CIK) ascending, zero-based
   rank r, decile ⌊10·r/n⌋: **A(D) = decile 9**, exactly ⌊n/10⌋ names, **plus every issuer whose GP/A equals the
   lowest decile-9 value** (ties are never split by CIK; counted). Any formation with n < 100 refuses the build.
8. **Artefact** (content-addressed; exclusive publish, verified loader and policy binding as `r6_pit_bundle` /
   #3360): every candidate series with its disposition (`chosen` / `not_chosen`), and every P(D) row with CIK, link
   basis and observations' accessions, liquidity, SIC, ladder rung, every field status (explicit nulls), period,
   components with winning accessions and alias, alias-disagreement flags, GP/A numerator/denominator, decile, and
   the census inputs below; per-formation counts. `POLICY` covers every construction and census constant (alias
   lists and labels, windows, thresholds, decile rule, outcome horizons). Digests: #3360 and #3361 manifests, #3360
   `submissions.zip`, the mirror commit, the market-calendar source, the termination evidence rows read, and the
   builder, census, reader, linkage, liquidity and termination modules. **Construction freezes when PR A merges**;
   the census runs after it, and any later construction change is a recorded amendment in the declaration.

## Census before any look (PR A; no returns)
Per formation from the artefact: ladder and independent field counts (including sign status per component),
|E(D)|, |A(D)|, boundary ties, `not_executable`, `not_chosen`, alias-disagreement counts; **joint**
cross-tabulations of rung × size × outcome, and per required field × size × outcome.
- **Size, two proxies, no market equity** (D8): liquidity decile (dollar volume, above) and assets decile (the
  resolved `Assets`, else its own bucket). Shares × price is not used: companyfacts drops per-class share counts for
  multi-class issuers (sec-edgar §7.17), ADR prices are per depositary share, and split timing mismatches shares and
  raw price. Deciles over P(D) by (value, CIK), `unavailable` separate, n < 10 → no deciles.
- **Outcome**, two channels never merged, each with its own horizon (D, D + 730]:
  - price: the chosen series ends in the horizon (`last_bar` ≤ D + 730 and not alive at capture) → its
    `TerminationClass`; still trading at D + 730 → `alive_730`; the horizon passes the capture date while alive →
    `censored_alive`;
  - Form 25: #3361 `form25` flags on the linked CIK filed in the horizon, **`symbol_match` only**, by raw provision
    label; else `no_form25_observed`, or `unobserved_horizon` when the horizon leaves the register span.
  `unknown_termination` and `unobserved_horizon` stay their own buckets (the pre-2019 unknown mass is shown, not
  interpreted). Events on D itself cannot occur in a channel keyed strictly after D; none is lost.
- Composition of A(D) vs E(D): SIC division, both size proxies, GP/A and assets tails.
- Linkage abstentions by year × reason × liquidity × outcome are the #3361 census (`census-2026-09-24-872d7256`, same
  formation grid and linkage bundle), cited not recomputed; it is series-level and does not reconcile to this
  issuer-level census.
Descriptive only; it gates nothing.

## Design history
Round 1 (84): GP = revenue − COGS only; annual period from annual-report accessions including rejection-only
candidates; equity screen `< 0`; exact ranks; the window change declared; the first simulator/statistics design.
Round 2 (94) put most of its weight on that simulator/statistics/verdict layer, so it moved to the declaration spec
with its open questions listed (as #3360 narrowed out field construction); construction changes: no bar on D, class
chosen before executability, parent equity only, revenue ≥ 0, n < 100 refusal, joint census. Round 3 (71): the
complete-case "cannot manufacture" claim withdrawn (collider) and its diagnostic carried; alias recency with the
declared order at equal acceptance — a first draft that dropped same-filing disagreements was measured at 106–243
eligible issuers per formation and rejected; FPI from the latest original annual accession or any winning accession; SIC validity;
ties kept together; operands converted to `Fraction` before arithmetic; liquidity from the pinned mirror with input
validation; zero-volume X(D) rows non-executable; market equity dropped as a size axis; an explicit two-channel
outcome algebra; freeze-before-census; the carried list completed (spread, termination classes, endpoint).
