## Condition 4 moves from a pre-look power gate to a post-look significance gate
Hunt 2 failed two conditions (#3449 "Outcome"): (3) the stress cells, and (4) excess-series MDE₈₀ 0.210 against
0.08. Hunt 3 inherits hunt 2's discovery data, so a pre-look condition 4 reproduces 0.210 and closes hunt 3 before
the look R2 orders. R2 does not say what happens to condition 4. v1 dropped it silently; that was an unsanctioned
widening, and v2 withdraws it.

v2 rule: condition 4 stops being a pre-look *prediction* that validation can detect 8%, and becomes a post-look
*requirement*. The validation excess series (arm − SPY, canonical cell) must itself be significant at the harness's
own threshold. That threshold is t > 3, Newey–West at the lag `hunt_inference.hunt_lag` assigns the validation
grid (the door's `power_statement` construction). The point estimate must also reach 0.08.

Reasons:
- It answers the finding that harness `PASS` tests arm against control. An arm can significantly beat a losing
  control and still fail to beat SPY. The capital claim is "beats SPY", so validation must test that.
- It is stricter than the gate it replaces: in hunt 2's terms it needs `excess_ann` ≳ 3 × 0.0547 ≈ 0.164, not
  0.08.
- The power problem is stated, not removed. Suppose discovery's variance carries over to a post-decimal market,
  giving SE(`excess_ann`) ≈ 0.164 / 3 ≈ 0.0547 on the 3,079-session grid (from hunt 2's MDE₅₀). Then the
  canonical cell passes t > 3 with probability ≈ 0.0013 at a true excess of 0, ≈ 0.062 at 0.08, and ≈ 0.54 at
  0.169. 0.169 is discovery's lower base cell, which selection inflates, so treat it as an upper reference.
  Reproduce: `1 − Φ(3 − μ/(0.164/3))`. These are canonical-cell, normal-approximation figures. They are not power
  for the worst base cell, DSR or the joint rule.

A miss is the likelier outcome. R2 orders the look anyway, and the lineage closure below makes it the last one.

**Programme consequence, stated and not acted on here.** #3453's families 1–4 "no trial" rests partly on
pre-look power-first. Whether this conversion should apply programme-wide is a queue decision for #2437.

## Forward cost trial (R3)
Its own declaration is frozen before the first capture. It measures cost only. It tests nothing about the return,
and the forward cohort's gross and net returns are descriptive.

**Cost form, exactly as the harness charges.** `hunt_evaluator` fills an entry at `entry·(1 + hs)` and marks an
exit at `mark·(1 − hs)`, with both half-spreads priced at entry (`hunt_compute._half_spreads`). So a position whose
band half-spread is b keeps the factor (1 − b)/(1 + b) of its gross.

Forward, from the eToro quotes:
- s_e = (ask − mid)/mid at the entry capture;
- s_x = (mid − bid)/mid at the exit capture;
- the realised factor is (1 − s_x)/(1 + s_e).

The position's **excess cost** is e_i = (1 − b_i)/(1 + b_i) − (1 − s_x)/(1 + s_e), a decimal return. b_i is the
band `cost_band_for` gives at the entry mid, with `price_basis="as_traded"`. The entry mid stands in for the printed
open the harness priced at, and that is a disclosed approximation. No order is placed, so the tariff is the frozen
schedule in both factors and is not part of e_i.

**Headroom H, computed exactly, not linearised.** H is the largest uniform extra round-trip charge (a decimal
return) such that every base cell's `excess_ann` stays ≥ 0.08:
- It is found by re-running the frozen compute on the stored candidate. Each position gets an extra half-spread
  δ/2 per side, in the harness's own cost form. δ is bisected to 1e-6, per base cell, and H is the minimum over
  cells.
- This re-reads discovery data already read for this candidate. It is recorded as a `hunt_trials` recording row
  with a note, not an evaluate row. It creates no candidate and no BY count.
- Linear cross-check only: (0.169 − 0.08) × 5/252 ≈ **0.00177** (0.18 percentage points).
- Edge cases: H ≤ 0 or non-finite ⇒ the forward trial cannot pass and closes the capital route. δ is searched on
  [0, 0.05]; if even 0.05 keeps every cell ≥ 0.08, H = 0.05.

**H_val.** The same procedure runs on the validation readout, frozen now as a formula and computed at the readout.
The forward verdict is computed against H as soon as the forward endpoint lands. The capital gate uses the verdict
against **min(H, H_val)** once validation exists. Two margins are not a confidence bound, and the minimum does not
pretend to be one. It stops discovery's selection-inflated margin from outliving a smaller validation margin.

**Population.** From the first entry session onward, each NYSE session t:
- run the trial's signal on the validated universe's `price_daily`, exactly as the construction defines it:
  26-bar no-gap history, close ≥ $5, the most illiquid third by 20-session Amihud, the 5-session return, the lowest
  f = 0.05, ties per the harness;
- the cohort is written append-only before the t+1 entry window opens: members, domain size and the sha256 of the
  ranked domain;
- `price_daily` corrections after that point never alter a written cohort;
- a selected name eToro cannot quote stays in the denominator as missing.

Relative selection need not select an actual loss (#3449 residual). "Post-loss" means "in the lowest 5% of the
5-session return".

At the 2026-09-24/25 census the domain is ≈ 3,560 names, so each cohort has ≈ 59 positions.

**Capture protocol** (a construction; no published formulation fixes execution-cost capture windows):
- **Entry:** the first eToro quote whose provider timestamp is in [open, open + 5 min) on t+1.
- **Exit:** the last eToro quote whose provider timestamp is in [close − 5 min, close) on t+5.
- "Open" and "close" come from the NYSE calendar: `hunt_panel.nyse_sessions` plus the official early-close
  schedule, so a half-day's exit window ends at 13:00 ET. Times are zoned America/New_York, so DST is handled.
- A quote is valid if bid and ask are finite and > 0 and ask ≥ bid. A locked quote is valid. An extreme valid
  spread is an observation, never "bad data".
- The windows put the capture as near as the feed allows to the printed open and close the backtest traded at.
  The price moves between the print and the capture are not a cost, and they are not measured.

**Missing.** The unit is the position. A missing or invalid leg makes the position missing. The verdict is computed
twice:
- (a) on the complete positions;
- (b) with every missing position charged the stress half-spread 0.00725 per side in the same form. That is the
  worst frozen charge.

PASS needs both. Missingness is plausibly informative: halts and absent quotes mark the costliest names. So there
is no tolerated missing share; the bound replaces one.

**Weighting and dependence.**
- A session's excess cost is the equal-weighted mean of its positions' e_i; that matches the book's weighting
  within a slot. The statistic is the mean ē of the session means, so each session counts equally, as the book's
  slots do.
- SE = sd(session means)/√n.
- Holdings overlap, so adjacent sessions' means can correlate. When the lag-1 autocorrelation ρ̂₁ > 0, the SE is
  inflated by √((1 + ρ̂₁)/(1 − ρ̂₁)), the AR(1) variance-inflation factor.
- The reference is Student t with n − 1 degrees of freedom. With few clusters, a normal 1.645 is anti-conservative
  (Cameron & Miller 2015, *J. Human Resources* 50(2), §VI).

**Sample size: internal pilot, variance only.**
- The first 5 entry sessions form a pilot. From it the readout computes **only** the SD of the session means. The
  mean is not computed and not shown.
- n = max(20, ⌈((t₀.₉₅,ₙ₋₁ + z₀.₈₀) · σ̂ / H)²⌉), capped at 60 sessions and iterated for the df. The target is
  80% power to pass when the true ē = 0, against H.
- The pilot sessions count in the final sample. Variance-only internal pilots of this form barely move the
  type-I error (Wittes & Brittain 1990, *Stat. Med.* 9(1–2)).
- If the cap binds, the readout states the achieved power.
- Design estimate only: the evening census gives a position-level SD of 0.0075–0.0100. Reproduce it with
  `scripts/hunt_recorded_spread_census.py`'s domain and spread SQL on snapshots 1 and 2. That SD would give a
  session-mean SD of about 0.0010–0.0034 under within-session correlation 0–0.1 (n ≈ 20–25 sessions at H ≈ 0.00177). Neither the correlation nor the
  opening-spread level is measured, which is why the pilot exists.

**Endpoint and dates.**
- The entry sessions are the first n NYSE sessions whose entry window opens after the declaration's freeze
  timestamp.
- A session lost to an outage stays in the sample, with every position missing. It is never skipped, so the
  measured regime cannot be chosen.
- The trial finalises at the last cohort's t+5 exit window. It has no extension, no early stop and no amendment.

**Verdict,** at the final sample, with the upper bound U = ē + t₀.₉₅,ₙ₋₁ · SE:
- PASS: U ≤ threshold in both (a) and (b);
- FAIL: ē ≥ threshold in (a) or (b);
- "undetermined": otherwise.

The threshold is H at the endpoint and min(H, H_val) for capital. FAIL is operational: the point estimate
exhausts the margin, not a proof that the true cost exceeds it. The readout states ē, SE, ρ̂₁, n, the missing
share, both arms and the achieved MDE.

**Terminal.** FAIL or "undetermined" closes the capital route for this construction. There is no second forward
cost trial on it. A PASS is valid for the forward window's regime and for order sizes up to the F2 notional below;
it certifies no other population.

## Stage F2: fill calibration, before capital
Quotes are not fills. F2 measures what a market order actually pays against the captured quote.

**Design.**
- 10 consecutive entry sessions after the forward endpoint, 3 positions each: 30 round trips.
- Names are drawn uniformly from that session's cohort, seeded by sha256(declaration id ‖ session date), so the
  draw is fixed before any fill.
- Order notional is a frozen demo amount (declared in the F2 declaration).
- Entry is a market buy placed in the entry window; exit is a market sell in the exit window.

**Measurement.** Slippage is signed against the executable side of the same-moment captured quote: (fill − ask)/ask
on entry and (bid − fill)/bid on exit. It is measured against the executable side, not the mid, so it does not
double-count the spread already in e_i. A rejected, unfilled or partially filled order counts as a failure and is
charged the stress half-spread on that leg.

**Verdict.** PASS needs U_quote + U_slip ≤ min(H, H_val).
- U_quote is the forward trial's upper bound.
- U_slip is the one-sided 95% t upper bound on the mean round-trip slippage over session means, with 9 df.
- Summing two upper bounds is conservative.

F2 has a fixed endpoint; FAIL or undetermined is terminal, as for the forward trial.

**Who runs it.** Placing orders mutates broker state, so F2 is **loop-ineligible**. It runs under the standing demo
approval in an attended or supervisor session.

**Limits.** A demo fill may not reproduce live queueing, depth, impact or rejections. F2 certifies the demo
execution path at the F2 notional, nothing more. Capital per position is capped at the F2 notional until a
larger-size calibration exists.

## Capital gate (R4)
All of these: hunt 3's validation promotion; forward cost PASS against min(H, H_val); F2 PASS; the #2844 sandbox;

- The forward trial measures eToro's 2026 quoted spread, not the cost the backtest charged for 2009–2021.
- The entry mid stands in for the printed open, and the quote capture happens up to 5 minutes after the print.
