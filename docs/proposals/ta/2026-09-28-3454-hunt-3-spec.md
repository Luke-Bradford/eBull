## Why the stress gate is a defect, not a result to argue with
#3449 set the stress charge at 1.450% round trip, **above every recorded mean and p90** in the illiquid census
(#3449 "The bar", stress-cell bullet), and then read that proxy as SC1's "RECORDED spreads" because no historical
spread exists. The only instrument that measures the real cost is forward paper, and #3449 put forward paper behind
the stress gate. Any small-cap construction with a realistic gross fails that gate whatever its true cost. The
defect is in the gate's construction and would have failed any hunt; it is not specific to hunt 2's number.

## Deviation 2, which R2 implies but does not state: condition 4 cannot stay a gate either
Hunt 2 failed two conditions, not one (#3449 "Outcome"): (3) the stress cells, and **(4) power at the bar,
excess-series MDE₈₀ 0.210 against 0.08**. Hunt 3 inherits hunt 2's discovery data unchanged (below), so its
condition 4 reproduces 0.210 exactly. Keeping condition 4 as a gate closes hunt 3 before its validation look, which
contradicts R2's "the validation look is the test". So hunt 3 also moves condition 4 from gate to reported
statement.

This is result-shaped in the same way as R1 and is disclosed the same way. The case that it is also a defect of the
gate's construction: condition 4 asks a 12-year window to separate an 8%/yr excess **over SPY** from zero at
t > 3. The excess series of a long-only small-cap book carries its whole tracking error to SPY. The families 1–4
re-screen (#3453) states the requirement as TE_NW ≤ 7.28%/yr (design IR ≥ 1.10), and no construction in the
operator's small-cap target is expected to reach it. That makes condition 4 a filter that no candidate in the
target can pass, the same class as the stress gate.

**What the validation look can and cannot establish, once condition 4 is not a gate.** Take hunt 2's figures,
MDE₈₀ 0.210 and MDE₅₀ 0.164, at t > 3 on the 3,079-session validation grid. They imply a standard error on
validation `excess_ann` of 0.164 / 3 ≈ 0.0547. This assumes discovery's variance carries over to a post-decimal
market. With that SE, the point-estimate gate `excess_ann` ≥ 0.08 passes:
- with probability ≈ 0.072 when the true excess is 0;
- with probability 0.5 when it is exactly 0.08;
- with probability ≈ 0.95 when it equals discovery's lower base cell, 0.169 (discovery is selection-inflated, so
  this is an upper reference, not an expectation).

Reproduce: `1 − Φ((0.08 − μ) / (0.164/3))`. Those rates apply to the canonical cell alone; every base cell must
pass, and the cells are highly correlated (#3449: every termination policy gave the same figures). A validation
PASS therefore establishes arm > control (harness `PASS`: t > 3 on the active series, plus DSR) and a point
estimate at or above the bar. It does **not** establish excess ≥ 8% with any stated confidence. The forward cost
trial, the fill calibration and the person-gated capital step all sit behind it.

**Programme consequence, stated and not acted on here.** #3453's "no trial" for families 1–4 rests partly on
power-first. If this ruling stands, that conclusion is reopenable on the same argument. That is a queue decision
for #2437, not part of #3454.

## Hunt 3's identity: inherit, do not recompute
Re-running discovery is impossible and would be wrong:
- `hunt_trials_one_evaluate_per_candidate_split` (`sql/427`) allows one evaluate row per (candidate, split), and
  `evaluate` refuses `candidate_owned_by_other_hunt` before that. Hunt 2 owns the discovery row.
- A second row on identical data would add nothing but a second copy of the same number.

So hunt 3 **inherits hunt 2's discovery outcome** (hunt_trial_id 2, outcome `151c3c6a…`):
- `HUNT_BUDGETS["hunt-3"] = 0`: hunt 3 registers no discovery row.
- A reviewed constant `HUNT_INHERITED_DISCOVERY = {"hunt-3": 2}` lets the door's pin read that outcome as hunt 3's
  discovery evidence. It is valid only for the identical candidate hash, and only while hunt 2 is in
  `HUNT_CLOSED`. Every other pin rule is unchanged.
- **The harness model must not move.** The stored row carries `hunt-harness-v1+58244f6f49e168f2`, which equals
  the current `HUNT_HARNESS_MODEL_ID` (checked 2026-09-28 on the dev DB). Any change that moves it makes the pin
  refuse `pin_*_stale_harness_model`. The implementation keeps the gate block and the inheritance out of the
  hashed modules (`hunt_compute`, `hunt_panel`, `hunt_evaluator`, `hunt_inference` and the model constants), and a
  test pins the id. If a move is ever unavoidable, hunt 3 closes "undetermined"; it is not re-run.
- **BY counts hunt 3 as one more search.** M rises by 1 through the r16 register entry
  `hunt-3-reregistration`. Unlike r14 / r15, this entry is **included** in M_inh, because no `hunt_trials` row
  carries it. The freeze recomputes BY at the new M.
- Validation registers normally: `evaluate` for (candidate, `validation`) has no existing row, so ownership passes,
  and the door pins it against hunt 3's frozen declaration.

## Forward cost trial (R3)
Its own declaration, frozen before the first recorded quote. It measures cost only; it does not test the return.

**Headroom H, by construction, from the stored discovery readout (no price read).**
H = (min over base cells of `excess_ann` − 0.08) × h / (252 × u), in round-trip percentage points per position.
Here u is the invested share of slot-sessions (1.0 in hunt 2). The derivation: a uniform extra round-trip charge Δ
on every position lowers each invested slot's return by ≈ Δ over its h sessions, so it lowers `excess_ann` by
≈ 252·Δ·u/h. Hunt 2's rounded figures give H ≈ (0.169 − 0.08) × 5/252 ≈ **0.18 points**; the implementation computes
H from the unrounded stored cells and freezes it. The construction ignores compounding inside the slot and the
fact that `_half_spreads` prices both sides at entry. As a check, hunt 2's own base and stress pair implies a slope
of 50.2 per point against the constructed 50.4 (the stress arm applies a max(), not a uniform shift).

**Also frozen before the look: H_val.** The same formula on the validation readout. Capital needs the forward
excess cost below **min(H, H_val)**, so the discovery headroom (selection-inflated) cannot carry a validation miss
of the margin.

**Population.** Each NYSE session t: run the trial's signal on the validated universe's `price_daily` exactly as
the construction defines it (26-bar no-gap history, close ≥ $5, most illiquid third by 20-session Amihud,
5-session return, lowest f = 0.05, ties per the harness). At the 2026-09-24/25 census that domain is ≈ 3,560
names, so ≈ 59 positions a day.

**Protocol.**
- Entry quote: eToro bid/ask for each selected name at the first capture at or after 09:31 ET on t+1. Exit quote:
  at 15:55 ET on t+5. One capture per leg, `etoro_rate_observations` shape, append-only.
- Per position, realised round-trip spread c_i = half-spread at entry + half-spread at exit, each as a share of its
  own mid. The charged base band b_i = the band `cost_band_for` gives at the entry mid, as traded. The tariff is
  identical in both and cancels. Excess cost e_i = c_i − b_i.
- A name with no quote, a crossed or zero quote, or a halt at a leg is recorded as missing and counted. More than 10%
  missing at the endpoint refuses the verdict ("undetermined"): a missing quote is not a zero cost.
- **Fixed endpoint:** 20 NYSE entry sessions after the freeze (and their exits). No extension, no early stop.
- **Dependence:** positions on one entry session share a market state, so the SE is clustered by entry session
  (20 clusters).
- **Verdict:** PASS if the one-sided 95% upper bound ē + 1.645·SE_cluster < min(H, H_val); FAIL if ē ≥ min(H, H_val);
  otherwise "undetermined". The readout states ē, SE, n, the missing share and the MDE the realised SE implied.
- **Design power, stated as an estimate:** the evening census gives a position-level SD of ≈ 0.75–1.0 points (run
  on snapshots 1 and 2 with `scripts/hunt_recorded_spread_census.py`'s domain and spread SQL). At ≈ 1,180 positions
  and a design effect of 6.8 (within-session correlation 0.1, 59 per cluster), SE ≈ 0.076, so a true ē = 0 passes
  with probability ≈ 0.75 at H = 0.18. Opening spreads in post-loss names are expected to be wider than the census;
  the prior is a FAIL, and that is the point of measuring it.
- Descriptive only: the forward cohort's gross and net returns against SPY. Not a gate at n = 20 sessions.

**Stage F2, fill calibration, before capital.** Quotes are not fills. Before any capital, the engine places demo
market orders on a declared subsample (≥ 30 round trips from the same protocol) and reports realised fill vs the
same-moment quote. Capital needs the realised mean slippage over quote to fit inside the remaining margin,
min(H, H_val) − ē. Placing those orders mutates broker state, so it is **loop-ineligible**. It runs under the
standing demo approval in an attended or supervisor session.
