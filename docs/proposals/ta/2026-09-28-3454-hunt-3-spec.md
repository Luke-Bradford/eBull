## Where the confirmatory claim lives, and why removing discovery gates does not touch it
Hunt 3's discovery evidence is not a test of anything. The same data chose the construction (hunt 1), and two of
its gates were changed after seeing it. Disclosure does not repair that, and BY's extra count (below) is
bookkeeping: hunt 2's discovery p-value underflows to 0, so the +2 it adds to M changes nothing.

The discovery gates decide only **whether to spend the validation look**. They do not enter the validation
statistics. The confirmatory claim is therefore made **only** on the validation window, under a gate frozen before
the look. The **admission procedure is relaxed**: this candidate reaches a look that the old rules would have
refused it. The validation test adds a requirement of its own (condition 4 below). The relaxation costs programme
budget: the look is spent on a candidate the old rules would have stopped. R2 accepts that cost, and the lineage
closure below caps it.

**Validation exposure audit: a previously examined window, not a first look.** The #3384 census (Finding 5)
counts 13 prior labels whose windows overlap validation. Those most relevant to this construction:
- **s4 and s8.** Both are daily-bar reversion strategies, run survivorship-free over 1962-01-02 → 2021-06-28, so
  they cover all of validation. Both were gross-positive (#2831). That is prior programme knowledge that
  short-horizon reversal paid in this window, and it favours the arm.
- **§2.8.** It measured 1-day, 5-day and 1-month return autocorrelation over 1962–2024 on the survivor-only
  corpus. The 5-day horizon is this construction's formation horizon.
- **#2481.** It ran on 2020+, which overlaps validation on 2020-01-02 → 2021-06-28. It took liquid names (≥ $20,
  ≥ $10m median dollar volume), fired on a single-session fall of ≥ 12%, and traded short. It found
  continuation, which is evidence *against* the arm.
- **SPY's validation-window return,** 0.1587, published by #3449. That is the tracker.

No hunt has read this candidate's arm or control returns on validation: `hunt_trials` holds no validation row
(dev DB, 2026-09-28).

So the validation look is the programme's standard one: the **least-examined window available**. It is not an
unseen one. The prior labels are charged through the inherited floor M_inh (377) in BY and DSR, which is how the
harness treats every hunt's validation (census "Inputs" 2). Hunt 3's claim is no stronger than that treatment.

## R1: why the stress gate is withdrawn

## Condition 4 moves from a pre-look power gate to a post-look significance gate
Hunt 2 failed two conditions (#3449 "Outcome"): (3) the stress cells, and (4) excess-series MDE₈₀ 0.210 against
0.08. Hunt 3 inherits hunt 2's discovery data, so a pre-look condition 4 reproduces 0.210 and closes hunt 3 before
the look R2 orders. R2 does not say what happens to condition 4. v1 dropped it silently; that was an unsanctioned
widening, and v2 withdrew it.

**The amendment, recorded.** Condition 4 is SC1's "power-first", from the supervisor's 09-27 21:50Z correction on
#2437. It is a supervisor rule, not an operator settled decision (`docs/settled-decisions.md` has no power-first
entry). The 01:04Z refill delegated the honesty call on hunt 3 to Codex ckpt-1. Round 2 returned "conditionally
honest": the amendment must be documented, and low power alone does not require closure. The amendment below is
therefore made here, and #2437 records it with this spec's merge.

v2 rule: condition 4 stops being a pre-look *prediction* that validation can detect 8%, and becomes a post-look
*requirement*. The validation excess series (arm − SPY, canonical cell) must itself be significant at the harness's
own threshold. That threshold is t > 3, Newey–West at the lag `hunt_inference.hunt_lag` assigns the validation
grid (the door's `power_statement` construction). The point estimate must also reach 0.08.

Reasons:
- It answers the finding that harness `PASS` tests arm against control. An arm can significantly beat a losing
  control and still fail to beat SPY. The capital claim is "beats SPY", so validation must test that.
- It is an added requirement at validation, not a stricter admission rule. The two constrain different things,
  and this candidate fails the old one and may pass the new one. Validation estimates its own SE. For illustration
  only, at hunt 2's discovery SE the test needs `excess_ann` ≳ 3 × 0.0547 ≈ 0.164.
- The power problem is stated, not removed. Suppose discovery's variance carries over to a post-decimal market,
  giving SE(`excess_ann`) ≈ 0.164 / 3 ≈ 0.0547 on the 3,079-session grid (from hunt 2's MDE₅₀). Then the
  canonical cell passes t > 3 with probability ≈ 0.0013 at a true excess of 0, ≈ 0.062 at 0.08, and ≈ 0.54 at
  0.169. 0.169 is discovery's lower base cell, which selection inflates, so treat it as an upper reference.
  Reproduce: `1 − Φ(3 − μ/(0.164/3))`. These are canonical-cell, normal-approximation figures. They are not power
  for the worst base cell, DSR or the joint rule.

A miss is the likelier outcome. R2 orders the look anyway, and the lineage closure below makes it the last one.

**Programme consequence, stated and not acted on here.** #3453's families 1–4 "no trial" rests partly on
pre-look power-first. Whether this conversion should apply programme-wide is a queue decision for #2437.

## Lineage closure (terminal invariant)
Hunt 3's validation look is the **last historical look for this construction lineage**, whatever it returns. The
lineage is `extreme_move_illiquid`: signal module `signal_code_sha256` of hunts 1–3, under any entry point, lag, h,
f, floor or cost variant.

**Durable before any number.** The closure is keyed on database state, not on a later code change:
- `evaluate` refuses `lineage_closed` for any spec with that family or that signal hash once a `validation`-split
  `hunt_trials` row exists for the lineage.
- That row is committed before compute reads a price (the harness's "log before number"). So a crashed,
  interrupted or abandoned look still closes the lineage.
- A substantive pre-look closure (flag drift) closes it through `HUNT_CLOSED`. Until that edit lands no look is
  possible: validation cannot register without a frozen declaration, and the refusal left none. The family and
  hash check runs inside `evaluate`, under `hunt_programme_lock`, with registration.
- Afterwards the reviewed constants `HUNT_CLOSED["hunt-3"]` and `CLOSED_LINEAGES = {"extreme_move_illiquid":
  "<signal_code_sha256>"}` record the terminal state.

An equivalent re-implementation under a new family name and a new source hash cannot be detected mechanically. It is
stopped where every hunt is stopped: a hunt opens only through a reviewed `HUNT_BUDGETS` PR, and this section
forbids one for the lineage. There is no hunt 4 re-registration and no further gate amendment. The forward trial
and F2 measure cost on this one candidate; they are not further looks at its return.

## Flag → validation declaration
Evaluated on the inherited outcome at the freeze, in one snapshot under the programme lock.

**Gating** (a refused cell, a non-finite input, a missing key or zero entered positions counts as not met):
1. BY flag at the recomputed M (`hunt_door.discovery_by_readout`).
2. Active mean (arm − control) > 0 in every base cell.
3. `excess_ann` ≥ 0.08 in every base cell.

**Reported only** (a finite value, a failing value and "unavailable" are all recorded, and none of them blocks the
freeze): the six stress cells' `excess_ann`, and the pre-look excess-power statement (`excess_power`). The door's
active-series power statement stays in the declaration unchanged.

From hunt 2's readout, 1–3 held (base `excess_ann` 0.175 with dividends, 0.169 without). The freeze recomputes
them; it does not copy them.

**`hunt_gate` block**, built as #3449 specified, with R1 and the condition-4 move applied. It holds:
- the bar 0.08 and the t threshold 3;
- the exact 12 cell keys, each marked `gated` (6 base) or `reported` (6 stress). The freeze refuses a key set that
  is not exactly these 12, and any non-finite gated value;
- conditions 1–3 as evaluated, and the reported values;
- the validation significance rule (canonical cell, excess series, NW lag rule);
- both tracker identities;
- the disclosure;
- the inheritance contract's pinned values;
- the decision-procedure pins above.

The freeze recomputes every key; a stale key refuses. The readout reads the gate from the frozen document, never
from code.

**Enforcement.** `HUNTS_AWAITING_GATE` gains `hunt-3` until the block ships. After that, the refusal is **per
declaration**: hunt 3's validation freeze, look and readout each refuse unless the frozen document carries a
`hunt_gate` block that is present, well-formed and matches its recomputation. The runner's readout is the only
promotion path out of hunt 3. A generic harness `PASS` on hunt 3 is recorded, but it promotes nothing without the
gate.

**Flag drift.** As in #3449, a substantive refusal closes hunt 3 with its label. Here "substantive" means
conditions 1–3, the stale-key and stale-model pins, and `inherited_discovery_invalid`. Reported values never close
it. A procedural refusal (the register tripwire, a non-canonical document) is fixed, then retried on the snapshot
current at the retry.

**At validation, one look.** Promotion to the capital gate needs **all** of:
- a harness verdict of `PASS`, or `PASS_CONTINGENT` whose listed failures are **all** stress failures
  (`hunt_inference.decide_verdict`: base cells pass t > 3, DSR and mean > 0, and only stress cells fail). This is
  R1 applied to the harness's own stress cells. Every `NOT_PASS_*`, `FAIL_CONTROL` or `UNDETERMINED` verdict fails;
- `excess_ann` ≥ 0.08 in every base cell;
- the canonical cell's validation excess series with NW t > 3.

Each of these counts as **not met** if its input is refused, missing or non-finite. That covers:
- a refused or missing cell key;
- a tracker coverage refusal (#3449's gap and validity rules);
- a degenerate HAC estimate (`hunt_inference.hac_estimate` returning a refusal);
- a non-finite t;
- zero entered positions.

The readout also reports the stress cells, each base cell's excess t, the realised excess-series SE and M_val
(below). A miss closes hunt 3 as "no demonstrated edge", naming the failing condition. An unavailable input closes
it as "undetermined". Either way the lineage closes. The significance claim is made **at base cost**. Consuming
the margin can drop the excess t below 3 while `excess_ann` still clears 0.08, and the capital gate does not
claim otherwise.

## Forward cost trial (R3): what this spec fixes
The forward trial has its **own declaration spec**, with its own ckpt-1, frozen before the first capture. The
statistical design belongs there: capture windows, quote validity, the missing-data bound, weighting, dependence,
sample size, endpoint and F2. Its checklist is every forward and F2 finding in this spec's three ckpt-1 files:
round 1 #22–#65, round 2 #8–#18 and #27–#45, and round 3 #14–#21 where they still apply to the contract below.
Each must be answered or rebutted there. This spec fixes only what that trial binds to.

**The binding quantity is the drag, in the bar's own units.** R3's threshold is "base band + headroom at the 8%
bar". The margin at the bar is already an annualised excess, so the forward trial measures its cost in that same
unit, and no conversion into a per-trade or per-side charge is needed.
- **Margin.** M_disc = min over the six base cells of (`excess_ann` − 0.08), read from hunt 2's stored discovery
  readout. There is no price read and no recomputation. Hunt 2's rounded figures give M_disc ≈ 0.089.
- **Validation margin.** M_val is the same formula on hunt 3's validation readout, computed at the readout and
  written into its immutable record. The capital threshold is **min(M_disc, M_val)**. It is a point threshold,
  not a confidence bound. It stops discovery's selection-inflated margin from outliving a smaller validation one.
- **Drag.** D is the annualised difference between two books over the forward cohort, both computed by the
  harness's own slot evaluator (`hunt_evaluator`, the frozen construction). Both books price entries and exits at
  the captured quote mids m = (bid + ask)/2, never at a provider mid field.
  - The **band book** charges each position hs = its `cost_band_for` band + commission, priced at the entry mid
    with `price_basis="as_traded"`: the backtest's own charge.
  - The **quote book** fills each entry at the captured ask and each exit at the captured bid, and adds the same
    commission.
  - D = 252 × mean over forward sessions of (band book return − quote book return).

  Gross returns, weights, slots, commission and every return–cost interaction are identical in the two books, so D
  isolates what the real spreads cost the construction relative to what the backtest charged. That answers the
  unit, asymmetry and heterogeneity findings (round 2 #19–#22, round 3 #14–#15) by construction, not by
  approximation.
- **Verdict shape** (the estimator, its SE and the dependence treatment are the forward spec's): PASS needs an upper
  confidence bound on D ≤ the threshold; FAIL is D's point estimate > the threshold; anything else is
  "undetermined". The dispositions are disjoint.

What D does not cover: the gap between the quote and the printed open or close, which is F2's and the forward
spec's to bound, and the upward biases in the gross (Residuals). The margin inherits those biases.

**Terminal rule.**
- The forward verdict is final at its fixed endpoint.
- If the endpoint lands before validation, the verdict against M_disc is **provisional**. When M_val arrives, the
  **same endpoint evidence** is re-judged once against min(M_disc, M_val), with no new sampling, and that result
  is final.
- If validation closes hunt 3, or M_val is unavailable or ≤ 0, the capital route closes.
- FAIL or "undetermined" closes the capital route for this candidate. There is no second forward cost trial. The
  same holds for F2.

**F2 (fill calibration) stays loop-ineligible.** It places demo orders, so it runs only under the standing demo
approval, in an attended or supervisor session. A demo fill certifies the demo execution path at the calibrated
notional, not live fills. Capital per position is capped at that notional.

## Residuals
- Everything in #3449 "Residuals". Survivorship before 2013-06-21, stale-mark exits, terminal haircuts, no market
  impact and uncosted slot rebalancing all push **both** the validation excess and the margin upward. Reporting them does
  not make the margin conservative, and the forward drag D does not detect them: it measures cost, not gross.
- Hunt 3's discovery evidence is not a test (above).
- The forward trial measures eToro's 2026 quoted spread, not the cost the backtest charged for 2009–2021.
- The forward quote mid stands in for the printed open and close the backtest traded at; the capture offset is
  the forward declaration's to bound.
