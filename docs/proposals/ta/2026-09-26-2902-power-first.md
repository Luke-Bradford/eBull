# #2902 valuation arm — power-first statement: not run as specified

Refs #2902, #2899, #2437 (queue rules 2026-09-26, rule 1). Programme: `2026-09-24-selection-programme-v2.md`.
Codex ckpt-1 ran on the first draft (34 findings, 2026-09-26). Its verdict: the arithmetic is right and dropping the arm
is allowed, but "every reachable variant fails" was overclaimed. This version is scoped to what is measured.

## Rule applied
Queue rule 1 (#2437, 2026-09-26): before any arm is built or frozen, compute power at the **smallest economically
worthwhile edge**. If power < 0.5 there, do not run it: redesign (longer window, price-only variant back to 1990,
broader universe) or drop it, and record why. No bar is lowered.

- **Declared edge: 1.5%/yr net**, arm minus its own annual 1/N control, as an annualised arithmetic mean of monthly
  active returns. The value is the rule's own example for a long-only selection arm. The verdict below does not
  depend on it: it holds for any declared edge below ~0.9 × TE, about 5%/yr here.
- **Bar: t > 3**, the programme's floor ("Statistics and bar"). #2902's deflated bar is at least that high, since 11
  historical rows are already charged (`2026-09-25-2901-quality-result.md`, "Trial accounting"). Power at the real
  bar is therefore no higher than shown. t > 1.96 is printed only to show the verdict does not hinge on the bar.
- **Window**: 134 months (2013-07 … 2024-08), the #2901 harness window #2902 would reuse (companyfacts XBRL from
  2011-06; price corpus to 2024-09-27). The programme's 13-year figure is printed as well.

## Measurement
Power at bar t is Φ(edge × √years / TE − t). It depends on the edge only through the **information ratio** IR =
edge / TE, and reaches 0.5 exactly when IR = t / √years. Mixing an arm with its control scales edge and TE together,
so dilution leaves IR unchanged. Lowering TE with a broader or milder tilt helps only if it raises IR.

Proxy: Kenneth French Data Library (CRSP, equal-weight, monthly), months 1963-07 … 2013-06. The script discards every
row from 2013-07 onward before any value is used. Each proxy is an arm minus the firm-count-weighted average of the
EW portfolios that partition its universe, which is the monthly-rebalanced EW universe:

- all-cap value quintile: `Hi 20` against the five B/M quintiles;
- all-cap value tertile: `Hi 30` against the three tertiles (a broader tilt);
- small-cap value: `SMALL HiBM` against the five `ME1` portfolios of the 5×5 size × B/M sort. This is the operator's
  small-cap variant (north-star comment on #2437, 2026-09-26).

**Limits.**
- The statistics are an approximation: IID with no autocorrelation adjustment, and normal (known-variance) power
  rather than Student-t or HAC power. Serial dependence can move effective TE in either direction.
- The proxy is not our arm. It uses NYSE breakpoints (not 20% of our universe), BE > 0 CRSP firms (not the eToro-
  reachable PIT universe), monthly EW rebalancing (not annual 1/N with drift), and gross returns. Its small-cap row
  includes micro-caps.
- It is a 2026 vintage of a revised library, and the TE is pooled over 1963–2013, so a 2013–2024 regime may differ.
- The gross mean includes the pre-publication years and pays no cost. McLean & Pontiff measure a 58% average post-
  publication decline (`.claude/skills/quant/strategy-evidence.md` §2.6). The gross IR is an optimistic reference,
  not an estimate of this arm's net edge.

## Verdict: power 0.018 (all-cap), 0.022 (small-cap) at the declared edge → not run as specified
At 1.5%/yr net over 11.2 years at t > 3, a real edge passes about 2% of the time (0.02 at 13 years). A pass would be
informative, since the null pass rate is 0.0013, but it is very unlikely. A fail would be close to uninformative.
Either way the attempt charges the register. The rule's threshold is 0.5, so the arm is not run.

The measurement also shows how much the test would need. 50% power at t > 3 needs a **net** IR of 0.90 (0.83 at 13
years). That is about the **gross, full-history, pre-publication** IR of the value tilt in the proxy (0.87–0.94). The
test is informative only if our arm keeps, after costs and publication decay, the whole risk-adjusted premium that
value earned in 1963–2013. The broader tertile tilt does not change this (IR 0.91 vs 0.94), which is the dilution
point above measured.

**Value × quality**: not measured. No pre-window series with the NMV summed-rank long-only construction is held.
Summed ranks can raise IR by combining imperfectly correlated signals, so value's IR is not a bound for it. The gate
requires power to be **shown** ≥ 0.5 before a build, and it has not been shown, so this arm is not built either.
Reopening it needs a pre-window estimate of its long-only net IR ≥ 0.90 against 1/N, e.g. from a published
combination series.

## Redesign options considered
- **Longer window.** At the declared IR (0.27–0.31), 50% power at t > 3 needs 93–124 years. Book equity in our corpus
  starts with XBRL (2009–2011). No free PIT pre-2009 fundamentals source is held. Acquiring one is a resource
  judgement, not something this note measures.
- **Price-only variant back to 1990.** Prices cannot rebuild B/M, so this is not a variant of #2902's valuation claim.
  A price-only hypothesis would be a new arm with its own ticket, declaration and power step.
- **Broader universe or milder tilt.** Changes TE, but only helps if IR rises. On the proxy, the broader tilt lowers IR.
- **Lower bar.** Forbidden. It would not rescue the test either: 0.14–0.20 at t > 1.96.

**Decision: #2902 closes unrun, as the rule directs for an arm below 0.5 with no measured redesign above it.** The power
step reads only published pre-window portfolio data. It touches no arm return, no holdout and no #2599 register row,
so no declaration is written and no row is charged. The 11 historical rows carry forward unchanged.

## Arithmetic for the rest of the selection line (does not decide #2903/#2904)
With the same window and bar, 50% power needs a net IR against the arm's own control of at least 0.90, i.e. an edge
of at least 0.90 × TE. Each later arm runs its own power step. Its worthwhile edge is declared on economic grounds
before that step, never sized to clear it.
