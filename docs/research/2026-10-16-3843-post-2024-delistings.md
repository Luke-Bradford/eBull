# Post-2024-09 delistings: which dead names we can price (#3843)

The survivorship-free Intrader archive ends 2024-09-27. Every out-of-sample test after that date, including
the #3822 momentum check, has used only names alive today. This note measures how much of the dead side
from 2024-10 onwards our own stored sources can price. `scripts/research_3843/post_2024_series.py` builds the
per-event manifest. It is read-only, and its docstring states the source rule for each verdict.

Reproduce (dev DB, 2026-10-16 22:40Z):

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. uv run python scripts/research_3843/post_2024_series.py --json /tmp/m3843.json
```

## Result

The register held 624 common-equity Form 25 events filed from 2024-10-01 to 2026-10-08. One event is one
(issuer CIK, resolved symbol); its filings and amendments count once.

- **325 events (52.1%) are covered:** a source holds a series that ends with the listing.
  - PWB@2026-09-09 covers 165.
  - eToro `price_daily` covers 160.
  - The 2026-07-08 PWB load covers none that the 09-09 capture does not already cover.
- **299 events are uncovered.** A test over this window is survivor-only for these names, and must say so.

## What the sources are, measured

- **PWB keeps a delisted name for under a year, then drops it.** The earliest event the 09-09 capture covers
  was filed 2025-10-17, and none of the 305 events filed before 2025-10 is in it. That includes HES, JNPR, ANSS, SKX and X, all large (a)(3) cash deals. PWB is a
  Yahoo scrape, and Yahoo drops dead tickers. So PWB's dead side depends on the capture date, and a later
  capture will have lost what this one holds. The capture date is in the vendor string for that reason.
- **eToro `price_daily` keeps bars for an instrument eToro still lists.** It covers 160 events. On HES,
  JNPR, ANSS, SKX and X, the last eToro bar comes **2 days before** the Form 25 filing (median 2 over all 160;
  quartiles 1 and 4). So an eToro terminal close can miss the last session or two. For a cash deal the price has
  converged to the deal price by then; for a failure it has not.
- **All 160 eToro-covered dead instruments are still `is_tradable = true` in `instruments`.**
- **Uncovered, by reason:**
  - **239 are absent from every source.**
    - 163 have a symbol: 86 are (a)(3) and 77 are (b), for example TUP and SAVE.
    - 76 have no resolved symbol, so no source can be matched to the removed security. These are mostly
      closed-end funds and foreign issuers (sec-edgar.md §2.6).
  - **46 continue past the effective date** in some source: 26 (a)(3), 19 (b) and 1 (a)(4).
    For an (a)(3) holding-company reorganisation that continuation is correct. Otherwise it is an OTC tail or a
    later occupant of the ticker.
  - **14 are refused:** 8 after capture, 4 conflict and 2 identity unverified.

## How to use it

- A post-2024 universe can add the 325 covered events, each with its source, series or instrument id, last bar
  and last close. Their termination is priced by `app/services/series_termination.py`.
  - PWB series carry `adj_close`, so they give total return. eToro series give price return only.
- Label the result survivor-only for the uncovered events. The (b) residue is the half that matters: 77
  failures with a symbol and no price anywhere is the bias direction for any long book.
- Nothing is written to `research_price_series`. `hunt_harness`'s archive identity hashes `delisting_source`
  and `updated_at`, so linking the 09-09 capture there would move the identity of every pinned PWB series.
