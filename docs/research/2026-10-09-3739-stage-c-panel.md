# #3739 — stage C: a panel for 2024-09..2026-08 with measured survivorship coverage

Status: spec, round 5. It replaces the round-1–4 design, which inferred corporate actions from vendor rescales
(§"Why the route changed"). Slice 1 (Form 25 register through 2026-10-08) is done (#3739, 2026-10-09 13:42Z). This
spec fixes slices 2–6 before any stage-C cohort is built. No book is evaluated here; #3740 owns the book, its
declaration and its verdict.

**Stage C:** formations M = 2024-08-31 .. 2026-07-31 (24), holding months 2024-09 .. 2026-08.

**What a pass means.** The acceptance test (§9) measures coverage of an enumerated cohort: the Form 25 common-equity
delistings of a bounded issuer set. A pass says that cohort is covered within the limits; it does not prove the panel
survivorship-free.

## Why the route changed

Rounds 1–4 reconstructed every split, ticker change and termination from rescales between revisions of one vendor's
dataset. Checkpoint 1 returned 55, 47, 53 and about 50 findings, most of round 4's blockers labelled FRAMING. The
inference has an unbounded edge-case surface, so the model was wrong, not the patches.

This design keeps the PWB revision history as the **price** source only. Corporate actions come from an **adjudicated
event file**, built from SEC documents over a bounded issuer set by a protocol that reads documents and never prices.
Vendor rescales are used only as a **detector**: a rescale the file does not explain removes the affected ME, it never
supplies a factor. The precedent is step 1 slice 3d's adjudicated CSV (`docs/research/3609-slice-3d-iv-adjudicated.csv`):
a cause per row, cited to a filing.

## Pre-stage measurement: Form 25 filing date against the archive's last bar

On Intrader series linked to a Form 25 (`delisting_source = 'sec_form25'`) whose last bar precedes 2024-08-01,
filing date minus **archive last bar**, in calendar days:

| provision | series | filed before last bar | 0–7 days | 8–31 | > 31 | median / p90 / p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `(a)(3)` | 682 | 60 | 618 | 1 | 3 | 1 / 3 / 4 |
| `(b)` | 194 | 4 | 22 | 88 | 80 | 23 / 76 / 91 |

```sql
select delisting_provision, count(*),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar < 0),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar between 0 and 7),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar between 8 and 31),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar > 31),
       percentile_cont(array[0.5, 0.9, 0.95]) within group (order by delisting_filed_date - last_bar)
from research_price_series
where vendor = 'icyDenev/Intrader' and delisting_source = 'sec_form25' and last_bar < '2024-08-01'
group by 1;
```

This is an archive-endpoint lag distribution, conditional on a successful link; it does not observe economic ends.
For `(b)` the filing trails the last bar by weeks, so it is not an end estimate. `sec-edgar.md` §2.6 trap 5 holds: the
filing date is a different event from the suspension.

## Contract

### 1. The price ladder (`pwb-revision-ladder-v2`)

- **Scan.** The data revisions from `8936ae04` (2024-07-07) to `a4aead25` (2026-10-01), ordered by commit timestamp,
  ties by the HF API's listing order. The ordered index (ordinal, commit id, timestamp, shard oids) is pinned. A read
  refuses if an oid differs.
- **Sessions.** The session calendar is the dates with a usable SPY bar in `a4aead25` (pinned). session(t) is the last
  session on or before t; s(M) = session(M). A month without one refuses the build.
- **Formation revision.** R(M) = the earliest revision holding a usable SPY bar at s(M). The 24 R(M) and `a4aead25` are
  mirrored whole under `var/research_corpus/mirrors/pwb-ladder/<commit>/`.
- **Evidence prefix.** Membership, ME and characteristics at M read only revisions with ordinal ≤ that of R(M), and
  only event-file records whose evidence was accepted by the SEC on or before s(M). A test asserts that appending later
  revisions or later-accepted records leaves every formation's membership and ME unchanged. Holdings (§6) and the
  census (§9) may read later revisions and records.
- **Read key.** A security is read in revision r under the symbol §3's identity intervals assign it at r's last bar.
  The ladder never decides identity.
- **Remote reads.** Other revisions are read by symbol- or date-filtered query. Each result is written to an extract
  file whose sha256 is recorded with the commit, filter and row count, so every decision replays offline.
- **Price bound.** No price, volume or return dated after 2026-08-31 leaves the reader. The reader may report that a
  security has a usable bar after the bound (presence, for §6), never its values.
- **Bar validity.** The #2261 bar rules (`price-quarantine` rule set, version pinned) are applied per revision to
  every bar a rule reads. Only usable bars enter a statistic, a level, a return or a presence test.
- **Licence posture** is unchanged from #3619 slice 3: research use, local mirror, no redistribution.

### 2. The adjudicated issuer set U

U is the set of issuers (CIK) the event file must cover. Defined only from pre-stage data and SEC form types:

1. **Incumbents:** issuers with a security admitted by the step-1 panel at formation 2024-07-31 with final-ME rank ≤ K.
2. **Entrants:** issuers with no admitted security at 2024-07-31 that, with acceptance in 2024-08-01 .. 2026-07-31,
   file a Form 8-A12B or 10-12B registering a class the step-1 security-type rules would admit as common equity, or a
   Form 8-K12B; and issuers whose `submissions` `items` list Item 5.06 (change in shell company status) in that window.
3. **Successors:** any CIK the event file itself records as the successor of a U issuer (§3.1), transitively.

**K, by construction on pre-stage data.** K is the smallest of {1,500, 2,000, 2,500, 3,000} such that, for every
formation pair (M₀, M₀ + h) with M₀ in stage B and 1 ≤ h ≤ 24 formations, inside the pre-stage panel, the final ME of
the top 1,000 at M₀ + h held by issuers ranked > K at M₀ (incumbents only; entrants excluded from both sides) is at most
0.25% of that top 1,000's ME. If none qualifies, K = 3,000 and the measured share is published. The calibration runs
in slice 2 before any adjudication and its output is pinned in the event file's manifest.

U is a bound for adjudication effort, not a universe filter: the panel admits any name its rules admit. §9 measures
admitted ME outside U.

### 3. The event file (`stage-c-event-file-v1`)

#### 3.1 Records

One CSV per record type under `docs/research/3739-event-file/`, every row with: CIK, the class it applies to,
accession number(s), the document file name, a verbatim quote of at most 300 characters, and the SEC acceptance
timestamp of the evidence. Window: events effective 2024-07-01 .. 2026-09-30.

| type | fields | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `identity` | CIK, symbol, valid_from, valid_to | intervals; open-ended at the window edges |
| `split` | CIK, class, ratio (new shares per old), effective date | reverse splits have ratio < 1; stock dividends ≥ 25% are splits (FINRA 11140(b)(2)); smaller stock dividends are recorded as `split` too when a filing states them, since vendors rescale for them |
| `succession` | predecessor CIK, successor CIK, effective date, exchange ratio when stated | holdco reorganisations and redomiciles under Rule 12g-3 |
| `termination` | CIK, class, provision, suspension date when stated, Form 25 accession | joined from `sec_form25_common_equity_delistings`; the register's rules are unchanged |

A split's `SplitStamp` for the panel is (effective date, ratio), so `split_product` applies it to counts dated before
the effective date, as for Intrader stamps.

#### 3.2 Candidate screens (structured sources only)

For each issuer in U, a candidate is any of:
- **split:** an XBRL `StockholdersEquityNoteStockSplitConversionRatio1` fact (Financial Statement and Notes data sets
  or the company-concept API) dated in the window; consecutive cover counts (`dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding`,
  ordered by acceptance) whose ratio lies outside [0.8, 1.25]; an 8-K whose `items` include 5.03 (charter amendment);
- **identity:** two symbols for one CIK, or one symbol for two CIKs, among insider-data-set and cover-page symbol
  observations accepted in the window;
- **succession:** an 8-K12B, a Form 25 under `(a)(3)` whose issuer files no further 10-Q or 10-K, or a successor CIK
  named in a Form 25 EX-99 or an 8-K Item 2.01;
- **termination:** a register row for the CIK.

Each screen is code, with its own tests, and its candidate list is pinned before adjudication starts.

#### 3.3 Adjudication protocol

- **Inputs:** the candidate row and SEC documents for that CIK (and a named counterparty CIK). No price, volume or
  market-data source is opened. Prices that appear inside a document (an offer price, a merger consideration) are not
  recorded.
- **Order of evidence:** the issuer's 8-K or press-release exhibit that announces the action; then the exchange's or
  issuer's statement of the effective trading date; then the next 10-Q or 10-K. When two disagree on a ratio, the
  candidate becomes `contested` and records both.
- **Outcomes:** `confirmed` (a record is written), `rejected` (with the reason: issuance, buyback, preferred class,
  announcement withdrawn), `contested`, or `no_document` (the screen fired but no document states the action).
- **Verification, deterministic:** a checker fetches every cited document by accession and asserts the quote occurs in
  it verbatim (whitespace-normalised) and that the effective date and ratio appear in the quote. A row that fails is
  not admitted.
- **Who:** an agent session or a person; the protocol is the same. The adjudicated CSVs are reviewed in their PR.

#### 3.4 Pre-stage acceptance of the protocol

Before stage C is adjudicated, the screens and protocol run on a pre-stage replica: U′ defined by §2 at formation
2022-07-31, events effective 2022-08-01 .. 2024-07-31. Truth is Intrader's `split_factor` stamps on series the #3361
linkage ties to U′ CIKs.
- **Recall:** confirmed splits matching a stamp (same CIK, ratio within 0.5%, effective date equal to the stamp date
  or the next session) ÷ stamps. Weighted by final ME at the formation before the stamp.
- **Precision:** confirmed splits with no stamp, each listed with its evidence.
- **Pass:** ME-weighted recall ≥ 99% and every miss and every unmatched confirmation explained in the PR. On a fail,
  the screens are revised and the replica is rerun on 2020-08-01 .. 2022-07-31 with U′ at 2020-07-31; the protocol is
  frozen only after a pass on a window it was not revised on.

#### 3.5 Detector-triggered adjudication after the declaration

Once the declaration is merged, the ladder reports every **rescale** on a security the panel reads: consecutive
revisions r₁, r₂ holding it under the same identity interval whose closes on shared usable sessions in the 21 sessions
ending at r₁'s last bar differ from a constant ratio of 1 by more than 0.5%. A rescale the event file explains (a split
effective in (r₁'s last bar, r₂'s last bar] whose ratio matches within 0.5%) needs nothing. Any other rescale becomes a
candidate under §3.3, handed to the adjudicator as (CIK, symbol, the two revision dates) only, with no ratio and no
price. Records confirmed this way are labelled `detector_triggered` and counted separately. The rules do not change.

#### 3.6 Freeze

The event file, the K calibration, the candidate lists, the replica result and the checker output are pinned by
sha256 in the event-file manifest. The stage-C file (§§3.2–3.3) is frozen before #3740's declaration; §3.5's records
are appended after it under their label.

### 5. Raw price and ME

**Raw price.** For a security, a revision R and a session t ≤ R's last bar:
raw_R(t) = close_R(t) × Π ratio over the security's `split` records effective in (t, last bar of R].
raw_R(t) is **unresolved** if any §3.5 rescale on the security between t and R's last bar is unexplained after
adjudication, or if the security has a `contested` or `no_document` split candidate effective in that interval.

**ME.** With R = R(M) and the step-1 share count selected at s(M) (basis date b), step 1's formula applies unchanged:
ME(M) = shares(b) × split_product(stamps, b, s(M)) × raw_R(s(M)), with stamps from §3.1. If raw_R(s(M)) is unresolved,
ME is missing with reason `basis_unresolved`. Checks 1–4 run as step 1 defines them on those stamps.

**$5 exclusion.** The price is raw_R(s(M)); unresolved means excluded as `price_basis_unresolved`.

**Volume basis (check 3).** Dollar volume is `close × volume` in R. Whether the vendor adjusts volume for splits is
undocumented, so it is established on pre-stage data: for each Intrader split stamp dated 2024-07-08 .. 2024-07-31 on
a linked security, compare `close × volume` in revisions captured before the stamp with Intrader's raw `close × volume`
over the 20 sessions before it. If at least 95% of compared sessions agree within 1%, check 3 runs; otherwise check 3 is
`untested` for every row.

**Overlap validation (pre-stage).** Key: (CIK, session t) for every t in 2024-07-08 .. 2024-07-31 where exactly one
Intrader series and one ladder security link to the CIK. Compare Intrader's raw close with raw_R(t) for R = the
earliest revision holding t, using the replica's and the stage-C file's splits. At least 10,000 compared pairs or the
build refuses. If more than 1% of pairs differ by more than 0.5%, the operation fails and stage C is inadmissible.
Runs before the declaration.

**Cap.** For every formation, the share of ME missing for `basis_unresolved`, estimated as shares(b) × close_R(s(M))
for those names, is at most 0.5% of the top 1,000's ME; otherwise the build refuses. The share is published per
formation. By construction.

### 6. Holdings: one state per holding

Weights are fixed at s(M). The holding month is m = M + 1, valued over (s(M), s(m)]. Let d = the security's latest
usable bar on or before s(m) in any revision. States, checked in order; the first match applies:

1. **`terminal`** — the event file has a `termination` for the security whose end (§9's E, upper edge) falls on or
   before s(m), and no `succession` continues it. Return from s(M) to d (zero if d = s(M)), then `classify_termination`
   on the Form 25 evidence and the vendor symbol's `Q` suffix, with `terminal_value_fraction` per arm.
2. **`succeeded`** — a `succession` takes effect in the month. The return runs to the effective date on the predecessor
   and continues on the successor at the recorded exchange ratio; with no stated ratio, the holding is `terminal` with
   `OPERATION_OF_LAW` at the predecessor's last close.
3. **`observed`** — a usable bar at s(m). Return to s(m).
4. **`observed`, early end bar** — d > s(M) and the security holds a usable bar after s(m) in some revision. Return
   to d.
5. **`coverage_exit`** — otherwise: no termination on file and no usable bar to value the month. Return to d (zero if
   d = s(M)), then cash at 0.

**Return arithmetic.** The return is `adj_close` within one revision holding both endpoint bars under the read key:
the latest such revision, because a later capture can carry distributions posted after an earlier one (#3619 slice 3).
If none holds both, it is the product of within-revision ratios bridged at the last shared usable session of
consecutive revisions. Order: observed segment, exit treatment, clip per arm. Raw values, clipped values and provenance
are kept as Amendment 3 stores them. eToro never supplies a return.

### 7. Vendor disagreement: detected and published, not corrected

Step 2's stage B had one top-1,000 name-month (COO, formation 2024-01, Intrader +301.5%) that eToro closes do not
show. Correcting one vendor from another is refused on bias grounds (`SPLIT_CORRECTION_POLICY`; step 1 Amendment 3),
and a disagreement does not say which feed is wrong.

- **Extractor.** One function returns monthly close pairs for any security and month: the last usable bar of each
  calendar month in the latest revision holding both month-ends, `close`, with both bar dates. eToro's side is
  `price_daily.close` with its bar dates.
- **Consistency link.** An eToro instrument with the security's symbol (US listing) is linked when they share at least
  12 pairs with equal start and end bar dates in the 24 months ending at the security's last observed bar or 2026-08,
  whichever is earlier, with median |r_PWB − r_eToro| < 2 pp.
- **Flag.** A linked name-month whose (1 + r_PWB)/(1 + r_eToro) lies outside [0.8, 1.25], step 1's discontinuity band.
- **Treatment.** None beyond the clip. No return, level, ME, state or membership changes.
- **Published:** every flagged name-month with both returns and bar dates; per formation, the count and ME share of
  flagged and of unlinked names. #3740's diagnostics print the book's weight in flagged months.

### 9. Survivorship coverage test (threshold fixed here)

Run once, on the final artefact.

**Events.** Every `termination` record for a U issuer (§3.1), one per (issuer CIK, exchange CIK,
`description_class_security`), collapsing a filing with its amendments within 30 days, with a filing in
2024-09-01 .. 2026-09-30. Events are keyed by CIK, so an issuer the ladder never captured is still an event. A
termination continued by a `succession` is listed as `succeeded` and does not count.

**End interval E:** a stated suspension date S gives [S, S]; else `(a)(3)` gives [filing − 7 days, filing], the
pre-stage lag interval; else `end_unknown`, with E = [filing − 12 formations, filing] for relevance only.

**Relevant formations:** M with s(M) before E's upper edge and no more than 3 formations before E's lower edge; for
`end_unknown`, every formation in E. Formations outside the 24 are not counted.

**Size at M.** w(event, M) = shares(b) × split_product(stamps, b, s(M)) × raw(s(M)), raw from the latest revision
holding a usable bar at s(M) for the security. If no revision does, w(M) is the security's w at its last sized
formation, carried forward, and the row is labelled `carried`. If the security was never sized at a stage-C formation,
the event is `unsized`. The event is **relevant** at M when w ≥ the 1,000th final ME of M's admitted population.

**Unsized events fail the test** unless the issuer's final-ME rank at 2024-07-31 was > K (it entered U only as an
entrant and no document sizes it) — those are listed with their documents.

**Admission axis**, per relevant (event, M): `admitted`, `not_in_R` (no usable bar at s(M) in R(M)), `excluded`
(failed a step-2 to step-6 rule, reason kept), or `below_cut`.

**End axis**, per event admitted at its last relevant formation:

| class | predicate (evaluated in order) | counts |
|---|---|---|
| `end_unknown` | no stated or `(a)(3)` E | yes |
| `post_anchor` | the last observed bar is after E's upper edge | yes |
| `served` | the last observed bar is in [E's lower edge − 5 sessions, E's upper edge] | no |
| `unserved` | the last observed bar is earlier | yes |

**Statistic.** For each formation M,
u(M) = [Σ w over relevant (event, M) with admission `not_in_R` or `excluded`, plus, at each event's last relevant
formation, w of events whose end class counts, plus the final ME of admitted names in M's top 1,000 whose issuer is not
in U and has an unexplained rescale anywhere in stage C] ÷ Σ final ME of M's top 1,000.
Each (security, M) contributes once.

**Pass iff** max over the 24 formations of u(M) ≤ 0.5% **and** their mean ≤ 0.05%, and no event fails as `unsized`.
These are coverage limits fixed by construction, not bounds on a book's error.

**For #3740 (recorded here, implemented there).** Each holding whose state is `terminal` or `coverage_exit` and whose
event counts in u gets two coverage scenarios that override the final clipped value: −100% and 0% for the holding
month, reported beside the source-rule treatment with raw values shown.

**Census published with the artefact:** every event with E and basis, relevant formations, w and how it was computed
(`carried` marked), both axes, the last observed bar and revision; `succeeded` and `unsized` events; per formation,
u(M), the ME share of admitted names outside U, of `detector_triggered` records, and of `basis_unresolved` and
`price_basis_unresolved` names.

**If the test fails,** stage C is not admissible and #3740's confirmation moves to forward paper accrual (#3739
§"If the survivorship test fails").

## Known limits

- **Not sealed against the designer.** The supervisor model's training data run to 2026-06, and the reads in §"Seal"
  happened.
- **One vendor for prices.** Revisions are captures of one Yahoo-derived scrape; agreement between them is
  consistency, not corroboration. eToro is the only independent feed and covers only the names it lists.
- **Event completeness is measured, not assumed:** by the replica's recall (§3.4) and by the detector (§3.5). A split
  the vendor never applied and no screen found is invisible.
- **Coverage is measured on a cohort.** Exits without a Form 25 in the register (paragraph (c) issuer withdrawals,
  which the register excludes) are listed, not tested.
- **Carried sizes** for securities the ladder dropped before their end are an estimate, not a bound; they are labelled.
- **Sparse 2026 captures** can leave a last observed bar weeks before an end; the census records each.
- **Omissions are counted, not recovered.**

## Appendix A — eToro agreement query

On `paperswithbacktest/Stocks-Daily-Price@2026-09-09` (the `a4aead25` capture) against `price_daily`, 2024-09..2026-08:

| population | names | paired months | outside [0.8, 1.25] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| all pairs | 5,252 | 79,675 | 54 |
| ≥ 12 pairs | 3,238 | — | — |
| linked (≥ 12 pairs, median < 2 pp) | 3,227 | 63,782 | 34 rows, 29 names |

Per-name median |Δ| over the ≥ 12-pair population: 11.4 bp (50th), 44.8 bp (90th), 69.4 bp (95th), 1.27 pp (99th).

```sql
with me as (
  select s.instrument_id, date_trunc('month', d.bar_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(d.close order by d.bar_date desc))[1] c, max(d.bar_date) bd
  from research_price_daily d join research_price_series s using (series_id)
  where s.vendor = 'paperswithbacktest/Stocks-Daily-Price@2026-09-09' and s.instrument_id is not null
    and d.bar_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and d.close > 0
  group by 1, 2),
ee as (
  select instrument_id, date_trunc('month', price_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(close order by price_date desc))[1] c, max(price_date) bd
  from price_daily where price_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and close > 0 group by 1, 2),
rp as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from me window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
re as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from ee window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
j as (select a.instrument_id, a.m, a.r rp, b.r re from rp a join re b using (instrument_id, m)
      where a.m0 = (a.m - interval '1 month')::date and b.m0 = a.m0 and a.bd = b.bd and a.bd0 = b.bd0),
per as (select instrument_id, count(*) n, percentile_cont(0.5) within group (order by abs(rp - re)) med
        from j group by 1 having count(*) >= 12),
lk as (select instrument_id from per where med < 0.02),
fl as (select * from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)
         and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25)
select (select count(distinct instrument_id) from j),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25),
       (select count(*) from per),
       (select percentile_cont(array[0.5, 0.9, 0.95, 0.99]) within group (order by med) from per),
       (select count(*) from lk),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)),
       (select count(*) from fl), (select count(distinct instrument_id) from fl);
```
