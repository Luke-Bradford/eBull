# #3739 — stage C: a panel for 2024-09..2026-08 with measured survivorship coverage

Status: spec, round 16, checkpoint 1 closed (§"Checkpoint log"). It replaces the round-1–4 design, which
inferred corporate actions from vendor rescales (§"Why the route changed"). Slice 1 (Form 25 register through
2026-10-08) is done (#3739, 2026-10-09 13:42Z). This spec fixes slices 2–6 before any stage-C cohort is built. No book
is evaluated here; #3740 owns the book, its declaration and its verdict.

**Stage C:** formations M = 2024-08-31 .. 2026-07-31 (24), holding months 2024-09 .. 2026-08.

**What a pass means.** The acceptance test (§9) measures coverage of an enumerated cohort: every register event (a Form 25
removing a common-equity security, under the inherited cohort rules) in the window, plus every admitted security whose
price history ends without one. A pass says that cohort is covered within the limits; it does not prove the panel survivorship-free.

**Parity with stages A and B.** Stage C confirms a book whose stages A and B were built under step 1's rules. Those
rules are inherited unchanged, and this spec does not re-open them: the session calendar from SPY bars, the share
checks and their tolerances, the termination classifier and its treatments, the Form 25 register's cohort rules,
Amendment 3's clip, the test-issue exclusion and the universe steps 3–6. A defect in one of them is a defect in stages
A and B too and is ticketed against step 1, not patched for stage C alone. What is new here is the replacement of
Intrader (prices, raw basis, split stamps, series ends) by the ladder and the event file, and the coverage test.

## Why the route changed

Rounds 1–4 reconstructed every split, ticker change and termination from rescales between revisions of one vendor's
dataset. Checkpoint 1 returned 55, 47, 53 and about 50 findings, most of round 4's blockers labelled FRAMING. The
inference has an unbounded edge-case surface, so the model was wrong, not the patches.

This design keeps the PWB revision history as the **price** source only. Corporate actions come from an **adjudicated
event file**, built from SEC documents over a bounded issuer set by a protocol that reads documents and never prices.
Vendor rescales are used only as a **detector**: a rescale the file does not explain removes the affected ME, it never
supplies a factor. The precedent is step 1 slice 3d's adjudicated CSV (`docs/research/3609-slice-3d-iv-adjudicated.csv`):
a cause per row, cited to a filing.

## Pre-stage measurement: Form 25 filing date against the archive's last bar

On Intrader series linked to a Form 25 (`delisting_source = 'sec_form25'`) whose last bar precedes 2024-08-01,
filing date minus **archive last bar**, in calendar days:

| provision | series | filed before last bar | 0–7 days | 8–31 | > 31 | median / p90 / p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `(a)(3)` | 682 | 60 | 618 | 1 | 3 | 1 / 3 / 4 |
| `(b)` | 194 | 4 | 22 | 88 | 80 | 23 / 76 / 91 |

```sql
select delisting_provision, count(*),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar < 0),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar between 0 and 7),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar between 8 and 31),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar > 31),
       percentile_cont(array[0.5, 0.9, 0.95]) within group (order by delisting_filed_date - last_bar)
from research_price_series
where vendor = 'icyDenev/Intrader' and delisting_source = 'sec_form25' and last_bar < '2024-08-01'
group by 1;
```

This is an archive-endpoint lag distribution, conditional on a successful link; it does not observe economic ends.
For `(b)` the filing trails the last bar by weeks, so it is not an end estimate. `sec-edgar.md` §2.6 trap 5 holds: the
filing date is a different event from the suspension.

## Contract

### 1. The price ladder (`pwb-revision-ladder-v2`)

- **Scan.** The data revisions from `8936ae04` (2024-07-07) to `a4aead25` (2026-10-01), ordered by commit timestamp,
  ties by the HF API's listing order. The ordered index (ordinal, commit id, timestamp, shard oids) is pinned. A read
  refuses if an oid differs.
- **Sessions.** As stages A and B: the dates with a usable SPY bar, here in `a4aead25`, with the NYSE-holiday guard
  above. session(t) is the last session on or before t; s(M) = session(M).
- **Formation revision.** R(M) = the earliest revision holding a usable SPY bar at s(M). The 24 R(M) and `a4aead25`
  are mirrored whole under `var/research_corpus/mirrors/pwb-ladder/<commit>/`.
- **Evidence prefix.** Membership and characteristics at M read only revisions with ordinal ≤ that of R(M), only
  detector output computed on those revisions, and only identity records whose evidence was accepted on or before
  s(M) under step 1's acceptance rule (`acceptance_ny_date`). Four record types and one candidate class are exempt, for stated reasons: a
  `split` or `symbol_change` effective in (s(M), commit of R(M)] is used only to undo the vendor's re-keying and
  re-scaling of R(M) back to the symbol and raw close that were public at s(M); a split effective on or before s(M)
  had traded by s(M); a `termination_end` whose stated end of exchange trading is on or before s(M), and a `first_trade`
  whose date is after s(M), record facts public on s(M), whenever the filing documenting them was accepted; and a split
  candidate accepted by the commit of R(M) is used only to judge whether R(M)'s close and the share count's basis can
  be unwound (§5). Formation M reads each candidate's outcome as of the commit of R(M): the latest outcome whose
  evidence was all accepted by then, else unadjudicated. Such a candidate can exclude a name at M (never admit one)
  on information accepted between s(M) and the commit of R(M), the horizon R(M) itself already carries. No record can admit or exclude a name by information dated after s(M); the candidate exemption below is the one
  stated exception. A test asserts that appending
  later revisions, detector output or identity records, other than those exempt types, leaves every formation's
  membership and ME unchanged.
- **Read key.** §3.1 gives each security a symbol per date. In revision r, a security's rows are read under its symbol
  at r's commit date; if that symbol is absent from r, under its previous symbols, latest first. Rows dated before an adjudicated
  symbol change are used only if they pass the continuity test of §3.5 against an earlier revision read under the
  earlier symbol; otherwise that revision's rows before the change are not used. A row dated t is used only if the
  #3361 linkage does not tie the read symbol at t to a different CIK (ticker reuse). Absence of a conflicting link is
  the authentication standard stages A and B used for Intrader rows; it is not positive proof of identity.
- **Decoding boundary.** Before the declaration, a revision committed after 2024-08-01 is decoded only in row groups
  whose `date` footer maximum is before 2024-08-01; a row group that straddles the boundary is not decoded, and the
  count of bars skipped that way is published. Footer statistics are read first, as the seal permits.
- **Remote reads.** Other revisions are read by symbol- or date-filtered query. Each result is written to an extract
  file whose sha256 is recorded with the commit, filter and row count, so every decision replays offline.
- **Price bound.** No price, volume or return dated after 2026-08-31 leaves the reader. The reader may report that a
  security has a usable bar after the bound (presence, for §6), never its values.
- **Bar validity.** The #2261 bar rules (`price-quarantine` rule set, version pinned) are applied per revision to every
  bar a rule reads. Only usable bars enter a statistic, a level, a return or a presence test.
- **Licence posture** is unchanged from #3619 slice 3: research use, local mirror, no redistribution.

### 2. The adjudicated issuer set U

U is the set of issuers (CIK) whose events are adjudicated. It is built in two passes; neither reads a stage-C price
before the declaration. The split screens of §3.2, which are code over structured data, run over **every** issuer in
the Financial Statement Data Sets and the insider data sets, not only U; adjudication is what U bounds.

**Pass 1, before the declaration** (documents and pre-stage data only). For a base formation F (here 2024-07-31):
1. **Incumbents:** issuers whose largest admitted security at F has final-ME rank ≤ K among all admitted securities.
2. **Entrants:** issuers with no admitted security at F that file a Form 8-A12B, 10-12B or 8-K12B, or an 8-K listing
   Item 5.06, accepted in (F − 92 days, F + 24 months].
3. **Terminated issuers:** every issuer CIK on a §9 register event, whatever its size.
A successor CIK (a holdco or redomiciled issuer) enters U through closure when its security ranks, or as a terminated
issuer when it has a register event. Each pass-2 iteration's candidate list is pinned as its own version; U only grows
and is finite, so pass 2 stops.

**K, by construction on pre-stage data.** For each formation pair (M₀, M₀ + h) with M₀ in stage B, 1 ≤ h ≤ 24 and
M₀ + h ≤ 2024-07-31 (both endpoints pre-stage), the share of the top 1,000's final ME at M₀ + h held by issuers present
at M₀ and ranked > K there (issuer rank as in item 1, no re-ranking; issuers absent at M₀ are excluded from both
sides). K is the smallest of {1,500, 2,000, 2,500, 3,000} with that share ≤ 0.25% at every pair; else 3,000, with the
measured share published. K bounds pass-1 effort, not correctness.

**Pass 2, after the declaration.** Run in this order, each to a fixed point:
- **Register refresh:** issuers on register events harvested after pass 1 join U and are adjudicated.
- **Closure:** the build ranks, at each formation, every security priced in R(M) and linked at s(M), before the $5
  rule and before any ME-missing reason applies, on an **upper-estimated** ME: ME_upper = the largest of the
  security's candidate share counts at s(M) × close_R(s(M)) × Π max(q, 1/q) over the issuer's split candidates (§3.2)
  not yet adjudicated in (b, commit of R(M)] and over its unexplained rescales there. q is the candidate's indicated
  ratio (the XBRL value in unit `pure`, or the cover-count ratio; orientation irrelevant under max(q, 1/q)); a candidate
  with no indicated ratio, or one that is not a finite positive number, takes the largest max(q, 1/q) of any confirmed
  split in the replica and stage-C files. A security with no candidate share count at all puts its issuer into U
  directly. That default
  is the largest observed, not a bound. Any issuer of a security with ME_upper rank ≤ 1,200 at any formation that is not in U joins U, is
  adjudicated under §3.3 (the adjudicator receives the CIK, not a price or rank), and the formations are rebuilt.
  Additions are labelled `closure` and counted per formation. The build asserts that no security ranked ≤ 1,000 has an
  issuer outside U.

What closure cannot see is a split that every full-population screen missed. Nothing here measures how many of those
stage C has. §3.4's replica recall is published beside the closure counts as context about the screens on earlier
data, not as a stage-C miss rate.

### 3. The event file (`stage-c-event-file-v1`)

#### 3.1 Records

One CSV per record type under `docs/research/3739-event-file/`. Every row carries: CIK; the class (title of class as
in the cover's Section 12(b) table, with its trading symbol on the evidence date); one to three evidence items, each an
accession number, document file name and a verbatim quote of at most 300 characters; the SEC acceptance timestamp of
each; and a version (1 unless superseded, §3.3). Splits effective 2024-07-01 .. 2026-10-01, the commit of `a4aead25`,
the last revision any reader opens, so no raw-price unwind, detector pair, continuity test or bridge can depend on a
later split (Amendment 1); symbol changes effective 2024-07-01 .. the build cutoff (§"Manifest"); a
`termination_end` row for every §9 register event, whatever the date.

| type | key | fields |
|---|---|---|
| `split` | (CIK, title of class, effective date) | ratio = new shares of the same class per old share; reverse splits < 1 |
| `symbol_change` | (CIK, title of class, effective date) | old symbol, new symbol |
| `first_trade` | (CIK, title of class) | the class's first exchange session (its inception, not a relisting), as a filing states it; adjudicated for every pass-1 entrant and every §9 event whose filings state one |
| `termination_end` | the register's event key (§9) | consideration as stated (`successor_shares` for a holdco reorganisation or redomicile; `cash`; `other_issuer_securities`, meaning securities of an issuer that is not such a successor; `mixed`; or `not_stated`); and the last exchange session, only as a filing states it (a stated last trading day, or a statement that trading is suspended before the open of a stated date, whose previous session is then the last); else a stated merger closing date, because the shares cease to exist then and cannot trade after it; or `not_stated`. §9's five-session window absorbs whether the closing day itself traded |

A distribution of another issuer's or another class's securities is not a split and writes no record. Its price
effect reaches returns only through the vendor's `adj_close`, as in stages A and B through Intrader's.

Baseline identity is the #3361 linkage; there is no baseline identity record. `symbol_change` records adjudicate only
the changes the linkage's evidence shows.

**Deduplication.** One row per (key, version). Several documents describing one action are evidence items on one row.
The build reads the highest version of each key; a repeated (key, version) is a build error. Every version of a key
must belong to one action's supersession chain (§3.3): version 1 supersedes nothing, and each later version names
the version it supersedes; a second
distinct action at the same key, whatever its version, is a build error (Amendment 1).

Split stamps for the panel: Intrader's for effective dates ≤ 2024-07-31 (as stages A and B), the event file's for
effective dates ≥ 2024-08-01. July 2024 event-file splits are compared with Intrader's stamps (§3.4) and are not stamps.

#### 3.2 Candidate screens

Screens propose; only adjudication writes a record. Each screen is code with tests, run over every issuer in the source
data, and its candidate list is pinned before adjudication starts. A screen's value (an XBRL ratio, a count ratio) is
an indication, never a record field.

- **split:** an XBRL `StockholdersEquityNoteStockSplitConversionRatio1` fact accepted in the window or its evidence
  horizon (below), whatever its
  context date; per class (`us-gaap:StatementClassOfStockAxis` member, spelled `ClassOfStock` in the data sets'
  `segments`, or none), consecutive cover counts
  (`dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding`) of consecutive 10-Q and 10-K filings, an amendment replacing its original,
  whose ratio is ≤ 0.8 or ≥ 1.25 (Amendment 1 closes the band's ends, so a 5-for-4 split's exact 1.25 is raised; within a filing, the class member's fact with the latest context date); any 8-K
  whose `items` include 5.03; and (Amendment 1) a positive value of a stock-dividend element (§"Source rules") in a
  10-Q or 10-K, under the default dimension or any member, accepted by the end of the evidence horizon, whose
  interval (below) meets the window (so the notes read reach back to window start − 92 days). Each candidate carries an interval: the two covers' dates for a count jump; [context end −
  1 year, acceptance] for a ratio fact; [8-K acceptance, + 92 days] for Item 5.03; [context start, acceptance + 92 days]
  for a stock-dividend fact, because a dividend recognised in one period can be distributed after it or after the
  filing, as Item 5.03's span allows for an amendment.

#### 3.3 Adjudication protocol

- **Inputs:** the candidate and SEC filings of that CIK, of a named counterparty CIK, and of the exchange CIK on the
  Form 25. Nothing outside EDGAR. No market-data source is opened.
- **Document lifecycle:** the record follows the latest-accepted filing that states the action's terms, whenever it was
  accepted; a filing that amends, corrects, postpones or cancels an earlier one supersedes it. `contested` is reserved
  for two filings that each state the completed action and disagree; both are recorded. A supersession found after
  the pass-1 freeze is a new version of the key; the earlier version stays in the file. A cancellation is a version
  with status `cancelled`, which the build reads as no action. A correction that moves the effective date cancels the
  old key and writes the new one.
- **Outcomes:** `confirmed`, `rejected` (reason: issuance, buyback, other class, repeated disclosure of an earlier
  action, withdrawn), `contested`, or `no_document`. A `contested` or `no_document` split candidate keeps its screen
  interval.
- **Checker, deterministic, per record type:** every evidence item's document is fetched by accession, its bytes
  mirrored with sha256, and the quote must occur in it (whitespace-normalised). Then: a `split` needs, in its quotes,
  the ratio (as a-for-b, a:b, a percentage stock dividend p%, ratio 1 + p/100, or x additional shares per share held,
  ratio 1 + x; Amendment 1), the effective date or the record and payable dates, and the class's title or symbol (or the cover lists a
  single class); a `symbol_change` needs both symbols and the date; a `termination_end` needs the date and either the words that make
  it the last exchange session or the words stating the merger's completion on that date; a `first_trade` needs the
  date and the words that make it the first exchange session. A row that fails is not admitted: a split candidate's
  outcome becomes `no_document`, and a register event's `termination_end` row is written as `not_stated` with the
  failed evidence logged. The checker proves the quotes exist; the adjudication log records why they describe one completed action.
- **Who:** an agent session or a person; the protocol is the same. Every document opened is logged (§3.6).

#### 3.4 Pre-stage measurement of split-screen recall

Before stage-C adjudication, the screens and protocol run on a replica: §2's pass 1 items 1–2 with base formation
F = 2022-07-31, events effective 2022-08-01 .. 2024-07-31. The comparison set is Intrader's `split_factor` stamps on

  within 0.5%, effective date equal to the stamp date or the next session.
- **Recall:** matched ÷ stamps (Amendment 1: also each `effective_basis`'s matched count and ME weight over the same
  total denominator, beside the unmatched total), by count and weighted by final ME at the formation before the stamp (stamps on
  securities with no final ME there weigh at the formation's 1,000th ME).

  confirmations unmatched fails the replica.
- **Unmatched stamps (Amendment 1): listed, never excluded.** Every comparison stamp stays in both denominators. Each
  unmatched stamp is read under §3.3's inputs, given (CIK, the class the linkage's symbol names at the stamp date,

  revised once and the replica reruns with F = 2020-07-31, events 2020-08-01 .. 2022-07-31. A second fail stops the
  route: stage C is not buildable by this protocol, and #3740 moves to forward paper accrual.
- **What the count bar can detect (Amendment 1).** The bar allows ⌊0.03 n⌋ misses among n stamps. An illustrative
  binomial table, assuming independent misses at a common true recall p (real misses cluster by issuer and

#### 3.5 The rescale detector (after the declaration)

**Convention.** The vendor's `close` is split-adjusted to the revision (pilot observations; tested on the full
pre-stage history by §5's unwinding validation). So a split effective on or before a revision's commit is taken as
applied in that revision unless the detector shows otherwise.

For a security and two revisions r₁ < r₂ that are consecutive among the revisions holding it under its read key: on the
usable sessions both hold in the 21 sessions ending at r₁'s last bar, ρ(t) = close_{r₁}(t) / close_{r₂}(t). At least 5
shared sessions are required; fewer makes the pair `untestable`.
- **No rescale:** every ρ(t) within 0.5% of 1.
- **Rescale ρ:** every ρ(t) within 0.5% of their median ρ ≠ 1.
- **Inconsistent:** neither. An inconsistent pair can still be explained below (a late-applied split is piecewise);
  otherwise the vendor changed history non-uniformly.

**Application state.** Each split is `pending` in revisions committed before its effective date and, for a security
first held by a revision committed after it, `applied` there (the convention). Walking a security's pairs in order, for a subset S of
the splits pending in r₁ and effective on or before the commit of r₂, let P_S(t) = the product of the ratios of the
splits in S effective after t. The pair is **explained by S** when ρ(t) equals P_S(t) within 0.5% at every shared
session t (this covers a vendor applying a split late, when the window holds sessions on both sides of it, and applying
only some pending splits). Exactly one subset must explain the pair (the empty subset explains a no-rescale pair); its
splits become `applied` from r₂ on and the rest stay `pending`, so a later pair can still explain them. Two explaining
subsets, or none, leave the pair unexplained. Any other pair
is unexplained; an unexplained pair, rescale or inconsistent, becomes a candidate under §3.3, handed over as (CIK, class, the two commit
dates) with no ratio and no price, and records confirmed this way are labelled `detector_triggered`. A detector-triggered candidate's interval is (r₁'s last bar,
commit of r₂], and its acceptance date, for §5's formation gate, is the commit of r₂. A security held by
only one revision has no pair and rests on the convention; it is counted as `single_capture`. The continuity test for
symbol changes (§1) is this detector across the two read keys.

### 5. Raw price and ME

**Raw price.** For a security, a revision R and a session t ≤ R's last bar for it:
raw_R(t) = close_R(t) × Π ratio over its splits effective in (t, commit of R] that are `applied` in R (§3.5). A split
still `pending` in R is not multiplied in: the vendor has not adjusted for it, so the close is already raw for it.
raw_R(t) is **unresolved** when, on that security over (t, commit of R]: a detector pair is unexplained (including an unexplained
inconsistent pair) or untestable; or a `contested`, `no_document` or unadjudicated split candidate interval overlaps. The share basis is
**unresolved** when a `contested`, `no_document` or unadjudicated split candidate interval overlaps (b, s(M)]. At
formation M, only candidates whose triggering filing was accepted on or before the commit of R(M) are considered, the
horizon of R(M) itself; a confirmed `split` record is exempt as §1 states. The append test covers candidate lists and
their outcomes.

**ME.** With R = R(M), step 1's formula: ME(M) = shares(b) × split_product(stamps, b, s(M)) × raw_R(s(M)). An
unresolved raw_R(s(M)) or share basis makes ME missing, reason `basis_unresolved`. Checks 1–4 run unchanged on these stamps; their
outcomes keep step 1's reasons (for example a split between a cover's date and its filing fails check 1, as it does in
stages A and B).

**$5 exclusion.** The price is raw_R(s(M)); unresolved is excluded as `price_basis_unresolved`.

**Volume basis (check 3), on pre-stage data.** For each Intrader stamp dated 2024-07-08 .. 2024-07-31 on a linked
security, compare, on the 20 sessions before the stamp, `close × volume` in the first revision committed after the
stamp with Intrader's raw `close × volume`. If the vendor adjusts volume with price, the two agree; if it adjusts only
price, they differ by the ratio. A security votes for a convention when at least 95% of its sessions fit it within 1%.
The convention is established if at least 5 securities qualify and all vote for it; a convention that adjusts only price
is applied as a correction. Otherwise check 3 is `untested` for every row whose security has a split effective in
(start of the 63-session window, commit of R(M)], with step 1's treatment of an untested check 3 (it blocks
verification and reference eligibility for those rows, as Amendment 2.1 already does when check 3 cannot run).

**Unwinding validation (pre-stage).** In revision `8936ae04` (committed 2024-07-07), for every security linked to
exactly one Intrader series by CIK and every session t ≤ 2024-07-05, compare Intrader's raw close with
close × Π Intrader stamps in (t, 2024-07-07]. This tests the §3.5 convention and the unwinding operation over the full
pre-stage history. Required: at least 500 distinct securities with a stamp in the compared span; at most 1% of
compared (security, t) pairs outside 0.5%; and at most 1% of securities with more than 5% of their own pairs outside
0.5%. Each limit is reported for pairs with and without a stamp after t. A fail makes stage C inadmissible. Runs before
the declaration.

**Cap.** Per formation, the upper-estimated ME of securities missing ME for `basis_unresolved` or excluded as
`price_basis_unresolved` is at most 0.5% of the top 1,000's ME, or the build refuses. A security with no candidate
count has no ME under step 1 whatever its price basis; its exclusion keeps step 1's no-shares reason and is reported
there, not in this cap. The estimate is the largest of
the security's candidate counts at s(M) (selected, cover, balance sheet), each first moved to s(M) by the stamps, ×
raw_R(s(M)) computed from the applied splits regardless of unresolved status × Π max(ρ, 1/ρ) over its unexplained
rescales in (b, commit of R] (for an unexplained inconsistent pair, the largest max(ρ(t), 1/ρ(t)) over its shared
sessions) × Π max(q, 1/q) over its split candidates there that are unadjudicated, `contested` or
`no_document` (§2's q) × Π max(ratio, 1/ratio) over its confirmed splits there that are `pending` in R or whose
application an `untestable` pair left undetermined.
Share and count are published per formation beside step 1's other ME-missing reasons, whose shares are compared with
stage B's.

### 6. Holdings: one state per holding

Weights are fixed at s(M). The holding month is m = M + 1, valued over (s(M), s(m)]. The security's **last observed
bar** L is its latest usable bar in any revision, capped at the stated `termination_end` of any **ending** event: every
register event except an `(a)(3)` event with consideration `successor_shares`, which continues and caps nothing. A cap is
permanent within stage C. An `(a)(3)` event whose consideration adjudication cannot find stated is treated as ending
by construction (no source rule decides the undocumented case): if the security in fact continued, it is realised at
its last close by the inherited `(a)(3)` treatment and then leaves the panel, and the census lists it with its ME as
`a3_not_stated`. A class that relists after an ending event is likewise not readmitted, and is listed as `relisted`. **Alive after m** means a usable bar
after s(m) in some revision (presence only beyond the price bound), not after a cap. d is the latest usable bar in (s(M), s(m)], or s(M) if there is none.

**Continuing reorganisations,** as in stages A and B, need no record: if the security's read-key series continues
(across a symbol change by the continuity test of §1), it stays alive and is observed; if it ends, it is terminal. A
successor with a new series enters the panel at the next formation its rules admit it.

**The event that explains an end.** Only ending events explain an end. The event associated with a security's end L is
the ending event with the earliest end date (for `end_unknown`, filing date) on or after the session 5 sessions before
L, provided the security has no usable bar between L and that date. If other ending events end within 5 sessions of
that one, a failure provision (`(b)`, `(a)(4)`, unparsed) is preferred over `(a)(3)`, the more severe treatment; then
the later filing. This is structural, not a lag window: an event belongs to the trading episode it follows. With none,
the classifier gets no register event.

States, in order:

1. **`terminal`** — not alive after m (so L ≤ s(m)). Return from s(M) to L (zero if L ≤ s(M)), then
   `classify_termination` on the register event associated with L, if any (linked, provision) and the `Q` suffix of the last read key, derived by `research_corpus_ingest.archive_symbol_candidates`'s rule
   (trailing `Q` on a symbol of more than one letter), with
   `terminal_value_fraction` per arm. With no register event the classifier returns `UNKNOWN` (two-armed) or
   `Q_SUFFIX_OTC`, as for stages A and B. Which holding month carries a termination whose last bar falls on a formation session, and whether the
   security can be admitted at that formation, follow step 1 §"Returns and holdings" unchanged, with L in place of
   Intrader's last bar.
2. **`observed`** — a usable bar at s(m). Return to s(m).
3. **`observed`, early end bar** — alive after m and d ≥ the session 5 sessions before s(m). Return to d.
4. **`coverage_exit`** — alive after m otherwise. Return to d (zero if d = s(M)), then cash at 0, as step 1.

Exclusive and total: a security is either alive after m or not; if alive, d either equals s(m), lies within 5 sessions
of it, or does not.

**Return arithmetic.** The return uses `adj_close` within one revision, as stages A and B use it within the one
Intrader capture: the latest revision holding both endpoint bars under the read key, because a later capture can carry
distributions posted after an earlier one (#3619 slice 3). A security is labelled `history_revised` for the month when a detector pair spanning
it is `inconsistent`, or when the same test run on `adj_close` instead of `close` finds the pair neither uniform nor
explained. If no revision holds both endpoints, the path is a valid chain of revisions: each link joins two revisions at a shared
usable session inside the holding interval, join sessions increase along the chain, and each pair is explained or
shows no rescale. Among valid chains: fewest links, then later revisions, then for each join from the first the
latest session that still lets the chain complete. With no valid chain, the return runs to the furthest bar any valid chain reaches,
labelled `bridge_break`. Order: observed segment, exit treatment, clip per arm. Raw values, clipped values and
provenance are kept as Amendment 3 stores them. eToro never supplies a return.

- **Unregistered exits:** every security admitted at any stage-C formation whose uncapped last bar in any revision
  falls on or before 2026-08-31, that holds no usable bar dated after 2026-08-31 in `a4aead25`, and that has no
  register event associated with its last bar (§6). These come from the
  ladder and need no filing.

**End date.** The `termination_end` record's stated date; for an unregistered exit, its last bar. A register event with
`not_stated` is `end_unknown`.

**Scope.** A register event is in scope when its end date is after 2024-08-31 (for `end_unknown`, when its filing
date or its uncapped last bar is after 2024-08-31),
unless a filing states that its security first traded after 2026-08-31. Missing evidence keeps an event in scope.
Every unregistered exit is in scope (its security was admitted in stage C). Events out of scope are listed and do not
count.

**Size at M, an upper estimate.** w(event, M) = shares × raw(s(M)): shares is the largest of the security's candidate
counts at s(M) (selected, cover, balance sheet; with none, the latest count of any of these accepted on or before
s(M)), moved to s(M) by the stamps; raw is from the latest revision holding a usable bar at s(M), or R(M); and w is
multiplied by the same factors as §5's cap (unexplained rescales, unresolved split candidates). Where no
revision holds one, raw is unresolved, or no count exists yet, w(M) = w at the security's nearest sized formation
(earlier or later; on a tie, the larger w) × G(gap), where G(g) is the 99.9th percentile of max(r, 1/r) for the final-ME ratio r over g
formations among all admitted securities of stages A and B, including each terminated security's ME at its last
formation (pre-stage, computed in slice 2 and pinned). No formation before a `first_trade` record's date (§3.1) has a
size or relevance; a first bar in the ladder is not an inception date. An in-scope event sized at no date from
2024-07-31 on is `unsized`.

**Relevant formations:** every stage-C M with s(M) on or before the event's end date (for `end_unknown`, the later of
its filing date and its uncapped last bar)
and w(event, M) ≥ the 1,000th final ME of M's admitted population.

**Admission axis**, per relevant (event, M): `admitted` (in M's top 1,000), `not_in_R` (no usable bar at s(M) in
R(M)), `excluded` (failed a step-2 to step-6 rule, reason kept), or `below_cut` (admitted outside the top 1,000 by
final ME although w ≥ the cut: the estimate and the panel disagree). Every value except `admitted` counts.

**End axis**, per event whose security was admitted at any relevant formation, unless it is `post_stage`. An event is
`post_stage` when both hold: its end date is after 2026-08-31 (for `end_unknown`: its filing date is more than 91 days
after 2026-08-31, the pre-stage `(b)` lag p95); and its uncapped last bar is no
earlier than 5 sessions before 2026-08-31, so the ladder covered it through the stage. A `post_stage` event is charged
on the admission axis only. "Last bar" here is
uncapped.

| class | predicate (evaluated in order) | counts |
|---|---|---|
| `continuing_a3` | provision `(a)(3)` with consideration `successor_shares`. It is not an end: only its end-axis charge is waived, its admission-axis charges stand, and the security's eventual end is judged on its own (an associated ending event, or an unregistered exit) | no |
| `end_unknown` | `not_stated` | yes |
| `unregistered` | an unregistered exit | yes |
| `post_anchor` | for a provision other than `(a)(3)`, the last bar is after the end date; for an ending `(a)(3)`, it is more than 5 sessions after the end date (bars after an end are not exchange trading) | yes |
| `served` | the last bar is in [end date − 5 sessions, end date], or for `(a)(3)` in [end date − 5 sessions, end date + 5 sessions] | no |
| `unserved` | the last bar is earlier | yes |

**Statistic.** For each formation M,
u(M) = [Σ w(event, M) over relevant (event, M) whose admission axis counts, plus, at each event's last relevant
formation, w of events whose end class counts] ÷ Σ final ME of M's top 1,000. Each (security, M) contributes once.

Every formation must have at least 1,000 admitted securities with finite final ME, or the build refuses.

**Pass iff** no in-scope event is `unsized`, max over the 24 formations of u(M) ≤ 0.5%, and their mean ≤ 0.05%. These
are coverage limits fixed by construction, not bounds on a book's error. They permit an omission of up to 0.5% of the
top 1,000's ME in a formation; the census lists every counted event by name, so #3740 sees each one.

**For #3740 (recorded here, implemented there).** Every holding in state `terminal` with a two-armed or counted class,
`coverage_exit`, `observed` with an early end bar, `bridge_break` or `history_revised` gets two coverage scenarios that
override the final clipped value for the holding month: −100% and 0%. They are scenarios reported beside the
source-rule treatment with raw values shown, not bounds. Securities never admitted have no holding; u is their only
measure.

**Census published with the artefact:** every event with its end date and evidence, scope, relevant formations, w and
how it was computed (G-scaled values marked), both axes, the last bar and revision; per formation, u(M), the counts and
ME shares of `closure` issuers, `detector_triggered` records, `single_capture` securities, `basis_unresolved`,
`price_basis_unresolved`, `bridge_break` and `history_revised` holdings, and `relisted` and `a3_not_stated` securities; and §3.4's measured screen miss rate.

**If the test fails,** stage C is not admissible and #3740's confirmation moves to forward paper accrual (#3739
§"If the survivorship test fails").

## Known limits

- **Not sealed against the designer.** The supervisor model's training data run to 2026-06, and the reads in §"Seal"
  happened, including document prices the adjudication reads.
- **One vendor for prices.** Revisions are captures of one Yahoo-derived scrape; agreement between them is
  consistency, not corroboration. eToro is the only independent feed and covers only the names it lists (§7). The
  vendor's adjustment conventions (§3.5, §5) are validated on pre-stage data and extrapolated to stage C, to later
  revisions and to first captures.
- **Split completeness** is measured against one independent vendor on pre-stage replicas (§3.4). After the
  declaration the detector checks only splits the vendor applied between two captures of a security; a security held
  by one revision rests on the vendor convention (`single_capture`, counted).
- **G-scaled sizes** are a high-percentile estimate from stages A and B, not a bound. G is computed on securities with
  sizes at both ends (terminated ones at their last formation), so it is conditioned on observability.
- **Exits with no register event and no admission** (a paragraph (c) withdrawal, which the inherited register
  excludes, by a security never admitted) are outside both event sources.
- **Stage C waits for its evidence** (Amendment 1): the build cutoff is no earlier than 2027-04-22. An action whose
  only screening evidence is filed after the 203-day horizon can stay missed, in the replica and in stage C.
- **Constructed (b)(1) dates** (`fallback_b1`, counted) can be wrong silently: raw price on the sessions between
  the constructed and the actual date is off by the distribution's ratio when no detector pair separates them.
- **A sub-tolerance distribution** (under 0.5%) that no screen raises passes the detector as no rescale; several
  compound into a share-basis drift that nothing reports.
- **A reverse consolidation between 0.8 and 1** escapes the cover screen; Item 5.03 and ratio facts must raise it.
- **Missed splits:** a split every full-population screen missed is invisible to closure and to share counts. Nothing
  measures how many stage C has; §3.4's replica recall describes the screens on earlier data only.
- **End dates from filings:** where filings date only a merger's completion, OTC trading between an undated
  suspension and the completion can fall inside §9's five-session window.
- **Late `not_stated` filings:** an `end_unknown` event filed more than 91 days after the stage (the pre-stage `(b)`
  lag p95) whose ladder series runs through the stage is `post_stage`; if those bars are OTC prints after an undated
  suspension, the holding is valued on them.
- **Relistings and undocumented `(a)(3)` consideration:** a class that relists after an ending event, and an
  `(a)(3)` security whose consideration no filing states but which continued, leave the panel; both are listed with
  their ME (`relisted`, `a3_not_stated`).
- **Accepted precision limits:** a class whose title changes is tracked by its symbol through the linkage, not by a
  lifecycle identifier; screen intervals for XBRL facts and Item 5.03 are by construction and can miss an action's date
  (the detector and the cap carry it only when a pair spans it and is unexplained); offsetting splits that no pair separates leave their individual application states
  unidentified; a raw price dated between them is then unresolved only if a pair is unexplained, so it can be wrong
  by the later split's ratio.
- **Path integrity** is tested at endpoints and bridges, not at every session.
- **Inherited rules** (§"Parity") carry whatever limits they carry in stages A and B.

with me as (
  select s.instrument_id, date_trunc('month', d.bar_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(d.close order by d.bar_date desc))[1] c, max(d.bar_date) bd
  from research_price_daily d join research_price_series s using (series_id)
  where s.vendor = 'paperswithbacktest/Stocks-Daily-Price@2026-09-09' and s.instrument_id is not null
    and d.bar_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and d.close > 0
  group by 1, 2),
ee as (
  select instrument_id, date_trunc('month', price_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(close order by price_date desc))[1] c, max(price_date) bd
  from price_daily where price_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and close > 0 group by 1, 2),
rp as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from me window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
re as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from ee window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
j as (select a.instrument_id, a.m, a.r rp, b.r re from rp a join re b using (instrument_id, m)
      where a.m0 = (a.m - interval '1 month')::date and b.m0 = a.m0 and a.bd = b.bd and a.bd0 = b.bd0),
per as (select instrument_id, count(*) n, percentile_cont(0.5) within group (order by abs(rp - re)) med
        from j group by 1 having count(*) >= 12),
lk as (select instrument_id from per where med < 0.02),
fl as (select * from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)
         and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25)
select (select count(distinct instrument_id) from j),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25),
       (select count(*) from per),
       (select percentile_cont(array[0.5, 0.9, 0.95, 0.99]) within group (order by med) from per),
       (select count(*) from lk),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)),
       (select count(*) from fl), (select count(distinct instrument_id) from fl);
