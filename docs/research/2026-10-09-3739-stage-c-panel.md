# #3739 — stage C: a panel for 2024-09..2026-08 with measured survivorship coverage

Status: spec, round 6 (checkpoint 1 round 5 applied, §"Checkpoint log"). It replaces the round-1–4 design, which
inferred corporate actions from vendor rescales (§"Why the route changed"). Slice 1 (Form 25 register through
2026-10-08) is done (#3739, 2026-10-09 13:42Z). This spec fixes slices 2–6 before any stage-C cohort is built. No book
is evaluated here; #3740 owns the book, its declaration and its verdict.

**Stage C:** formations M = 2024-08-31 .. 2026-07-31 (24), holding months 2024-09 .. 2026-08.

**What a pass means.** The acceptance test (§9) measures coverage of an enumerated cohort: every SEC Form 25 filed for
a common-equity security, or filed by its issuer, in the window, plus every admitted security whose price history ends
without one. A pass says that cohort is covered within the limits; it does not prove the panel survivorship-free.

**Parity with stages A and B.** Stage C confirms a book whose stages A and B were built under step 1's rules. Those
rules are inherited unchanged, and this spec does not re-open them: the session calendar from SPY bars, the share
checks and their tolerances, the termination classifier and its treatments, the Form 25 register's cohort rules,
Amendment 3's clip, the test-issue exclusion and the universe steps 3–6. A defect in one of them is a defect in stages
A and B too and is ticketed against step 1, not patched for stage C alone. What is new here is the replacement of
Intrader (prices, raw basis, split stamps, series ends) by the ladder and the event file, and the coverage test.

## Why the route changed

Rounds 1–4 reconstructed every split, ticker change and termination from rescales between revisions of one vendor's
dataset. Checkpoint 1 returned 55, 47, 53 and about 50 findings, most of round 4's blockers labelled FRAMING. The
inference has an unbounded edge-case surface, so the model was wrong, not the patches.

This design keeps the PWB revision history as the **price** source only. Corporate actions come from an **adjudicated
event file**, built from SEC documents over a bounded issuer set by a protocol that reads documents and never prices.
Vendor rescales are used only as a **detector**: a rescale the file does not explain removes the affected ME, it never
supplies a factor. The precedent is step 1 slice 3d's adjudicated CSV (`docs/research/3609-slice-3d-iv-adjudicated.csv`):
a cause per row, cited to a filing.

## Contract

### 1. The price ladder (`pwb-revision-ladder-v2`)

- **Scan.** The data revisions from `8936ae04` (2024-07-07) to `a4aead25` (2026-10-01), ordered by commit timestamp,
  ties by the HF API's listing order. The ordered index (ordinal, commit id, timestamp, shard oids) is pinned. A read
  refuses if an oid differs.
- **Sessions.** As stages A and B: the dates with a usable SPY bar, here in `a4aead25`, with the NYSE-holiday guard
  above. session(t) is the last session on or before t; s(M) = session(M).
- **Formation revision.** R(M) = the earliest revision holding a usable SPY bar at s(M). The 24 R(M) and `a4aead25`
  are mirrored whole under `var/research_corpus/mirrors/pwb-ladder/<commit>/`.
- **Evidence prefix.** Membership and characteristics at M read only revisions with ordinal ≤ that of R(M), only
  detector output computed on those revisions, and only identity records whose evidence was accepted on or before
  s(M) under step 1's acceptance rule (`acceptance_ny_date`). Split records are not prefixed: a split effective on or
  before s(M) had traded by s(M), and a split effective in (s(M), commit of R(M)] is used only to undo the vendor's
  adjustment of R(M) back to the raw close of s(M), which was public at s(M). A test asserts that appending later
  revisions, detector output or identity records leaves every formation's membership and ME unchanged.
- **Read key.** §3.1 gives each security a symbol per date. In revision r, a security's rows are read under its symbol
  at r's commit date. Rows dated before an adjudicated symbol change are used only if they pass the continuity test of
  §3.5 against an earlier revision read under the earlier symbol; otherwise that revision's rows before the change are
  not used. A row dated t is used only if the #3361 linkage does not tie the read symbol at t to a different CIK
  (ticker reuse).
- **Remote reads.** Other revisions are read by symbol- or date-filtered query. Each result is written to an extract
  file whose sha256 is recorded with the commit, filter and row count, so every decision replays offline.
- **Price bound.** No price, volume or return dated after 2026-08-31 leaves the reader. The reader may report that a
  security has a usable bar after the bound (presence, for §6), never its values.
- **Bar validity.** The #2261 bar rules (`price-quarantine` rule set, version pinned) are applied per revision to every
  bar a rule reads. Only usable bars enter a statistic, a level, a return or a presence test.
- **Licence posture** is unchanged from #3619 slice 3: research use, local mirror, no redistribution.

### 2. The adjudicated issuer set U

U is the set of issuers (CIK) the event file covers. It is built in two passes; neither reads a stage-C price before
the declaration.

**Pass 1, before the declaration** (documents and pre-stage data only):
1. **Incumbents:** issuers whose largest admitted security at formation 2024-07-31 has final-ME rank ≤ K among all
   admitted securities.
2. **Entrants:** issuers with no admitted security at 2024-07-31 that file a Form 8-A12B, 10-12B or 8-K12B, or an 8-K
   listing Item 5.06, accepted 2024-05-01 .. 2026-07-31.
3. **Terminated issuers:** every issuer CIK on a §9 census event (a Form 25 row in the window), whatever its size.
4. **Successors:** every CIK a confirmed `succession` record names, with screens and adjudication rerun on each new CIK
   until no record names a CIK outside U. U only grows and is finite, so this stops; each iteration's candidate list is
   pinned as its own version.

**K, by construction on pre-stage data.** For each formation pair (M₀, M₀ + h) with M₀ in stage B and 1 ≤ h ≤ 24, the
share of the top 1,000's final ME at M₀ + h held by issuers present at M₀ and ranked > K there (issuer rank as in item
1, no re-ranking; issuers absent at M₀ are excluded from both sides). K is the smallest of {1,500, 2,000, 2,500,
3,000} with that share ≤ 0.25% at every pair; else 3,000, with the measured share published. K bounds pass-1 effort; it
is not a correctness bound, because pass 2 closes the gap.

**Pass 2, after the declaration (closure).** The slice-6 build computes each formation's admitted population with ME.
Any issuer of a security ranked ≤ 1,200 at any formation that is not in U joins U, is screened and adjudicated under the
same protocol (§3.3: documents only; the adjudicator receives the CIK, not a price or rank), and the formation is
rebuilt. Repeat until no issuer ranked ≤ 1,200 is outside U. The rule does not change; additions are labelled
`closure` and counted per formation in the census. A security ranked ≤ 1,000 whose issuer is outside U after closure is
impossible by construction; the build asserts it.

### 3. The event file (`stage-c-event-file-v1`)

#### 3.1 Records

One CSV per record type under `docs/research/3739-event-file/`. Every row carries: CIK; the class key (the trading
symbol the class carries in the cover's Section 12(b) table on the evidence date); one to three evidence items, each an
accession number, document file name and a verbatim quote of at most 300 characters; and the SEC acceptance timestamp
of each. Events effective 2024-08-01 .. the build date, plus July 2024 events for the overlap check (§5).

| type | key | fields |
|---|---|---|
| `split` | (class key, effective date) | ratio = new shares per old share; reverse splits < 1. Same-class share distributions only: a distribution of another issuer's or another class's securities is not a split and is recorded as `spin` (no price effect is modelled; listed in the census) |
| `symbol_change` | (CIK, effective date) | old symbol, new symbol |
| `succession` | (predecessor class key, effective date) | successor CIK and class key; recorded only when holders receive the successor's shares and nothing else (holdco reorganisation, redomicile). Any cash or third-party consideration is a termination |
| `termination_end` | (register event, §9) | the end date the filings state (last trading day, suspension date, or merger closing date), or `not_stated` |

Baseline identity is the #3361 linkage; there is no baseline identity record. `symbol_change` records only adjudicate
the changes the linkage's evidence shows.

**Deduplication.** One row per key. Several documents describing one action are evidence items on one row. A row whose
key repeats is a build error.

Split stamps for the panel: Intrader's for effective dates ≤ 2024-07-31 (as stages A and B), the event file's for
effective dates ≥ 2024-08-01. July 2024 event-file splits are compared with Intrader's stamps (§5) and are not stamps.

#### 3.2 Candidate screens

Screens propose; only adjudication writes a record. Each screen is code with tests, and its candidate list is pinned
before adjudication starts.

- **split:** an XBRL `StockholdersEquityNoteStockSplitConversionRatio1` fact accepted in the window, whatever its
  context date (a repeated fact for an old split is rejected in adjudication); per class (dei `StockClassAxis` member,
  or none), consecutive `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` cover counts ordered by acceptance, amendments
  replacing their originals, whose ratio lies outside [0.8, 1.25]; any 8-K whose `items` include 5.03.
- **symbol_change:** a CIK whose linkage evidence shows two symbols in the window.
- **succession and termination_end:** every register row (§9 events) for a U issuer; the adjudicator reads the issuer's
  8-Ks within 120 days before the Form 25 and the Form 25's exhibits.

Screen recall for splits is measured (§3.4). Any split the vendor applied but no screen found is caught by the
detector after the declaration (§3.5); a split neither applied nor found leaves the vendor's own series unadjusted,
which §5 then uses as raw (see §5, "unapplied").

#### 3.3 Adjudication protocol

- **Inputs:** the candidate and SEC filings of that CIK, of a named counterparty CIK, and of the exchange CIK on the
  Form 25. Nothing outside EDGAR. No market-data source is opened.
- **Document lifecycle:** a later filing that amends, corrects, postpones or cancels an earlier one supersedes it. The
  record follows the latest filing accepted before the effective date that states the action, confirmed where one
  exists by a later periodic report. `contested` is reserved for two filings both accepted after the effective date
  that disagree; both are recorded.
- **Outcomes:** `confirmed`, `rejected` (reason: issuance, buyback, other class, repeated disclosure of an earlier
  action, withdrawn), `contested`, or `no_document`. A `no_document` or `contested` split candidate carries an
  interval: from the earlier to the later acceptance of the two filings that triggered it.
- **Checker, deterministic, per record type:** every evidence item's document is fetched by accession, its bytes
  mirrored with sha256, and the quote must occur in it (whitespace-normalised). Then: a `split` needs the ratio, and the
  effective date or the record and payable dates, in its quotes; a `symbol_change` needs both symbols and the date; a
  `succession` needs the successor's name and the share-for-share terms; a `termination_end` needs the date, or carries
  `not_stated`. A row that fails is not admitted.
- **Who:** an agent session or a person; the protocol is the same. Every document opened is logged (§3.6).

#### 3.4 Pre-stage measurement of split-screen recall

Before stage-C adjudication, the screens and protocol run on a replica: U′ = items 1–2 of pass 1 at formation
2022-07-31, events effective 2022-08-01 .. 2024-07-31. The comparison set is Intrader's `split_factor` stamps on series
the #3361 linkage ties to U′ CIKs: an independent vendor's record, which measures recall against what that vendor
saw, not completeness.
- **Match:** one-to-one, same CIK and class (the linkage's symbol at the stamp date equals the record's class key),
  ratio within 0.5%, effective date equal to the stamp date or the next session.
- **Recall:** matched ÷ stamps, by count and weighted by final ME at the formation before the stamp (stamps on
  securities with no final ME there weigh at the formation's 1,000th ME).
- **Unmatched confirmations:** each is a checker-passed document record; they are counted and listed. More than 5% of
  confirmations unmatched fails the replica, since either the vendor or the protocol is then wrong too often to measure.
- **Pass:** recall ≥ 99% by ME and ≥ 97% by count, and the unmatched-confirmation limit. On a fail, the screens are
  revised once and the replica reruns on 2020-08-01 .. 2022-07-31 (U′ at 2020-07-31). A second fail stops the route:
  stage C is not buildable by this protocol, and #3740 moves to forward paper accrual.

The replica measures split screens only. Symbol changes and successions are covered by the linkage's evidence and the
register respectively; terminations by the register.

#### 3.5 The rescale detector (after the declaration)

For a security and two revisions r₁ < r₂ that are consecutive among the revisions holding it under its read key: on the
usable sessions both hold in the 21 sessions ending at r₁'s last bar, ρ(t) = close_{r₁}(t) / close_{r₂}(t). At least 5
shared sessions are required; fewer makes the pair `untestable`.
- **No rescale:** every ρ(t) within 0.5% of 1.
- **Rescale ρ:** every ρ(t) within 0.5% of their median ρ ≠ 1.
- **Inconsistent:** neither (the vendor changed history non-uniformly).

A rescale is **explained** when the product of the security's split ratios effective in (first shared session,
commit of r₂] equals ρ within 0.5%. A file split with no rescale over a pair spanning it is `unapplied`. An unexplained
rescale becomes a candidate under §3.3, handed over as (CIK, class key, the two commit dates) with no ratio and no
price; records confirmed this way are labelled `detector_triggered`. The continuity test for symbol changes (§1) is
this detector across the two read keys.

#### 3.6 Freeze

Pinned by sha256 in the event-file manifest: the K calibration; each candidate-list version; the replica result; every
record; every mirrored document; the checker output; and the adjudication log (each document opened, by whom, for which
candidate, with the outcome). The pass-1 file is frozen before #3740's declaration. Pass-2 and detector-triggered
records are appended after it under their labels; no frozen record changes.

### 5. Raw price and ME

**Raw price.** For a security, a revision R and a session t ≤ R's last bar for it:
raw_R(t) = close_R(t) × Π ratio over its splits effective in (t, commit of R] that the detector shows applied in R.
A split effective in (t, commit of R] is applied in R when some detector pair (r₁, r₂ ≤ R) spanning it is an explained
rescale. raw_R(t) is **unresolved** when, on that security over (t, commit of R]: a file split is `unapplied` but R
holds no bar on or after its effective date (whether the vendor adjusted is unknown); a detector pair is unexplained,
inconsistent or untestable; or a `contested` or `no_document` interval overlaps. An `unapplied` split over a pair whose
r₂ holds bars after the effective date means the vendor left the series unadjusted; the close is then already raw for
that split, and it is not multiplied in.

**ME.** With R = R(M), step 1's formula: ME(M) = shares(b) × split_product(stamps, b, s(M)) × raw_R(s(M)). An
unresolved raw_R(s(M)) makes ME missing, reason `basis_unresolved`. Checks 1–4 run as step 1 defines them on these
stamps (check 1's product tolerance is 100×, `factor_panel.SCALE_TOLERANCE`, so adjudicated splits pass it).

**$5 exclusion.** The price is raw_R(s(M)); unresolved is excluded as `price_basis_unresolved`.

**Volume basis (check 3), on pre-stage data.** For each Intrader stamp dated 2024-07-08 .. 2024-07-31 on a linked
security, compare, on the 20 sessions before the stamp, `close × volume` in the first revision committed after the
stamp with Intrader's raw `close × volume`. If the vendor adjusts volume with price, the two agree; if it adjusts only
price, they differ by the ratio. The convention is established if at least 5 securities qualify and at least 95% of
their sessions agree within 1% (or all differ by the ratio within 1%, which establishes the other convention, applied
as a correction). Otherwise check 3 is `untested` only for rows whose 63-session window contains a split effective
date, with step 1's treatment of an untested check 3 (it blocks verification and reference eligibility for those rows,
as Amendment 2.1 already does when check 3 cannot run).

**Unwinding validation (pre-stage).** In revision `8936ae04` (committed 2024-07-07), for every security linked to

published per formation beside step 1's other ME-missing reasons, whose shares are compared with stage B's.

### 6. Holdings: one state per holding

Weights are fixed at s(M). The holding month is m = M + 1, valued over (s(M), s(m)]. The security's **last observed
bar** is its latest usable bar in any revision, capped at its `termination_end` date when one is stated. **Alive after
m** means a usable bar after s(m) in some revision (presence only beyond the price bound), not after a stated
`termination_end`. States, in order:

1. **`terminal`** — not alive after m, and the last observed bar L ≤ s(m). Return from s(M) to L (zero if L = s(M)),
   then `classify_termination` on the security's register event (linked, provision) and the read key's `Q` suffix, with
   `terminal_value_fraction` per arm. With no register event the classifier returns `UNKNOWN` (two-armed) or
   `Q_SUFFIX_OTC`, as for stages A and B. A `succession` predecessor is terminal as `OPERATION_OF_LAW` at L; the
   successor is eligible from the next formation.
2. **`observed`** — a usable bar at s(m). Return to s(m).
3. **`observed`, early end bar** — alive after m, and the latest bar in the month d satisfies d ≥ session(s(m) − 5
   sessions). Return to d.
4. **`coverage_exit`** — alive after m otherwise. Return to d (zero if d = s(M)), then cash at 0, as step 1.

Exclusive and total: a security is either alive after m or not; if not, L ≤ s(m) because L is its last usable bar.

**Return arithmetic.** The return uses `adj_close` within one revision: the latest revision holding both endpoint bars
under the read key, because a later capture can carry distributions posted after an earlier one (#3619 slice 3). Within
one revision a rescale is uniform, so it does not distort a ratio. If no revision holds both endpoints, the path is
deterministic: start in the latest revision holding the start bar, take its ratio to its last bar ≤ the end, then
continue in the next revision (by ordinal) holding that bar and a later one, linking only where §3.5 finds the pair
explained or with no rescale. A break leaves the return at the last linked bar, labelled `bridge_break`, and the holding
gets §9's coverage scenarios. Order: observed segment, exit treatment, clip per arm. Raw values, clipped values and
provenance are kept as Amendment 3 stores them. eToro never supplies a return.

### 7. Vendor disagreement: detected and published, not corrected

- **Extractor.** One function returns monthly close pairs for any security and month: the last usable bar of each

- **Consistency link.** An eToro instrument with the security's symbol (US listing), and the same CIK where eToro's

### 9. Survivorship coverage test (threshold fixed here)

Run once, on the final artefact, after pass-2 closure.

**Events.** Two sources, both full-population:
- **Register events:** every row of `sec_form25_common_equity_delistings`, plus every issuer-filed Form 25 (paragraph
  (c), no class description) whose issuer has a security admitted at any stage-A, B or C formation, filed from
  2024-08-01 to the harvest date. The register is re-harvested at slice 6 to the build date and the harvest date is
  recorded. An amendment supersedes its original (same issuer CIK, exchange CIK and class description); the latest
  amendment's fields stand. Events are keyed by CIK, so an issuer no revision ever captured is still an event.
- **Unregistered exits:** every security admitted at some stage-C formation that is not alive after its last holding
  month and has no register event. These come from the ladder and need no filing.

**End date.** The `termination_end` record's stated date; for an unregistered exit, the last observed bar. A register
event with `not_stated` is `end_unknown`.

**Size at M, an upper estimate.** w(event, M) = shares × raw(s(M)), with the step-1 selected count at s(M) whatever its
check outcomes (else the latest cover count accepted on or before s(M)), moved to s(M) by the stamps, and raw from the
latest revision holding a usable bar at s(M), or from R(M). Where no revision holds one, or raw is unresolved, w(M) =
w at the security's latest sized date before M × G(gap), where G(g) is the 99.9th percentile of the ME growth ratio over
g formations among all admitted securities of stages A and B (pre-stage, computed in slice 2 and pinned). An event never
sized at any date from 2024-07-31 on is `unsized`.

**Relevant formations:** every stage-C M with s(M) before the event's end date (for `end_unknown`, before its filing)
and w(event, M) ≥ the 1,000th final ME of M's admitted population.

**Admission axis**, per relevant (event, M): `admitted` (in M's top 1,000), `not_in_R` (no usable bar at s(M) in
R(M)), `excluded` (failed a step-2 to step-6 rule, reason kept), or `below_cut` (admitted outside the top 1,000 by
final ME although w ≥ the cut: the estimate and the panel disagree). Every axis value except `admitted` counts.

**End axis**, per event with an admitted security at its last relevant formation:

| class | predicate (evaluated in order) | counts |
|---|---|---|
| `end_unknown` | `not_stated` | yes |
| `succeeded` | a confirmed `succession`, and the successor is admitted at the formation after the effective date | no |
| `unregistered` | an unregistered exit | yes |
| `post_anchor` | the last observed bar is after the end date | yes |
| `served` | the last observed bar is in [end date − 5 sessions, end date] | no |
| `unserved` | the last observed bar is earlier | yes |

A `succession` whose successor is not admitted at the next formation falls through to the rows below it.

**Statistic.** For each formation M,
u(M) = Σ w(event, M) over relevant (event, M) whose admission axis counts, plus, at each event's last relevant
formation, w of events whose end class counts, ÷ Σ final ME of M's top 1,000. Each (security, M) contributes once.

**Pass iff** no event is `unsized`, max over the 24 formations of u(M) ≤ 0.5%, and their mean ≤ 0.05%. These are
coverage limits fixed by construction, not bounds on a book's error. They permit an omission of up to 0.5% of the
top 1,000's ME in a formation; the census lists every counted event by name, so #3740 sees each one.

**For #3740 (recorded here, implemented there).** Every holding in state `terminal` with a two-armed or counted class,
`coverage_exit`, or `bridge_break` gets two coverage scenarios that override the final clipped value for the holding
month: −100% and 0%. They are scenarios reported beside the source-rule treatment with raw values shown, not bounds.
Securities never admitted have no holding; u is their only measure.

**Census published with the artefact:** every event with its end date and evidence, relevant formations, w and how it
was computed (G-scaled values marked), both axes, the last observed bar and revision; per formation, u(M), the counts
and ME shares of `closure` issuers, `detector_triggered` records, `basis_unresolved`, `price_basis_unresolved`,
`bridge_break` and `spin` events.

**If the test fails,** stage C is not admissible and #3740's confirmation moves to forward paper accrual (#3739
§"If the survivorship test fails").

Per-name median |Δ| over the ≥ 12-pair population: 11.4 bp (50th), 44.8 bp (90th), 69.4 bp (95th), 1.27 pp (99th).

```sql
with me as (
  select s.instrument_id, date_trunc('month', d.bar_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(d.close order by d.bar_date desc))[1] c, max(d.bar_date) bd
  from research_price_daily d join research_price_series s using (series_id)
  where s.vendor = 'paperswithbacktest/Stocks-Daily-Price@2026-09-09' and s.instrument_id is not null
    and d.bar_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and d.close > 0
  group by 1, 2),
ee as (
  select instrument_id, date_trunc('month', price_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(close order by price_date desc))[1] c, max(price_date) bd
  from price_daily where price_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and close > 0 group by 1, 2),
rp as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from me window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
re as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from ee window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
j as (select a.instrument_id, a.m, a.r rp, b.r re from rp a join re b using (instrument_id, m)
      where a.m0 = (a.m - interval '1 month')::date and b.m0 = a.m0 and a.bd = b.bd and a.bd0 = b.bd0),
per as (select instrument_id, count(*) n, percentile_cont(0.5) within group (order by abs(rp - re)) med
        from j group by 1 having count(*) >= 12),
lk as (select instrument_id from per where med < 0.02),
fl as (select * from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)
         and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25)
select (select count(distinct instrument_id) from j),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25),
       (select count(*) from per),
       (select percentile_cont(array[0.5, 0.9, 0.95, 0.99]) within group (order by med) from per),
       (select count(*) from lk),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)),
       (select count(*) from fl), (select count(distinct instrument_id) from fl);
```
