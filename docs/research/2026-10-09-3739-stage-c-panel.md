## Pre-stage measurement: Form 25 filing date against the archive's last bar

On Intrader series linked to a Form 25 (`delisting_source = 'sec_form25'`) whose last bar precedes 2024-08-01,
filing date minus **archive last bar**, in calendar days:

| provision | series | filed before last bar | 0–7 days | 8–31 | > 31 | median / p90 / p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `(a)(3)` | 682 | 60 | 618 | 1 | 3 | 1 / 3 / 4 |
| `(b)` | 194 | 4 | 22 | 88 | 80 | 23 / 76 / 91 |

```sql
select delisting_provision, count(*),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar < 0),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar between 0 and 7),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar between 8 and 31),
       count(*) filter (where delisting_filed_date - last_bar > 31),
       percentile_cont(array[0.5, 0.9, 0.95]) within group (order by delisting_filed_date - last_bar)
from research_price_series
where vendor = 'icyDenev/Intrader' and delisting_source = 'sec_form25' and last_bar < '2024-08-01'
group by 1;
```

This is an archive-endpoint lag distribution, conditional on a successful link; it does not observe economic ends.
For `(a)(3)`, the archive's last bar falls 0–7 days before the filing in 618 of 682 linked series. The 60 that run
past their filing are not individually attributed here. For `(b)`, the filing trails the last bar by weeks, so it is
not usable as an end estimate. `sec-edgar.md` §2.6 trap 5 still holds: the filing date is a different event. §9 uses
it only for `(a)(3)`, as an interval this table supports.

## Contract (`pwb-revision-ladder-v1`)

### 1. The ladder

- **Scan.** The data revisions from `8936ae04` (2024-07-07) to `a4aead25` (2026-10-01), ordered by commit timestamp,
  ties by the HF API's listing order. The ordered index (ordinal, commit id, timestamp, shard oids) is pinned. A read
  refuses if an oid differs.
- **Sessions.** The session calendar is the dates with a usable SPY bar in `a4aead25` (pinned). session(t) is the
  last session on or before t. s(M) = session(M). A month without one refuses the build.
- **Formation revision.** R(M) = the earliest revision holding a usable SPY bar at s(M). The 24 R(M) and `a4aead25`
  are mirrored whole under `var/research_corpus/mirrors/pwb-ladder/<commit>/`.
- **Evidence prefix.** Anything that decides membership, ME or a characteristic at M reads only revisions with
  ordinal ≤ that of R(M). A test asserts that appending later revisions leaves every formation's membership and ME
  unchanged. Holdings (§6) and the census (§9) may read later revisions.
- **Remote reads.** Other revisions are read by symbol- or date-filtered query. Each result is written to an extract
  file whose sha256 is recorded with the commit, filter and row count, so every decision replays offline.
- **Price bound.** No price, volume or return dated after 2026-08-31 leaves the reader. The reader may report that a
  chain holds a **usable, identity-verified** bar after the bound (presence, for §6), never its values.
- **Bar validity.** The #2261 bar rules (`price-quarantine` rule set, version pinned) are applied per revision to
  every bar a rule reads. Only usable bars enter a statistic, a level, a return or a presence test.
- **Licence posture** is unchanged from #3619 slice 3: research use, local mirror, no redistribution.

### 2. Chains

Chains are built for every symbol over the scan, independently of admission.

- **Pairs.** A symbol's pairs are its consecutive presences: revisions r₁ and r₂ that both hold the symbol, with no
  revision between them holding it. A pair separated by revisions without the symbol is a `gap` pair.
- **Pair ratio.** d₁ = the symbol's last usable bar in r₁; ρ = close_{r₁}(d₁) / close_{r₂}(d₁). No usable bar at d₁ in
  r₂ makes the pair `unpaired`.
- **Same security.** Over the 63 sessions ending d₁, every shared usable date has close_{r₁}/close_{r₂} within 0.5%
  of ρ, with at least 10 shared dates, **and** the #3361 linkage gives the same result for the symbol at d₁ and at
  d₂ (both the same CIK, or both unlinked). Sixty-three sessions is step 1's minimum liquidity window, reused.
- **Outcomes per pair, in order:**
  1. `linkage_change`: the linkage differs. The chain ends at d₁.
  2. `revision_disagreement`: at most 2 dates in the window fall outside the band. The chain continues; those dates
     are unusable in both revisions.
  3. `identity_break`: more than 2 dates outside the band, or `unpaired`. The chain ends at d₁.
  4. `history_corrected`: the window passes but older shared dates fall outside the band. The chain continues; the
     older disputed dates are unusable in both revisions.
  5. `continues`.
- **Rescale.** A continuing pair with |ρ − 1| > 0.5% records a rescale with window (d₁, d₂], where d₂ is the symbol's
  last usable bar in r₂. If d₂ ≤ d₁ it is `retroactive`. Rescales never multiply a share count; they mark ambiguity
  (§4).
- **Ticker change.** When a chain ends without a Form 25 on the CIK within the 30 days around d₁, a symbol Y in the
  next revision continues it if Y links to the same CIK at d₁ and passes the price test against the ended symbol in
  its last revision. Every symbol linked to that CIK is a candidate; two that pass end the chain
  (`continuation_ambiguous`).
- **Gaps.** A `gap` pair continues the chain only if it passes the same-security test; otherwise the later presence
  starts a new chain.
- **Last observed bar** = the latest usable bar the chain holds in any of its revisions, with the revision.

A successor appended under the same symbol, the same linked CIK and continuous prices is undetectable here
(§"Known limits").

### 3. The population at M

Step 1 §"Universe at M", with steps 1 and 2 replaced:

1. **Priced at the decision.** R(M) holds a usable bar at s(M) for the symbol, and it is not a Nasdaq test issue (the
   directory response and its normalised symbol set are pinned).
2. **Linked.** The #3361 linkage (§5) returns `LINKED` for the symbol at s(M).

Steps 3–6 are step 1's. A name that traded at s(M) but was not captured in R(M) is not recovered; §9 counts it.

**Vendor aliases.** Two symbols at s(M) are aliases when both link to the same CIK at s(M) and their open, high, low,
close and volume are equal on every date either holds in R(M), with at least 21 dates. Aliases group by connected
components. The member equal to the linkage's basis symbol is kept; if none or several are, step 5's multi-security
exclusion applies to the group.

### 4. ME from contemporaneous captures

**Raw price at a date.** For a security and a session t:
- **t ≤ 2024-09-27:** Intrader's raw `close` at t, on the Intrader series that the #3361 linkage ties to the same CIK
  at t. No such series, or more than one, makes raw(t) `unresolved`. Intrader `split_factor` stamps date its splits.
- **t > 2024-09-27:** A(t) is the earliest revision (by ordinal) holding a usable bar at t for the chain. raw(t) =
  close_{A(t)}(t), **only if the capture is contemporaneous**: the chain's last usable bar in A(t) is within 10
  sessions after t. A later first appearance is a backfill, and raw(t) is `unresolved`. A contemporaneous raw(t) is
  `ambiguous` if the pair ending at A(t) records a rescale, if A(t) is the chain's first revision, or if t ≤ d₁ of
  that pair is not in r₁ (a backfill inside the window).
- A raw(t) is `basis_corrected` when A(t) and R disagree beyond a constant ratio, i.e. close_{A(t)}/close_R is outside
  0.5% of its value at t on any usable shared session within 21 sessions of t.

**One normalisation, used everywhere.** With R = R(M) and session dates:
F(t, s(M)) = [raw(t) / close_R(t)] / [raw(s(M)) / close_R(s(M))].
Every share count used at M, whatever its date t (the selected count, DQC comparators, recovery sides, check-4
references, Amendment 2.1 consensus partners), is put on the s(M) basis as count × F(session(t), s(M)). If raw at
either date is `unresolved`, `ambiguous` or `basis_corrected`, or F lies outside [1/100, 100], that count is
**unusable**: a selected count makes ME missing (reason `basis_capture_ambiguous`); a comparator, recovery side,
reference or consensus partner is treated as an unusable read, which the step-1 rules already handle.

**ME.** ME(M) = shares(b) × raw(session(b)) × close_R(s(M)) / close_R(session(b)). This equals step 1's
`shares_s × raw close` (since close_R(x) = raw(x) / F(x, C)), and it does not use raw(s(M)), so uncertainty in raw(s(M))
alone does not remove ME. It needs usable bars at session(b) and s(M) in R on one chain; if b precedes the chain's
history in R, ME is missing (`basis_history_unavailable`).

**Check 1** fires when F(session(b), s(M)) is unusable, or when a split falls in (context date, acceptance NY date] of
the count: an Intrader stamp there, or a ladder rescale window overlapping it. Checks 2–4 run as step 1 defines them,
with every count normalised as above.

**Check 3 and the volume basis.** Dollar volume is `close × volume` in R. Whether the vendor adjusts volume for
splits is undocumented, so the build establishes it on pre-stage data: for each Intrader split stamp dated
2024-07-08 .. 2024-07-31 on a linked security, compare `close × volume` in `8936ae04`'s successors against Intrader's
raw `close × volume` on the 20 sessions before the stamp. If at least 95% of compared sessions agree within 1%, the
convention holds and check 3 runs; otherwise check 3 is `untested` for every row.

**$5 exclusion.** The price is raw(s(M)). If it is `ambiguous` with a recorded ρ, the candidates are raw and raw × ρ;
if `unresolved`, `basis_corrected`, or ambiguous with no ρ, the price is unknown. A name whose candidates straddle $5,
or whose price is unknown, is excluded as `price_basis_ambiguous`. This reason is recorded whether or not ME was
already missing.

**Overlap validation** (pre-stage and stage boundary). Key: (CIK, session t) for every t in 2024-07-08 .. 2024-09-27
where exactly one Intrader series and one chain link to the CIK. Compare Intrader's raw close with the ladder's
contemporaneous raw(t). Ambiguous or unresolved ladder values are reported and excluded from the rate. The validation
needs at least 10,000 compared pairs; with fewer, the build refuses. If more than 1% of compared pairs differ by more
than 0.5%, the operation fails validation and stage C is inadmissible (§"Seal"). The report is stratified by whether
a split falls within 10 sessions of t. This window is dense in captures, so it does not test the sparse 2026 cadence.

**Fixtures** (slice 2b tests): ORLY 15:1, FAST 2:1, IBKR 4:1; a reverse split; a backfilled first appearance; a
retroactive rescale; offsetting splits inside one pair (raw(t) between them must come out `ambiguous` or
`unresolved`, never resolved); `revision_disagreement`, `history_corrected` and `identity_break` pairs; a gap pair; a
`linkage_change`; an alias group.

### 6. Holdings: one state per holding

Weights are fixed at s(M). The holding month is m = M + 1, valued over (s(M), s(m)]. Let d = the chain's latest usable
bar on or before s(m). **Alive after m** means the chain holds a usable, identity-verified bar after s(m) in any
revision (presence only beyond the price bound; for m = 2026-08 this comes from `a4aead25`, bars to 2026-09-09).

States, checked in order; the first match applies:

1. **`terminal`** — the chain ended (no later pair continues it) and is not alive after m. Return from s(M) to d (zero
   if d = s(M)), then `classify_termination` on the chain's Form 25 evidence and `Q` suffix, with
   `terminal_value_fraction` per arm. A chain that ended in `identity_break`, `linkage_change` or
   `continuation_ambiguous` is classified the same way, so with no Form 25 evidence it is `unknown_termination` and
   two-armed.
2. **`observed`** — alive after m, with a usable bar at s(m). Return to s(m).
3. **`observed`, early end bar** — alive after m and d > s(M). Return to d, as the #3619 reader does when the month's
   later bars are missing or quarantined.
4. **`coverage_exit`** — alive after m and d = s(M): no usable bar in the month although the security continues.
   Return zero, then cash at 0.

These are exclusive and total: a chain either ended or is alive after m, and if alive, d is either s(m), inside the
month, or s(M).

**Return arithmetic.** The return is `adj_close` read inside one revision holding both endpoint bars: the latest such
revision in the chain. If none holds both, it is the product of within-revision ratios bridged at the last shared
usable session of each consecutive pair. The latest revision is chosen because a later capture can carry
distributions posted after an earlier one (#3619 slice 3); a name that left the dataset gets no such repair, and the
census records the revision used. Order: observed segment, exit treatment, clip per arm. Raw values, clipped values
and provenance are kept as Amendment 3 stores them. eToro never supplies a return.

### 7. Vendor disagreement: detected and published, not corrected

Step 2's stage B had one top-1,000 name-month (COO, formation 2024-01, Intrader +301.5%) that eToro closes do not
show. Correcting one vendor from another is refused on bias grounds (`SPLIT_CORRECTION_POLICY`; step 1 Amendment 3),
and a disagreement does not say which feed is wrong.

- **Extractor.** One function returns monthly close pairs for any chain and month: the last usable bar of each
  calendar month in the latest revision holding both month-ends, `close` (split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted),
  with both bar dates. The same function serves held and non-held names and months before stage C. eToro's side is
  `price_daily.close` with its bar dates.
- **Consistency link.** An eToro instrument with the chain's symbol (US listing) is linked when they share at least 12
  pairs with equal start and end bar dates in the 24 months ending at the chain's last observed bar or 2026-08,
  whichever is earlier, with median |r_PWB − r_eToro| < 2 pp.
- **Flag.** A linked name-month whose (1 + r_PWB)/(1 + r_eToro) lies outside [0.8, 1.25], step 1's discontinuity band.
  Every priced, linked name-month is tested.
- **Treatment.** None beyond the clip. No return, level, ME, state or membership changes.
- **Published:** every flagged name-month with both returns and bar dates; per formation, the count and ME share of
  flagged and of unlinked names. #3740's diagnostics print the book's weight in flagged months.

### 9. Survivorship coverage test (threshold fixed here)

Run once, on the final artefact. Two axes are kept separate: whether a relevant security was **admitted**, and
whether its **end** was captured.

**Events.** From `sec_form25_common_equity_delistings`: one event per (issuer CIK, exchange CIK,
`description_class_security`), collapsing a filing with its amendments when they share those three and fall within 30
days; amendments outside 30 days stay separate and are listed. Collapse first, then keep events with a filing in
2024-09-01 .. 2026-09-30. The register's harvest cutoff (2026-10-08) is recorded.

**Mapping.** An event maps to the chains linked to its CIK at a date before its filing. One chain: `mapped`. Several:
`mapped_ambiguous`, sized at the largest. None: `unmapped`.

**End interval E per event**, evaluated first:
- a stated suspension date S: E = [S, S];
- else `(a)(3)`: E = [filing − 7 days, filing], the pre-stage lag interval;
- else `end_unknown`.
Calendar dates convert to sessions by session().

**Relevant formations.** M is relevant to an event when s(M) is inside its stage-C window: before E's lower edge and
no more than 3 formations before it; for `end_unknown`, from 4 formations before the filing (the pre-stage `(b)` lag
p95 is 91 days) to the filing. Formations outside the 24 are not counted; events with no relevant formation are listed
as `out_of_window`.

**Size, independent of admission.** w(event, M) = shares(b) × raw(session(b)), with the step-1 share count selected at
s(M) and raw from §4 (Intrader or the contemporaneous capture), read from any revision of the event's chains. The event
is **relevant** at M when w ≥ the 1,000th final ME of M's admitted population. If w cannot be computed (no count, no
raw price, or ambiguity), the event is `unsized` and treated as relevant with w = that 1,000th ME.

**Admission axis**, per relevant (event, M): `admitted` (in M's top 1,000), `not_in_R` (no usable bar at s(M) in R(M)),
`excluded` (failed a step-2 to step-6 rule, reason kept), or `below_cut` (admitted but outside the top 1,000 by final
ME: not counted).

**End axis**, per event with a mapped chain admitted at its last relevant formation:

| class | predicate (evaluated in order) | counts |
|---|---|---|
| `end_unknown` | no E | yes |
| `continuing` | the chain is alive after the holding month that contains E's upper edge, with no `linkage_change` | no |
| `post_anchor` | the chain ended and its last observed bar is after E's upper edge | yes |
| `served` | the chain ended and its last observed bar is in [E's lower edge − 5 sessions, E's upper edge] | no |
| `unserved` | the chain ended earlier than that | yes |

For `(b)` and `(a)(4)` events, `continuing` additionally requires the §2 same-security test to hold across every pair
after E: the corpus rule's distinction between the same company trading on and a later occupant. A chain that passes
is listed as `continuing_after_exchange_exit`. It does not count, and #3740 sees it in the census.

**Statistic.** For each formation M:
u(M) = Σ w over relevant (event, M) with admission `not_in_R` or `excluded`, plus, at the event's last relevant
formation, w of events whose end class counts ÷ Σ final ME of M's top 1,000.
Each (chain, M) contributes once; `mapped_ambiguous` events at their largest w. Contaminated `me.raw` never enters.
Every formation must have at least 1,000 admitted names with finite ME, or the build refuses.

**Pass iff** max over the 24 formations of u(M) ≤ 0.5% **and** their mean ≤ 0.05%. These are coverage limits fixed
by construction, not bounds on a book's error: a small universe weight can be a large leg weight.

**For #3740 (recorded here, implemented there).** Each holding whose state (§6) is `terminal` or `coverage_exit` and
whose event counts in u gets two coverage scenarios that override the final clipped value: −100% and 0% for the
holding month. They are scenarios, not bounds, reported beside the source-rule treatment with raw values shown.
Exposures with no holding row (`not_in_R`, `excluded`, `unsized`) have no book weight; u is their only measure.

**Census published with the artefact:** every event with its mapping, E and basis, relevant formations, w and how it
was computed, both axes, the last observed bar and revision, and u(M) per formation; `out_of_window` and `unmapped`
events; counts and ME shares of `basis_capture_ambiguous`, `basis_history_unavailable` and `price_basis_ambiguous`.

**If the test fails,** stage C is not admissible and #3740's confirmation moves to forward paper accrual (#3739
§"If the survivorship test fails").

## Known limits

- **Not sealed against the designer.** The supervisor model's training data run to 2026-06, and the reads in §"Seal"
  happened.
- **One vendor.** Revisions are captures of one Yahoo-derived scrape. Agreement between them is consistency, not
  corroboration. eToro is the only independent feed and covers only the names it lists.
- **Coverage is measured on a cohort.** Exits without a Form 25 in the register (paragraph (c) moves to OTC, which
  the register excludes) and events whose CIK maps to no chain are listed, not tested.
- **Sparse 2026 captures** can leave a last observed bar weeks before an end; the census records each.
- **Omissions are counted, not recovered.**
- **Undetectable cases:** a successor appended under the same symbol, linked CIK and continuous prices; offsetting
  splits inside one pair where neither endpoint is on a contemporaneous capture are made `ambiguous`, but a pair whose
  windows hide them entirely is not detectable.
- **Retrospective identity.** Chains use later captures to say what a security's history was. Membership and ME at M
  read only the evidence prefix up to R(M).

On `paperswithbacktest/Stocks-Daily-Price@2026-09-09` (the `a4aead25` capture) against `price_daily`, 2024-09..2026-08:

| population | names | paired months | outside [0.8, 1.25] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

Per-name median |Δ| over the ≥ 12-pair population: 11.4 bp (50th), 44.8 bp (90th), 69.4 bp (95th), 1.27 pp (99th).

```sql
with me as (
  select s.instrument_id, date_trunc('month', d.bar_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(d.close order by d.bar_date desc))[1] c, max(d.bar_date) bd
  from research_price_daily d join research_price_series s using (series_id)
  where s.vendor = 'paperswithbacktest/Stocks-Daily-Price@2026-09-09' and s.instrument_id is not null
    and d.bar_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and d.close > 0
  group by 1, 2),
ee as (
  select instrument_id, date_trunc('month', price_date)::date m,
         (array_agg(close order by price_date desc))[1] c, max(price_date) bd
  from price_daily where price_date between '2024-08-01' and '2026-08-31' and close > 0 group by 1, 2),
rp as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from me window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
re as (select instrument_id, m, bd, lag(bd) over w bd0, c / lag(c) over w - 1 r, lag(m) over w m0
       from ee window w as (partition by instrument_id order by m)),
j as (select a.instrument_id, a.m, a.r rp, b.r re from rp a join re b using (instrument_id, m)
      where a.m0 = (a.m - interval '1 month')::date and b.m0 = a.m0 and a.bd = b.bd and a.bd0 = b.bd0),
per as (select instrument_id, count(*) n, percentile_cont(0.5) within group (order by abs(rp - re)) med
        from j group by 1 having count(*) >= 12),
lk as (select instrument_id from per where med < 0.02),
fl as (select * from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)
         and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25)
select (select count(distinct instrument_id) from j),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and (1 + rp) / (1 + re) not between 0.8 and 1.25),
       (select count(*) from per),
       (select percentile_cont(array[0.5, 0.9, 0.95, 0.99]) within group (order by med) from per),
       (select count(*) from lk),
       (select count(*) from j where re > -1 and instrument_id in (select * from lk)),
       (select count(*) from fl), (select count(distinct instrument_id) from fl);
```
