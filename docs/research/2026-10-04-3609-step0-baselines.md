# #3609 step 0 — cheap baselines, net of our costs

Status: spec, revised after Codex checkpoint 1 (48 findings: 46 applied, 2 rebutted in §"Registration"), then
amended twice before any result was computed (§"Amendment 1", §"Amendment 2"). No result has been computed. Programme: `docs/research/2026-10-04-strategy-research-sweep.md` §4 item 0. Every later
#3609/#3620 result is reported against these.

## Question

What would the simplest alternatives have earned, net of eToro spread costs, over the window the factor programme
will test? Nothing here is selected, ranked or tuned. Every baseline is fixed below before its first run, and the
report prints all of them.

**Status of each baseline:** all are **references, not deployable claims**.
- B1/B2 hold ETFs, and UK clients may only get those as CFDs.
- B3 draws from the historical US stock archive without any check of historical eToro eligibility.
- The zero carry and FX charges hold only under the stated assumption of real, unleveraged settlement.

## Timing, window and sub-windows

- **One continuous path per baseline,** monthly, starting at the **2009-12 month-end**. Every sub-window statistic
  is computed on that path's monthly returns, with no restart and no boundary transaction.
- **Trade timing.** Each decision (formation, rebalance) is taken on a month-end close and filled at that same
  close. This is a stated market-on-close assumption, and the spread cost is the only friction charged. The first
  return a trade earns is the following month's.
- **Comparison windows:**
  - **W1 = 2010-01 .. 2021-05.** Strictly before the house `HOLDOUT_BOUNDARY` (2021-06-29); June 2021 straddles
    the boundary.
  - **W2 = 2021-06 .. 2024-08.** Reused validation, still survivorship-free.
  - **W3 = 2024-09 .. E**, where E is the common end month (§"Amendment 1"). **Survivor-only:** a stock that died after 2024-08 is invisible, so B3 is biased
    upward here and labelled so.
  - The full 2010-01 .. E figure is printed as **supplementary, mixed-coverage**, never as the headline.
- **End of the path.** Every baseline is liquidated at its last month-end and the sale cost is charged; W1 and
  W2 statistics carry no liquidation, because the path continues through them.
- **Later same-window comparisons.** The monthly net return paths are saved: B1, B2a, B2b, and each B3 draw per
  arm. A later study slices those paths to its own declared months rather than citing these summary figures.

## Baselines

**B1 — core alone.** SPY total return.
- Source: `etf_total_return_reader.load_etf_total_return_panel(conn, ["SPY"])`.
- Basis: Intrader `adj_close` through 2021-12, then N-PORT Item B.5.a **NAV** total return of IVV. The later
  part is a **synthetic history**: a different fund, NAV rather than market close, different fees. It is
  labelled that way wherever printed.
- One buy at 2009-12 and the liquidation at the path end.

**B2 — two static ETF mixes.** The allocations are published. ETF tickers stand in for each asset class, and
rebalancing to target every January (decided on the December close) is a **house rule**: 60/40 has no single
source and Swensen asks only for regular rebalancing. Both allocations were chosen retrospectively, in 2026. That
they predate the window rules out tuning to it; it does not rule out choosing them knowing how they went.
- **B2a, 60/40:** 60% SPY, 40% AGG.
- **B2b, Swensen's *Unconventional Success* (2005):** 30% domestic equity (VTI), 15% foreign developed (EFA),
  5% emerging (EEM), 20% real estate (VNQ), 15% Treasuries (IEF), 15% TIPS (TIP).
- **Coverage is required, not assumed.** Before any statistic is printed, the report prints a symbol-by-month
  matrix of source and verdict for 2010-01..E. A fund missing any month in that span refuses the run; within
  that span nothing is truncated, filled or substituted. A fund returning price return only also refuses.

**B3 — random-basket control.** It is a **seasoned, complete-history subset** of the survivorship-free archive.
- **Formation population** (each December month-end from 2009-12 to 2025-12) is every name in the
  `survivorship_free` selection that meets three conditions:
  - it has a `total_return_reader` row for the formation month and each of the 11 months before it. December
    2009 needs Intrader bars back to 2008-12;
  - the formation month's `end_bar` is within 7 calendar days of the month-end. This is a by-construction
    staleness rule, so a name with no December close is not "bought" at a stale price;
  - **only rows up to the formation month are read.** No future coverage, splice verdict or survival enters
    eligibility.
- **Exclusion census.** Each formation prints the population, the count failing each rule, and the
  formation-month return distribution of the excluded against the included.
- **Identity contract.** Membership and identity are exactly `universe_selection`'s admission (vendor archive
  series, linked to an eToro instrument where a link exists). This is not a point-in-time security-type
  classification. Non-common securities that the archive carries and the selection admits are in the population,
  and the report prints the share of formation names with no instrument link. That is a disclosed reliance, not
  something certified here.
- **Variants:**
  - **B3-all:** the population above.
  - **B3-5:** the subset whose **raw** formation close is at least $5, the `market-segments.md` low-price flag.
    Raw close is Intrader `close`, which is unadjusted. Intrader ends 2024-09, so **B3-5 forms through 2023-12
    only**, and its path is reported through 2024-08. B3-all forms through 2025-12; its formations from 2024-12
    on are survivor-only, and their names are charged as having no raw price (see Costs).
- **Draws:** 1,000 complete paths per variant. Each path draws, at each formation, 50 names uniformly without
  replacement.
  - The population is sorted by `name_key`, and the sampler is `random.Random(f"3609-step0:{variant}:{draw}:{year}")`
    (Python's version-2 string seeding: deterministic across runs and platforms).
  - Each draw-year's holdings are persisted, and the same holdings serve both termination arms.
  - A population below 50 at any formation refuses the run.
- **Holding:** equal weight on post-cost NAV at formation. Weights drift until the next formation. Names drawn in
  consecutive years trade only their weight difference.
- **Endings inside a holding year.** There are three kinds, and they are kept apart:
  1. **Economic termination:** `AdmittedSeries.termination` is set and the name's rows stop. The class is
     `series_termination.classify_termination(admitted.termination)`, on the evidence `universe_selection`
     already built; nothing is re-parsed here.
     - The month after the last row, the position is revalued to `terminal_value_fraction(class, arm)` × its
       last value and moves to cash at 0% until the next formation.
     - This is an **imputed** realisation: no sale is placed, and it is labelled as such.
     - It is applied only after the last observed row, so no advance knowledge of the endpoint is used.
  2. **Coverage exit:** a non-terminating name whose rows stop at 2024-08 (refused or unspliced extension), or
     an interior gap. The position moves to cash at its last value with no haircut and no cost, and is counted
     separately.
  3. **End-of-study censoring** at the path end: liquidated as above.
- **The two arms** (`best_case`, `worst_case`) are the house's documented termination sensitivities. They are
  not bounds on economic outcome: an acquisition premium above the last print, or a recovery after an OTC
  collapse, lies outside them.
- **Reported:** for each statistic, the 5th, 50th and 95th percentile across draws (nearest-rank on the sorted
  1,000), each statistic separately. These describe **basket-selection dispersion conditional on this one
  history**. They are not confidence intervals, not 1,000 independent histories, and the medians of different
  statistics need not come from one portfolio.

## Monthly-return screen (the consumer screen #3619 slice 3 requires)

The total-return reader does not screen; this consumer does, before results and over the full **formation
population**, not only drawn names.
- **Flag:** every population (name, month) row with a monthly return above +300% or below −90%. These thresholds
  are a by-construction screen for adjustment-boundary errors of the BURU class (×39.9) and for wipeouts; legitimate
  moves of that size exist, so a flag deletes nothing.
- **Arbitration.** A flagged PWB-sourced row up to 2024-08 is compared with Intrader's `adj_close` return for the
  same name and month. A disagreement of more than 50 percentage points marks it **contradicted**; otherwise it is
  **corroborated** or **unarbitrated**. The slice 3 limit applies from 2022-01, not only after 2024-08.
- **Primary result:** as-is.
- **Sensitivity arm:** contradicted rows replaced by Intrader's return.
- **Also reported:** counts by class and the fraction of draws that held a flagged or contradicted row.

## Costs

The basis is the `cost_model.py` bands, half the round trip per side, on traded notional. Each cost scenario
prints `COST_MODEL_ID`, the band values and each band's `sample_size`, plus the calibration's limits: nine summer
dates, no stressed regime, and extrapolation to 2010–2026 and to every stock price level.

- **Band per position.** A position's band is fixed by its entry raw price (the `half_spread_for` rule: one band
  per position, used for entry and exit, never re-keyed). Adds and trims to a held position use that band.
- **Raw price sources.**
  - Stocks: Intrader `close` at the trade.
  - ETFs: Intrader `close` up to 2024-08, then eToro `price_daily` close on the trade date (the execution venue's
    price; the report prints each fund's band per trade).
  - A stock with no raw price (formations from 2024-12) gets `UNKNOWN_NOMINAL_PRICE_BAND`, through
    `cost_band_for(..., price_basis="split_adjusted")`.
  - A missing, non-positive or non-finite price refuses the run. A missing price is not a reason to charge the
    widest band.
- **ETFs are a stated exception to `research-process.md` §Costs**, which wants a separate ETF calibration; none
  exists. B1 and B2 are charged the stock band of the fund's raw price as a provisional assumption. They are not
  calibrated eToro ETF costs.
- **Stress scenario:** every band doubled, for all baselines. It is a sensitivity, not a bound: real costs can
  exceed it.
- **Charged as zero, stated as limits:**
  - slippage beyond the quoted spread, gaps, and fixed fees or commissions (not verified for the account);
  - carry and FX, which are zero only under the real-settlement assumption (`cost_model` closures);
  - the $10 minimum ticket, assumed non-binding (the account is assumed large enough).
- **Self-financing.** Costs are paid from NAV at the rebalance. Target weights apply to post-cost NAV, solved in
  one pass: cost = Σ|Δnotional| × half-spread at the pre-cost NAV, then targets are applied to NAV − cost. There is
  no leverage and no negative cash; fractional holdings are allowed.
- **Gross comparator:** the same decision path with zero cost. Cost drag = annualised gross minus annualised net.
- **Turnover:** one-way traded notional, (buys + sells) / 2, over the pre-trade NAV, summed per calendar year. The
  initial purchase, final liquidation and imputed terminal realisations are excluded from turnover but charged
  (imputations excepted). Reported as the window total divided by window years.
- **Distributions:** a total-return index approximation. Distributions are reinvested at the ex-date close at no
  cost, the payment lag is ignored, and no withholding is charged. **Every figure is pre-withholding**; no drag
  estimate is printed.

## Outputs (`scripts/report_3609_baselines.py`)

**Per baseline, B3 per variant and arm, for each of W1, W2 and W3, plus the supplementary full window:**
- **Annualised net return:** geometric, (Π(1+r))^(12/n) − 1.
- **Annualised volatility:** monthly sample standard deviation (n−1) × √12.
- **Maximum drawdown from monthly observations,** labelled as such.
- **Against B1**, with monthly active return a = r − r_B1:
  - active return = 12·mean(a);
  - tracking error = √12·sd(a);
  - IR = active / TE, printed "n/a" when TE = 0, as for B1 against itself;
  - beta = OLS slope of r on r_B1;
  - maximum relative drawdown of Π(1+r)/Π(1+r_B1).
- Turnover, and gross, net and cost drag. CAGR is this "annualised net return"; Sharpe is not printed.
- **FF5 + momentum regression**, only on windows of at least 60 months (W1 and the full window):
  - data: `french_five_factor_monthly` and `french_momentum_monthly` from `reference_data_observations`, the
    latest accepted snapshot. The values are stored as `decimal_return` (the parser converts the published
    percent), so they are used as stored; the script asserts that unit and refuses any other;
  - excess return = r − RF; the join is on calendar month;
  - the regression runs on the window's months that the path and both factor series cover (Amendment 1), and the
    span, the observation count and both snapshot ids are printed. Any interior missing month refuses;
  - Newey–West HAC with lag ⌊4(T/100)^(2/9)⌋ (the Newey & West 1994 rule);
  - labelled **exploratory**. Nothing is selected and no efficacy is claimed from these t-statistics.
- **Disclosures:**
  - the B2 coverage matrix and the B3 exclusion census;
  - termination counts and held exposure by `TerminationClass`, with the `(a)(4)` equivalence and the Q-suffix
    class labelled "asserted, unverified per name" as the source module states;
  - the coverage-exit count;
  - the weighted exposure share of `dividend_capture_degraded` rows;
  - the screen census.
- **Run manifest:** `var/research/3609_step0/<UTC timestamp>/manifest.json`. It holds the git SHA, this spec's
  path, and these rule versions: `TOTAL_RETURN_SPLICE_VERSION`, `ETF_TOTAL_RETURN_VERSION`,
  `UNIVERSE_SELECTION_RULE_VERSION`, `TERMINATION_RULE_VERSION`, `COST_MODEL_ID` and the quarantine rule set. It
  also holds the PWB and Intrader capture dates, the N-PORT snapshot (count, max filing date), the factor
  snapshot ids, E and its limiting funds, the sha256 of the persisted holdings, paths and ETF input rows, and the
  admitted `name_key` list hash.
  - The panels' own warning stands: prices, links and verdicts can move beneath those versions, so a re-run is
    compared on the manifest, not assumed identical.

**Segmentation:** an explicit baseline exception to sweep §2. The baselines carry no signal to segment.
Cell-matched controls (same universe, N and rebalance as the strategy, per segment) belong to each strategy's
declaration. This report's B3 is the unconditioned reference.

## Registration and the hold-out

These are not studies under `research-process.md` §"Every study is registered": there is no hypothesis, nothing
is selected, and every definition is fixed here before the first run. They therefore add **zero** to the
programme's trial count and carry no `trial_register` declaration. The script is registered in
`tests/test_sealed_outcome_scripts_are_gated.py::_NON_TRIAL_RESEARCH_READERS` with that reason, on the precedent
of #3471's planning table.

**Rebutted from checkpoint 1 (findings 45, 46):** the hold-out access log records a strategy's outcomes on
withheld months, so that a later declaration can be refused if its outcome was seen. A benchmark's
post-2021 returns are not any strategy's outcome, and SPY's are public. Logging them would attribute an access to
no trial. The months are labelled "reused validation" (W2) in every output.

## Amendment 1 (before any result): the common end month E

Checkpoint 1 ran on this amendment too (17 findings, all applied).

**Why.** The spec as first checkpointed ended every path at 2026-08, the last complete month of the PWB capture.
No B1/B2 fund reaches it, so the coverage rule refused every run. Measured on N-PORT snapshot
`rows=2535909;latest_filing=2026-06-30`, each fund's last total-return month is 2026-02 (AGG, EEM, IEF), 2026-03
(SPY via IVV, VTI) or 2026-04 (EFA, VNQ, TIP). That is consistent with SEC publishing N-PORT data sets
quarterly, but the endpoints are the measurement, not the explanation. Reproduce with
`load_etf_total_return_panel(conn, ["SPY", "AGG", "VTI", "EFA", "EEM", "VNQ", "IEF", "TIP"])`: the report's coverage
matrix prints each fund's verdict, identity check, first and last month, and missing months.

**The rule.** E is the common B1/B2 return endpoint, capped by PWB:
1. Each fund must carry its accepted extension verdict: `nport_proxy` for SPY, `nport` for the other seven. Any
   other verdict (an Intrader-only fallback) refuses the run; it never shortens E.
2. Each fund's candidate endpoint is its **latest** returned month. Coverage means the reader's accepted rows, by
   the reader's own source rules; no calendar or filing-date heuristic is added here.
3. E = the minimum of `LAST_PWB_MONTH` and the candidates. Then every fund must have every month from 2010-01 to E;
   an interior gap refuses the run and never moves E earlier.
4. E must be at least 2026-01, the first holding month of B3-all's last formation (2025-12), so that every
   scheduled formation is held and W3 is non-empty. An earlier E refuses the run.
5. E, the limiting fund(s), each fund's endpoint and the months excluded after E are printed and recorded in the
   manifest. On the snapshot above, E is 2026-02, limited by AGG, EEM and IEF.

**What E governs.** B1, B2a, B2b and B3-all end at E. The other inputs keep their own rules: stock rows, execution
prices and factor coverage are checked as specified above, not by E.

**B3-5 has its own endpoint, 2024-08.** It has no W3; its full window is 2010-01..2024-08; and its liquidation
cost is charged in 2024-08, inside W2. B3-5's W2 is therefore the one W2 figure that carries a liquidation, and
it is labelled so.

**Saved paths and later comparisons.** Each saved path holds, per month: the **continuing** return (a decision
month's includes its rebalance cost; no month includes a liquidation), the rebalance cost as a fraction of the
pre-trade NAV, and the cost of liquidating that month-end's pre-trade book as a fraction of its NAV. The printed
statistics charge the liquidation in the path's last month. A later study slices the continuing returns to its own
declared months, which must lie inside the saved artifact's coverage. For its last month it removes that month's
rebalance cost and charges that month's liquidation, so the slice ends exactly as a path that stopped there would.
The file states the formula. Comparing months beyond E needs a new run with its own manifest, not an
extrapolation.

**Reruns.** The declared run (1,000 draws) refuses a dirty checkout, so the manifest's git SHA identifies the
code, and every query reads one repeatable-read snapshot. A later N-PORT data set may advance E, up to the PWB cap; it may also leave E unchanged or move it
back. The report prints each fund's endpoint so that a rerun shows which happened. The ETF input rows behind E
(symbol, month, return, source, source key, filing accession), the verdicts and identity checks are written to the
run directory and hashed in the manifest.

**What E does not fix.**
- **Coverage-selected censoring.** E is chosen from observed availability, so one fund's reporting lag removes
  the tail for every baseline. The excluded months are disclosed, and a later comparison freezes its own
  endpoint and inputs before reading outcomes.
- **Survival conditioning.** B3 still reads the 2026-09-09 PWB capture. A name that stopped trading between E and
  that capture can be missing from the archive, so shortening the report does not restore point-in-time coverage.

**No per-fund truncation.** Within 2010-01..E nothing is truncated, filled or substituted for any fund. E itself
drops the months after it, for every baseline alike.

**Factor data.** The French values are stored as `decimal_return` (`reference_data_observations.unit`); the
original text called them percent, to be divided by 100, which would shrink every factor a hundredfold. They are
used as stored and any other unit refuses. Each dataset reads its latest accepted snapshot under the current
parser version, by fetch time; both snapshot ids are printed and recorded, and a reproduction can pin them. A
regression runs on the declared window intersected with the path's months and both factor series' months, needs at
least 60 months after that intersection, and prints its actual span.

## Amendment 2 (before any result): formation names with no raw close

**Why.** The first smoke run refused at the 2022-12 formation: 20 of its 6,883 population names have a total-return
row but no Intrader close that month (for example `name_key` 2451, BATRK, whose Intrader series starts 2023-07-20).
The splice takes every month from 2022-01 from PWB for a spliced name, so its history can predate its Intrader
series. Every other formation through 2023-12 has a raw close for every population name. Reproduce: for each
formation, the population names with no Intrader December month-end in `load_month_ends`.

**The rule.** Such a name is treated exactly as the spec already treats a formation from 2024-12, which has no raw
price either:
- **B3-all** keeps it and charges `UNKNOWN_NOMINAL_PRICE_BAND`, through
  `cost_band_for(<its PWB December close>, price_basis="split_adjusted")`;
- **B3-5** excludes it, because its $5 test needs a raw close, and counts it in a separate "no raw close" column
  of the exclusion census.

A raw close that exists but is non-positive or non-finite still refuses the run, and so does a name with no price
from either archive.

## Amendment 3 (after the declared run): W1 is survivor-only for B3 before 2015

**Why.** The declared run (`var/research/3609_step0/20261004T205019Z`) labelled W1 survivorship-free. That label is
wrong for B3 before 2015.
- The Intrader archive holds one series that terminates before 2014-09.
- Terminations start in 2014-09 and then run between 45 and 83 a month.
- Evidence and query: `docs/research/2026-10-04-3609-step1-factor-panel.md` premise 1, and the #3609 comment of
  2026-10-04.
- So the formations from 2009-12 to 2013-12 draw only from names that survived to about 2014-09. Their holding years
  2010–2014 are survivor-conditioned. 2014-12 is the first December formation made after terminations begin to be
  recorded.
- Completeness after that is verified only from 2019, against Form 25 delistings carrying a symbol (step-1 spec,
  premise 1).

**The rule.**
- W1 is reported as two sub-windows on the same continuous paths:
  - **W1a = 2010-01 .. 2014-12**, labelled **survivor-only for B3**;
  - **W1b = 2015-01 .. 2021-05**, labelled "terminations recorded; completeness unverified before 2019".
- The W1 figure is kept as supplementary, under the same survivor-only label.
- B1 and B2 are ETFs with complete histories and are unaffected, but they are split identically so that every row
  compares like with like.
- The run manifest records the amendment.
- The figures already posted on #3609 are not changed. Their W1 label is corrected by the re-run's output.

## Known limits

- B3 before 2015 is survivor-only (Amendment 3).
- B1/B2 use ETF costs that are an assumption, and B1's history from 2022 is a synthetic IVV NAV series.
- All figures are pre-withholding.
- W3 is survivor-only and ends at E (2026-02 on the current N-PORT data); B3-5 does not reach it. B3 stays
  conditioned on the 2026-09-09 PWB capture whatever E is.
- Termination arms are sensitivities, not bounds; coverage exits are not delistings.
- The B3 population relies on the selection's admission, not on a point-in-time security classification.
